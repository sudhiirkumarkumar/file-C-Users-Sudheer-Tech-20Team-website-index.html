## E. 10 LinkedIn Promotional Posts

**1 — The problem**
Most small businesses don't struggle with filming. They struggle with the blank page.
What do I say in the first 3 seconds? What do I show? When does the product appear?
I've spent the last few weeks turning those answers into 50 second-by-second UGC ad templates. Launching this week.

**2 — The framework**
Every effective UGC-style ad I've studied follows roughly the same arc:
0–3s: a hook in the viewer's language
3–7s: the problem, shown not told
7–13s: the product as the turning point
13–17s: proof
17–20s: one clear CTA
Save this. It works for products, SaaS and services.

**3 — For agencies & freelancers**
If you produce social ads for clients, the slowest part isn't editing — it's concepting.
I built a kit with 50 UGC video templates, 24 photo layouts and a 100-hook swipe file so the concepting step takes minutes. It's licensed for client work (finished ads, not reselling templates). [LINK]

**4 — Lesson learned**
The biggest thing I learned writing 50 ad templates: specificity beats cleverness.
"My cheeks feel tight by lunch" is far more relatable than "hydration you'll love". Your customers' reviews are your best copywriter.
