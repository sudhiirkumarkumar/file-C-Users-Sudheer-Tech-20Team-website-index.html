# Product Thumbnails & Mockups — Creative Directions
## UGC Ad Creative Template Kit

---

## Brand Look for Promo Graphics

| Element | Direction |
|---|---|
| Palette | Warm off-white #F7F5F2 background · near-black #161616 text · accent coral #FF5A3C · soft accent peach #FFD9CC · optional mint #CFF3E3 for "✓" chips |
| Fonts | Headline: Poppins ExtraBold or Montserrat Black · Body: Inter / Montserrat Regular · Accent: Caveat (handwritten arrows/labels) |
| Motifs | Phone frames showing UGC scenes, comment bubbles, subtitle captions, timeline bars, sticky-note labels, ✓ chips |
| Avoid | Heavy gradients, fake luxury gold, stock-photo business people, 3D clip-art, cluttered collages |

Phone frames should be **generic** (rounded rectangle with a notch/punch-hole) — no specific brand's device or app interface.
