## Market Context (for reference, check current market before setting prices)

- Basic Canva template packs (layouts only) typically sell in the low-price range.
- Script/hook swipe files typically sell at a similar low-to-mid price.
- A single UGC video from a freelance creator commonly costs considerably more than a template kit, and that's for one video.
- This kit combines templates + scripts + process + bonuses, which positions it above a plain template pack but well below done-for-you services.

| **Regular price (full kit)** | **$47** | Everything in the full kit | Positioned as a premium template system: 50 full video templates + 24 photo + editable Canva + 6 bonuses. Sits above basic template packs and far below the cost of one hired UGC video |
| **Premium bundle** | **$79** | Full kit + agency extras: (1) 20 additional seasonal/holiday template variations, (2) client-ready Creator Brief & Ad Report templates for freelancers/agencies, (3) a Canva Pro-optimised Brand Kit starter, *or* bundled with your existing DM Script Pack | Serves agencies/freelancers and multi-product buyers; the higher anchor makes $47 feel reasonable |

**Only offer the premium bundle once the extra assets actually exist.** A simple, legitimate alternative: bundle this kit with the existing **Instagram & Facebook DM Script Pack** (ads bring people in, DMs convert them) at a genuine combined saving.

## Reasoning

1. **Value-to-price:** at $47, the buyer pays roughly $0.12 per asset. Even valuing only the 50 video templates, that's under $1 per fully scripted ad.
2. **Comparison anchor:** compare honestly to the cost of the alternative (hiring a creator or agency for each video) rather than a fake "was $199" price.
3. **Launch pricing:** a launch price is fair when it's a *real* introductory period with a *real* end date that you honour. Don't run a permanent "launch sale" and don't show a crossed-out price you never charged.
4. **Good–better–best:** the entry tier captures budget buyers, the full kit is the main offer, and the premium bundle captures agencies — without discounting the core product.
5. **Room to grow:** adding new template packs over time justifies the regular price and gives past buyers a reason to return.

## What Not to Do

- ❌ Fake "was $297, now $47" anchors
- ❌ Countdown timers that reset on refresh
- ❌ "Only 3 left" on a digital product
- ❌ Claims like "guaranteed viral ads" or "10x your sales"

## Optional Honest Promotions

- **Launch week price** with a published end date and time
- **Seasonal sale** (e.g., once a year) announced in advance
- **Bundle saving** with another product you sell
- **Existing-customer discount code** for your DM Script Pack buyers
