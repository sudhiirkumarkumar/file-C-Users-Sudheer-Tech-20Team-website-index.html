## 2. How to Access the Templates

1. Open the **Template Links** page at the end of this guide.
2. Click a link while logged in to Canva (a free account works; some features like Background Remover and Brand Kit need a paid plan).
3. Canva will ask **"Use template"** → click it. This creates **your own copy** in your Canva account. The original stays untouched, so you can always start fresh.
4. Rename your copy (e.g., "V01 – My Brand – Oct") so you can find it later.

**No Canva?** Every template in the PDFs includes full layout, copy and timing instructions, so you can rebuild them in CapCut, InShot, Adobe Express, or your phone's built-in editor.

---

## 3. How to Edit a Template (5-Minute Version)

1. **Choose a template** using the Quick Selector at the end of File 02.
2. **Fill in the script:** copy the voiceover column from the template's timeline into your notes app and replace every `[bracket]`.
3. **Film** the scenes listed in the timeline (use the Shot List, File 07, for framing).
4. **Open the Canva template** → replace the placeholder clips/photos → edit the text.
5. **Export** (see step 8) and post.

---

## 4. How to Replace Photos & Videos

1. In Canva, click **Uploads** (left panel) → **Upload files** → select your clips/photos. On mobile: tap **+** → **Uploads**.
2. **Drag** your file directly onto the grey placeholder frame — it snaps into place.
3. **Double-click** the media to reposition or crop inside the frame.
4. For video: select the clip → **scissors** icon or drag the clip's edges on the timeline to trim to the scene length in the template.
5. If a clip shows black bars: right-click → **Set image/video as background** or scale it up to fill.

**Filming tip:** shoot vertically (9:16), face a window, hold each shot 3–5 seconds longer than you need.

---

## 5. How to Customise Colours

1. Click any coloured element (text pill, sticker, card).
2. Click the **colour tile** in the top toolbar.
3. Enter your brand hex code (e.g., #FF6B4A).
4. Click **"Change all"** to swap that colour everywhere in the design at once.
5. Paid plan: add your colours to **Brand Kit** once and apply across every template in one click.

Use your brand colour as an **accent only** (CTA pills, highlights, ticks). Keep backgrounds neutral so the ads still look native.

---

## 6. How to Add Your Logo

1. Upload a **transparent PNG** logo (one white, one dark version).
2. Drag it onto the canvas.
3. **Photo ads:** bottom-right corner, about 100 px wide, ~80% transparency setting.
4. **Video ads:** only on the final end-card page, or leave it off — the product itself is your branding, and UGC ads generally feel more native without a big logo.

---

## 7. How to Customise the Scripts

Use this 4-step method on any template:

| Step | Do this | Example |
|---|---|---|
| 1. Replace the pain | Use a phrase your real customers use | "My cheeks feel tight by lunch" (from a review) |
| 2. Replace the product line | One sentence, plain words — what it does, not what it is | "One layer and I'm done" |
| 3. Replace the proof | Something specific and true | "Second tub", "14-day trial", "ships in 2 days" |
| 4. Replace the CTA | Match your goal (File 05) | "Try it free" vs "Shop now" |

**Read it out loud with a timer.** If you run over the suggested duration, cut adjectives first.

**Faster:** paste the template into Prompt 02 in File 09 with your Brand Brief and let an AI assistant draft it — then fact-check and adjust the wording to sound like you.

## 9. Using the Creative for Organic Content

- **Post natively:** upload the exported MP4 directly to each platform (don't cross-post with another platform's watermark).
- **Add platform-native touches:** trending or commercially licensed sounds, native text stickers, polls and question boxes on Stories.
- **Caption:** use the template's caption suggestion, first line = hook or CTA.
- **Reply to comments with video** (template V50) — it turns objections into new content.
- **Follow the 30-Day Calendar** (File 08) to post consistently without thinking about what's next.
- **Track** 3-second hold rate, average watch time, saves and shares — they tell you which hooks and templates your audience responds to.

---

## 10. Adapting the Creative for Paid Advertising

1. **Start with what worked organically.** A post with above-average watch time or saves is your first ad candidate.
2. **Make 3 hook variations** of the same ad (swap only the first 3 seconds using File 04). Test them against each other.
3. **Export the right sizes:** 9:16 for Reels/Stories/TikTok, 4:5 for Feed. Re-check safe zones — ad placements show extra buttons.
4. **Match the CTA** to the ad's button and campaign objective ("Shop Now" button → "Shop now" CTA).
5. **Write ad copy** with Prompt 15 (File 09): short primary text, a headline, one description.
6. **Stay compliant:** no guaranteed results, no fake reviews, no fake urgency. Use real testimonials only with permission. Disclose paid partnerships and affiliate links. Check each platform's current advertising policies — especially for health, beauty, finance and before/after content.
7. **Refresh creatives regularly:** audiences get used to the same ad. Rotating templates and hooks from this kit helps keep your ads feeling new.
8. **Judge by your business numbers** (cost per purchase/lead, return on ad spend), not just views.
