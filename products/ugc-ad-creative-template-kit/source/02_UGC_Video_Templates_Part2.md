### V25 · The 3-Step Show-Me

| | |
|---|---|
| **Objective** | Conversions — show how easy it is |
| **Platform** | TikTok, Instagram Reels, YouTube Shorts |
| **Format** | Hands-on tutorial |
| **Duration** | 20 seconds |
| **Aspect ratio** | 9:16 |
| **Hook** | "Here's how to [result] in three steps — no skill needed." |
| **CTA** | "Everything you need is linked." |

**Timeline**

| Time | Visual | On-screen text | Voiceover |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0–3s | Final result shown first | [Result] in 3 steps ✨ | "Here's how to [result] in three steps — no skill needed." |
| 3–8s | Step 1 hands close-up | Step 1: [action] | "Step one: [action]." |
| 8–13s | Step 2 | Step 2: [action] | "Step two: [action]." |
| 13–17s | Step 3 + result | Step 3: [action] ✅ | "Step three: [action] — done." |
| 17–20s | Product + result together | Linked 👇 | "Everything you need is linked." |

**Visual direction:** Overhead or over-the-shoulder shots. Clean surface, good light on hands.

**B-roll:** Each step from a second angle, final result macro.

**Product placement:** Product is used in at least 2 of the 3 steps; label visible in step 1.

**Caption:** "Saving you a YouTube rabbit hole 😅 3 steps, that's it."

**Editing:** Show the result first (reverse hook). Step numbers large, top third. Speed-ramp slow parts 2×.

**Customise:** If there are more than 3 steps, group them. Simplicity sells.

**Example — "Fernwood Cold Brew Concentrate":**
"Here's how to make a café iced latte in three steps — no skill needed. Step one: two tablespoons of Fernwood in a glass. Step two: fill with ice. Step three: top with your milk — done. Everything you need is linked."

| **Aspect ratio** | 9:16 |
| **Hook** | "Let me show you how I [task] in under [time]." |
| **CTA** | "Try it free — link's below." (only if a free plan/trial exists) |

**Timeline**

| Time | Visual | On-screen text | Voiceover |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0–3s | Creator face full screen, then shrinks to circle | [Task] in under [time] ⏱ | "Let me show you how I [task] in under [time]." |
| 3–10s | Screen recording: open tool | Step 1: [action] | "I open [Product], [action]." |
| 10–18s | Screen recording: key feature, cursor highlight | This part 🤯 | "And this is the part I love — [feature] does [job] for me." |
| 18–25s | Screen: final output | Done ✅ [time] | "That's it. [Output] in [time]." |
| 25–30s | Face full screen again | Try it free 👇 | "Try it free — link's below." |

**Visual direction:** Clean desktop/phone screen, notifications off, large zoom on the relevant area.

**B-roll:** Creator typing at desk, the finished output being used.

**Product placement:** The product IS the screen; logo visible in the UI.

**Caption:** "My [task] workflow went from [before time] to [after time]."

**Editing:** Zoom 150–200% on UI areas. Circle cursor highlight. Face bubble bottom-left (outside the caption zone).

**Customise:** Record at high resolution, crop to 9:16. Blur any personal/customer data.

**Example — "Quillnote AI Meeting Notes":**
"Let me show you how I get meeting notes in under a minute. I open Quillnote, and it's already joined my call. And this is the part I love — it pulls out action items and tags who owns each one. That's it — a clean summary in my inbox before I've closed the tab. Try it free — link's below."

| **Format** | Challenge / test demo |
| **Duration** | 20 seconds |

**Timeline**

| Time | Visual | On-screen text | Voiceover |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0–3s | Creator with product, sceptical | "[Claim]"? Let's test 🧪 | "They say it's [claim]. Let's test that." |

| 12–16s | Result reveal | Result: [outcome] | "And… [result]." |

"They say it keeps ice for 24 hours. Let's test that. I'm filling it with ice at 8am and leaving it in a hot car all day. And… 8am the next morning, still ice. Passed. Link's below."
