# 05 — 50 CTA Ideas (Bonus 2)
## Calls-to-Action for Every Industry, Funnel Stage and Platform

---

## How to Use This Library

- **Match the CTA to commitment level.** Cold audiences get low-commitment CTAs ("See how it works"). Warm audiences get direct CTAs ("Get yours today").
- **Say it AND show it.** Voiceover CTA + on-screen text + a gesture (pointing down/at the caption).
- **One CTA per ad.** Two CTAs split attention.
- **Match the platform button.** If the ad button says "Shop Now", the spoken CTA should lead to shopping, not "learn more".

**Legend:** 🟢 Low commitment (cold) · 🟡 Medium (consideration) · 🔴 Direct (warm / retargeting)

## CTA Placement Cheatsheet

| Placement | How |
|---|---|
| Spoken | Last 2–3 seconds, said calmly, not shouted |
| On-screen | Lower-middle third, above the platform's caption area |
| Gesture | Point down (toward caption) or to the side (toward button) |
| Caption | Repeat the CTA in the first line of the caption for paid ads |
| End frame | Product + CTA text held for at least 1.5 seconds |

## CTA Do / Don't

| Do | Don't |
|---|---|
| "Try it free for 14 days" (if true) | "Buy now before it's gone forever!!!" |
| "Offer ends Sunday 11:59pm" (real deadline) | "Only 2 left!" (unless your stock counter is real) |
| "See why people love it" | "Guaranteed results" |
| One clear action | "Like, follow, share, comment, shop, DM me" |
