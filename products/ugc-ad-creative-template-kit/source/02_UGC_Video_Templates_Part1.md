## How to Read a Template Card

Every template follows the same layout so you can scan, copy and shoot fast.

| Card element | What it tells you |
|---|---|
| **Snapshot table** | Objective, platform, format, duration, aspect ratio, hook, CTA |
| **Timeline** | Second-by-second scenes with the visual, the on-screen text and the voiceover line for each beat. Read the VO column top to bottom and you have the full copy-paste voiceover script. |
| **Visual direction** | The overall look and feel of the shoot |
| **B-roll** | Extra clips to film so you have cut-away options in the edit |
| **Product placement** | Where, when and how the product appears |
| **Caption** | A post caption to copy and personalise |
| **Editing** | Cuts, text timing, music and pacing notes |
| **Customise** | What to swap so the ad fits your brand |
| **Example** | A fully filled version for a fictional product |

**Placeholders:** anything in `[square brackets]` gets replaced with your details.
**Default spec:** 9:16 vertical, 1080 × 1920 px, 30 fps, captions on, text inside the safe zone (see File 10).

### V01 · The "Six Months Too Late" Ad

| | |
|---|---|
| **Objective** | Conversions — turn a relatable regret into a purchase |
| **Platform** | Instagram Reels, TikTok, Facebook Reels |
| **Format** | Talking head + product B-roll |
| **Duration** | 20 seconds |
| **Aspect ratio** | 9:16 |
| **Hook** | "I genuinely wish someone had shown me this six months ago." |
| **CTA** | "Link's right here if you want to skip the six months I wasted." |

**Timeline**

| Time | Visual | On-screen text | Voiceover |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0–3s | Creator close to camera, product held just below chin | I wish I found this 6 months ago… | "I genuinely wish someone had shown me this six months ago." |
| 3–7s | Quick cut to the old frustrating way (messy drawer, failed attempt, cluttered screen) | What I was doing before 🙃 | "Because I was doing [old method] every single day and it was [pain in their words]." |
| 7–13s | Creator unboxes or opens product, turns it to camera | Then I found [Product] | "Then I found [Product]. It [core function in one line]." |
| 13–17s | Product in use, 2 quick angles | [Key result in 4–6 words] | "Now [specific result] — and it takes me [time]." |
| 17–20s | Creator points to caption/link area, product in frame | Link below 👇 | "Link's right here if you want to skip the six months I wasted." |

**Visual direction:** Natural window light, creator at arm's length, casual clothes, real room behind (kitchen, desk, bathroom). No studio backdrop. Slight handheld movement is good.

**B-roll:** The "before" mess; hands opening the package; product close-up with label visible; product in use from above; a satisfied reaction (small smile, nod).

**Product placement:** Visible in the first frame (held low), hero moment at 7s, logo facing camera at least once for 1+ second, final frame includes product.

**Caption:** "Six months of [pain] and the fix was this simple 😩 If you're still doing [old method], this is your sign. [Product] → link in bio / tap Shop Now."

**Editing:** Cut every 2–3 seconds. Pop text on the exact word it matches. Lo-fi or trending low-volume track under VO (10–15% volume). Add a subtle zoom-in (105%) on the hook.

**Customise:** Swap "six months" for a timeframe that is honest for your customer. Replace the "before" clip with your audience's real pain. Keep the result specific (a number, a time, a feeling).

**Example — "Solenne Barrier Cream" (skincare):**
"I genuinely wish someone had shown me this six months ago. I was layering three different moisturisers every night and my cheeks were still tight and flaky by lunch. Then I found Solenne Barrier Cream — one layer, that's it. Now my skin feels calm all day and my routine takes two minutes. Link's right here if you want to skip the six months I wasted."

### V02 · The "Stop Doing This" Interrupt

| | |
|---|---|
| **Objective** | Traffic / conversions — challenge a common habit |
| **Platform** | TikTok, Instagram Reels, YouTube Shorts |
| **Format** | Talking head with hand-signal interrupt |
| **Duration** | 18 seconds |
| **Aspect ratio** | 9:16 |
| **Hook** | "If you're still [common habit], please stop — here's why." |
| **CTA** | "Try it the easy way — it's linked below." |

**Timeline**

| Time | Visual | On-screen text | Voiceover |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0–2s | Creator holds palm to camera "stop" gesture | STOP doing this ✋ | "If you're still [common habit], please stop." |
| 2–6s | Re-enact the habit, slightly exaggerated | This = [negative consequence] | "It's the reason you keep [negative consequence]." |
| 6–11s | Creator picks up product | Do this instead 👇 | "Do this instead. [Product] [how it solves it in one sentence]." |
| 11–15s | Demo shot, satisfying result | [Result] in [time] | "Same job, [benefit], zero [pain]." |
| 15–18s | Creator nods at camera, product beside face | Linked below | "Try it the easy way — it's linked below." |

**Visual direction:** Direct, confident energy. Creator centred, eye-level. Bright, clean room. Re-enactment can be slightly comedic.

**B-roll:** Wrong-way attempt, product macro, side-by-side result, hand gesture close-up.

**Product placement:** Enters at 6s as the "instead" answer; stays in frame to the end.

**Caption:** "Nobody warns you about this one 😬 Switched to [Product] and I'm not going back. Would you try it?"

**Editing:** Hard cut on "stop" with a quick record-scratch or pop SFX. Red ✕ sticker over the old habit, green ✓ sticker over the product.

**Customise:** Pick a habit your audience genuinely does. Keep the consequence honest and non-shaming — "It's not your fault, nobody tells you" softens it.

**Example — "Kettlewise Gooseneck Kettle" (home):**
"If you're still boiling water in a saucepan for your pour-over, please stop. It's why your coffee tastes bitter one day and weak the next. Do this instead — Kettlewise heats to the exact temperature you pick and holds it. Same coffee, way more consistent, zero guessing. Try it the easy way — it's linked below."

### V03 · The "Nobody Told Me" Confession

| | |
|---|---|
| **Objective** | Awareness + consideration |
| **Platform** | TikTok, Instagram Reels |
| **Format** | Talking head, sitting (car, couch, bed) |
| **Duration** | 25 seconds |
| **Aspect ratio** | 9:16 |
| **Hook** | "Nobody told me that [surprising truth] — so I'm telling you." |
| **CTA** | "Save this, and check out [Product] if you're dealing with the same thing." |

**Timeline**

| Time | Visual | On-screen text | Voiceover |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0–3s | Creator leaning in, candid framing | Nobody told me this 😤 | "Nobody told me that [surprising truth] — so I'm telling you." |
| 3–9s | Creator talking, cut to a relevant detail shot | I spent [time/money] on… | "I spent [time/money] trying [failed solutions] and nothing worked." |
| 9–15s | Product appears in hand | The actual problem was [root cause] | "Turns out the real problem was [root cause]. That's what [Product] fixes." |
| 15–21s | Using product, then reaction | [Before] → [After] | "After [timeframe], [honest result]." |
| 21–25s | Creator to camera | Save this 📌 | "Save this, and check out [Product] if you're dealing with the same thing." |

**Visual direction:** Feels like a friend venting. Seated, relaxed, low-key. Car selfie framing works very well.

**B-roll:** Pile of failed products/tabs/receipts, product close-up, process shot, calm result shot.

**Product placement:** Revealed at the "root cause" moment so the product reads as the insight, not an advert.

**Caption:** "Wish this was common knowledge. What's something nobody told you about [topic]? 👇"

**Editing:** Keep it slightly longer shots (3–4s) — story pace. Burn-in captions word-by-word. Question in caption to invite comments.

**Customise:** The "surprising truth" must be true and specific to your niche. Pull it from customer reviews or support questions.

**Example — "Stridewell Arch Insoles" (e-commerce/health):**
"Nobody told me that my knee pain after runs could start at my feet — so I'm telling you. I spent months buying new trainers and foam rolling every night. Turns out my arches were collapsing, and that's what Stridewell insoles support. After three weeks my runs feel noticeably easier. Save this, and check out Stridewell if you're dealing with the same thing."

### V04 · The "Before You Buy Another One" Warning

| | |
|---|---|
| **Objective** | Conversions — intercept buyers comparing options |
| **Platform** | Facebook Ads, Instagram Reels |
| **Format** | Creator standing over a pile of alternatives |
| **Duration** | 22 seconds |
| **Aspect ratio** | 9:16 (4:5 cut for Feed) |
| **Hook** | "Don't buy another [category] until you've seen this." |
| **CTA** | "Check it before you buy anything else — link below." |

**Timeline**

| Time | Visual | On-screen text | Voiceover |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0–3s | Creator gestures at a pile of old products | Don't buy another [category] yet ⚠️ | "Don't buy another [category] until you've seen this." |
| 3–8s | Pick up old items one by one, toss aside | Tried: ❌ ❌ ❌ | "I've tried [option A], [option B], and the one everyone recommends." |
| 8–14s | Product revealed from behind back | This is the one that stayed | "This is the only one I actually kept using. Here's why." |
| 14–19s | 2 features shown quickly | ✓ [Feature 1] ✓ [Feature 2] | "[Feature 1], and [Feature 2] — which none of the others did." |
| 19–22s | Product held to camera | Link below 👇 | "Check it before you buy anything else — link below." |

**Visual direction:** Tabletop or bed with products laid out. Top-down establishing shot, then creator framing.

**B-roll:** The pile top-down, each rejected item's flaw, product feature macros.

**Product placement:** Reveal from behind back at 8s for a "hero moment." Make sure it is visually distinct from the pile.

**Caption:** "Saving you the money I wasted 💸 [Product] is the only one that made the cut."

**Editing:** Quick 1-second cuts on the rejected items with a "whoosh" SFX. Checkmarks animate in one at a time.

**Customise:** Don't name or show competitor brands — use generic/unbranded items or blur labels. Focus on your differentiators.

**Example — "Nimbra Insulated Bottle":**
"Don't buy another water bottle until you've seen this. I've tried the cheap one, the glass one, and the one everyone recommends. This is the only one I actually kept using. Here's why — it fits in my car cup holder, and the lid doesn't leak in my bag, which none of the others did. Check it before you buy anything else — link below."

| | |
|---|---|
| **Objective** | Emotional connection → consideration |
| **Platform** | Instagram Reels, TikTok, Facebook |
| **Format** | Storytime talking head with re-enactment clips |
| **Duration** | 30 seconds |
| **Aspect ratio** | 9:16 |
| **Hook** | "The day I finally snapped and fixed this for good…" |
| **CTA** | "If that was you this week, here's what I use." |

**Timeline**

| Time | Visual | On-screen text | Voiceover |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0–3s | Creator, exasperated expression | The day I finally snapped 😮‍💨 | "The day I finally snapped and fixed this for good…" |
| 3–10s | Re-enactment of the breaking-point moment | [Specific moment] | "I was [specific moment], and [thing went wrong] — again." |
| 10–16s | Creator scrolling phone / researching | So I went looking… | "So I went looking for something that actually worked and found [Product]." |

| 24–30s | Creator to camera, product in hand | If this was you this week 👇 | "If that was you this week, here's what I use." |

**Visual direction:** Two contrasting moods: the stressful moment (tighter framing, faster cuts) vs the calm moment (wider, slower, warmer light).

**Editing:** Colour-grade the stressful section slightly cooler, the resolved section warmer. Music shift at the turning point.

**Customise:** Use a real, specific moment your customers describe (reviews are gold here).

If that was you this week, here's what I use."

|---|---|

**Timeline**

### V07 · The "Split-Screen Struggle" Ad

| | |
|---|---|
| **Objective** | Conversions — visual before/after contrast |
| **Platform** | Instagram Reels, TikTok, Facebook Ads |
| **Format** | Split screen (top/bottom or left/right) |
| **Duration** | 15 seconds |
| **Aspect ratio** | 9:16 |
| **Hook** | "Me doing [task] without [Product] vs with it." |
| **CTA** | "Which side are you on? Get yours below." |

**Timeline**

| Time | Visual | On-screen text | Voiceover |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0–2s | Split screen: left = struggle, right = product | Without 😩 / With 😌 | "Me doing [task] without [Product]… versus with it." |
| 2–8s | Both sides play simultaneously | ⏱ [long time] vs ⏱ [short time] | "Left side: [pain detail]. Right side: [easy detail]." |
| 8–12s | Right side expands to full screen | [Main benefit] | "It's [benefit] — that's the whole review." |
| 12–15s | Product close-up, end card | Get yours 👇 | "Which side are you on? Get yours below." |

**Visual direction:** Same person, same outfit, same location on both sides so the only difference is the product.

**B-roll:** Film the full task twice — once each way — from the same tripod position.

**Product placement:** Only on the "with" side; final full-screen frame is product.

**Caption:** "The difference is honestly embarrassing 😅"

**Editing:** Use Canva/CapCut split layout. Add small timer stickers counting on both sides. Optional light comedic music.

**Customise:** The task must be something visual (cleaning, styling, setting up, cooking, editing).

**Example — "Pawpebble De-shedding Brush" (pets):**
"Me brushing my husky without Pawpebble… versus with it. Left side: fur everywhere and a very annoyed dog. Right side: one tidy clump and a dog who's actually enjoying it. It's faster and he doesn't run away — that's the whole review. Which side are you on? Get yours below."
