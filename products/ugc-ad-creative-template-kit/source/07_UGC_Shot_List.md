# 07 — UGC Shot List (Bonus 4)
## 60 Shots to Film So You Never Run Out of Footage

---

## How to Use the Shot List

- **Batch-film.** Pick 15–20 shots, film them in one session, and you'll have B-roll for 5–10 ads.
- **Film each shot 3 ways:** close, medium, wide — or 3 angles. Editors (including future you) need options.
- **Hold each shot 3–5 seconds**, even if you'll only use 1 second.
- **Vertical first.** Film 9:16 at 1080p or 4K, 30fps. Use 60fps for anything you might slow down.
- **Tick the box** when captured and note the file name.

**Gear you actually need:** phone, window light, a cheap tripod or phone clamp, a clean surface. Optional: clip-on mic, white card as a light bounce.

## Lighting & Framing Quick Rules

1. **Face the window.** Soft daylight on the face beats any ring light for authenticity.
2. **Eyes on the top third line** of the frame.
3. **Clean lens** — wipe it before every session.
4. **Lock exposure** (tap and hold on most phones) so brightness doesn't pulse.
5. **Keep the background real but tidy** — remove clutter that competes with the product.
6. **Leave headroom and bottom space** for text and platform UI (see safe zones in File 10).
7. **Record room tone** (10 seconds of silence) for smoother audio edits.
