# 10 — Canva Editing Guide
## The Design System Behind Every Template — and How to Recreate or Edit It

---

## 1. Design Principles

**AUTHENTIC + NATIVE + MODERN + RELATABLE + CONVERSION-FOCUSED**

| Do | Avoid |
|---|---|
| Real phone photos and footage | Stock photos of models in offices |
| One clear message per frame | Paragraphs of text |
| Clean, rounded text boxes | Heavy gradients, bevels, drop shadows |
| Casual sans-serif fonts | Script fonts that are hard to read |
| Native-looking bubbles, comments, stickers | Copies of specific app interfaces or logos |
| Brand colour used as an accent | Brand colour flooding every surface |
| Space around the product | Busy collages |

If a viewer would think "that's an ad" before they think "that's interesting", simplify.

## 3. Safe Zones (9:16)

Platform buttons, captions and usernames cover parts of the screen. Keep key text and the product inside the safe zone.

```
┌──────────────── 1080 ────────────────┐
│  TOP ~250 px: profile/status bar     │  ← no text
│──────────────────────────────────────│
│                                      │
│   SAFE ZONE                          │
│   ~60 px side margins                │
│   Hooks: top third of safe zone      │
│   Product: centre                    │
│   CTA text: lower-middle             │
│                               ~150px │ ← right side: like/comment/share icons
│──────────────────────────────────────│
│  BOTTOM ~400 px: caption, username,  │  ← no key text
│  button                              │
└──────────────────────────────────────┘ 1920
```

**In Canva:** create a transparent rectangle guide at those margins on a hidden layer, or use **File → Settings → Show rulers and guides** and drag guides to 250 px (top), 1520 px (bottom), 60 px (left) and 900 px (right, allowing for the icon column).

For 4:5 feed: keep a 60 px margin on all sides; avoid the bottom 120 px if you'll show a "Shop Now" overlay.

## 4. Typography Hierarchy

| Level | Use | Size (on 1080 wide) | Weight | Max words |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **H1 — Hook** | First-frame hook text | 64–80 px | Bold / Extra Bold | 8 |
| **H2 — Section** | Step labels, "Before / After", list numbers | 48–56 px | Bold | 5 |
| **Body** | Supporting text on photos | 34–40 px | Medium / Regular | 20 |
| **Subtitles** | Burned-in captions | 44–52 px | Bold | 3–6 per line |
| **Micro** | Names, dates, terms, "no filter" | 24–28 px | Regular | 10 |
| **CTA** | Button/pill text | 36–44 px | Bold | 3 |

**Line spacing:** 1.1–1.2 for headlines, 1.3–1.4 for body. **Letter spacing:** 0 (don't stretch).

---

## 5. Font Pairing Recommendations

All available in Canva's free library at the time of writing (check your plan):

| Style | Headline | Body | Best for |
|---|---|---|---|
| Clean creator | Montserrat Bold | Montserrat Regular | Any — safest choice |
| Friendly modern | Poppins SemiBold | Inter Regular | SaaS, digital, coaching |
| Soft beauty | DM Serif Display | DM Sans | Beauty, skincare, wellness (serif headline only) |
| Bold & punchy | Anton / League Spartan | Open Sans | Fitness, offers, food |
| Casual handwritten accent | Caveat or Nanum Pen (accents only) | Poppins | Labels, arrows, "notes" |

**Rule:** maximum 2 fonts per design, plus one handwritten accent if needed.

---

## 6. Text Placement

- **Hooks:** top third of the safe zone, centred or left-aligned.
- **Labels (Step 1, Day 3):** top-left corner inside safe zone, same position every time.
- **Supporting text on photos:** bottom-left over a subtle darkening (black, 0–40% opacity), never over faces.
- **Never** cover the product or the creator's mouth.
- **One text block per frame** in videos; change text with each scene.

---

## 7. Caption & Subtitle Styling

Many people scroll with the sound off — always add subtitles.

**Standard UGC subtitle style**
- Font: Montserrat or Poppins Bold, 44–52 px
- Colour: white text with black outline (2–4 px) **or** white text on a black rounded box at 60–80% opacity
- Position: middle to lower-middle, above the bottom safe zone
- 3–6 words per line, maximum 2 lines
- Highlight 1 keyword per line in your brand accent colour (optional)

**How to add in Canva:** Canva's auto-caption feature (where available) → edit text → apply style. Or add text boxes timed to each clip on the timeline. CapCut's auto-captions can also be exported and brought into Canva as a finished video.

---

## 8. Sticker Usage

| Sticker type | Use for | Limit |
|---|---|---|
| Arrows / hand-drawn circles | Point to product features | 1–2 per frame |
| ✓ / ✕ marks | Comparisons, do/don't | Pairs only |
| Rating stars | Real reviews only | 1 row |
| "No filter" / "Real result" chip | Before/after | 1 |
| Timer / clock | Speed demos | 1 |
| Offer badge | Genuine promotions | 1 |

Style: flat, simple, one or two colours. Avoid 3D, glitter or cartoonish clip-art that looks dated.

---

## 9. Emoji Usage

- **1–2 emoji per text block**, at the end of a line.
- Use emoji to add *tone*, not to replace words: 😩 frustration, 👀 curiosity, ✨ result, 👇 CTA, 🤍 warmth, 📌 save.
- Avoid emoji in the middle of headlines and in serious categories (finance, health) keep them minimal.

---

## 10. Screenshot Placement

- Place screenshots (reviews, messages, app screens) on a **rounded card** with a soft shadow (Canva: Elements → rounded square frame, shadow effect 10–20%).
- Scale to **70–85% of the canvas width** so text stays readable.
- **Blur** surnames, profile photos, emails and order numbers (Canva: Edit photo → Blur brush, or cover with a solid shape).
- Use **generic designs**: plain white or light-grey bubbles, simple sans-serif — don't replicate a specific app's logos, icons or exact interface.

---

## 11. Product Image Placement

- Product should occupy **30–50% of frame** in product shots.
- Place on the **centre or right third**; text on the left.
- Remove backgrounds only for end cards — UGC body shots should keep the real environment.
- Keep the **label facing camera** at least once.
- Canva: use Background Remover (paid) for end cards; add a soft shadow under the cut-out so it doesn't float.

---

## 12. Video Clip Placement (Canva Video Editor)

1. Create a 1080 × 1920 design.
2. Upload clips → drag to the timeline; each clip = one scene/page.
3. Right-click → **Fill background** so the clip fills the frame without black bars.
4. **Trim** each clip to 1.5–4 seconds (scissors icon / drag edges).
5. Add text per scene; set text animation to *Fade* or *Pop* (keep it subtle).
6. Transitions: use *None* (hard cut) by default — hard cuts feel native. Use *Match & move* or a simple dissolve only for before/after reveals.
7. Add audio: voiceover track + music at 10–20% volume.
8. For picture-in-picture (face bubble on screen recording): place the face clip on top, crop to a circle frame, position bottom-left above caption zone.

---

## 13. CTA Placement

- **Video:** final 2–3 seconds, lower-middle, as a rounded pill: brand colour background, white bold text, 36–44 px.
- **Photo:** bottom-left or bottom-centre pill, 60 px from edges.
- **Carousel:** CTA only on the last slide + a subtle "swipe →" on slide 1.
- Keep CTA text to **2–4 words** ("Shop now", "Try it free", "See how it works").

---

## 14. Brand Colour Customisation

Build a mini palette of 4 colours:

| Role | Use | Example |
|---|---|---|
| Neutral light | Backgrounds, cards | #F7F5F2 |
| Neutral dark | Text | #1A1A1A |
| Brand accent | CTA pills, keyword highlights, ticks | Your main brand colour |
| Soft accent | Stickers, secondary chips | A lighter tint of your brand colour |

**In Canva:** Brand Kit (paid) → add colours → apply with one click. Free plan: save hex codes in a text note and use the colour picker → "+" to add them to Document colours.
**Global swap:** click any coloured element → colour picker → "Change all" to replace a colour across the whole design.
**Accessibility:** make sure text has strong contrast against its background (dark text on light, or white text on a dark/saturated colour).

---

## 15. Logo Placement

- UGC ads should feel creator-made: **keep logos small and quiet.**
- Photo ads: bottom-right corner, 80–120 px wide, 60 px margin, 70–90% opacity.
- Video ads: **only on the end card**, or as a small watermark in the top-left inside the safe zone. Many UGC ads work without a logo at all because the product itself is the branding.
- Use a transparent PNG version (white and dark versions for different backgrounds).

## 17. Pre-Export Quality Checklist

- [ ] Hook visible and readable in the first second
- [ ] All text inside safe zones
- [ ] Subtitles on and synced
- [ ] Product visible within the first 3–5 seconds
- [ ] One CTA, clearly stated and shown
- [ ] No spelling errors (read it backwards once)
- [ ] Personal data blurred on screenshots
- [ ] Music licensed for commercial use (use the platform's commercial library or royalty-free tracks)
- [ ] Claims are true and supportable
