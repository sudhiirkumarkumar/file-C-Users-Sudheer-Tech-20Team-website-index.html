# 04 — 100 UGC Hooks (Bonus 1: Hook Swipe File)
## Natural, Say-It-Out-Loud Opening Lines Organised by Angle

---

## How to Use This Swipe File

1. **Pick the angle** that matches your ad's objective (use the table below).
2. **Fill the brackets** with your customer's words — pull phrases from reviews, DMs and comments.
3. **Say it out loud.** If it sounds like an ad, simplify it until it sounds like a text to a friend.
4. **Pair it with a visual hook** — the first frame should show something (the product, the problem, a reaction) that matches the words.
5. **Test 3 hooks per ad.** Keep the body of the video the same, swap only the first 3 seconds, and compare hold rate (3-second views ÷ impressions).

| Objective | Best hook angles |
|---|---|
| Cold audience awareness | Curiosity, POV, Product Discovery, Educational |
| Problem-aware audience | Problem, Story, Confession, Before/After |
| Retargeting / warm | Review, Testimonial, Social Proof, Comparison |
| Launches & promos | Offer, Founder |

## Hook Upgrade Checklist

- [ ] Could a real person say this without sounding scripted?
- [ ] Does it name a specific moment, object or feeling?
- [ ] Is the first frame visually doing something (not a logo, not a blank wall)?
- [ ] Does the on-screen text match or add to what's being said?
- [ ] Is every claim in the hook true for your product?
