# 11 — Industry Adaptation Playbook
## How to Adapt the Templates for 15 Industries (with Ready Examples)

---

## How to Adapt Any Template in 4 Steps

1. **Swap the problem** — use the exact pain your customer describes (check reviews, DMs, forum threads).
2. **Swap the setting** — film where your customer lives the problem (bathroom, gym, desk, kitchen, car, shop floor).
3. **Swap the proof** — use the proof type your industry trusts (visual result, screen recording, customer clip, credentials, taste reaction).
4. **Swap the CTA** — match your buying process (shop, trial, book, visit, DM).

Each industry below includes **3 hooks, 1 short UGC script, 1 photo ad, 1 video ad and 1 CTA.** All brands are fictional.

**Compliance reminder:** beauty, skincare, fitness, food/supplements, finance and education/coaching are regulated or policy-sensitive on ad platforms. Avoid medical claims, income claims and unrealistic before/afters. Check current platform policies before running paid ads.
