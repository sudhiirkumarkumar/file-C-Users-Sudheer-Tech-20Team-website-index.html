# COMMUNITY MONETIZATION PLANNER

A beginner-friendly overview of common ways communities generate revenue, plus a worksheet to help you choose an approach that fits your situation. This is educational information, not a guarantee — outcomes depend on your niche, effort, offer quality, and audience.

---

## Common Community Monetization Models

| Model | How It Works | Good Fit For |
|---|---|---|
| Free Community | No charge; monetized indirectly (leads to paid offers elsewhere) | Building an audience before you have a paid offer |
| Paid Community | Flat recurring fee for membership/access | Communities with clear, ongoing value (support, resources, accountability) |
| Course + Community | A paid course with a community included for support/accountability | Educators and coaches teaching a specific skill or process |
| Membership | Tiered access with ongoing content/benefits | Creators who can consistently produce new value over time |
| Coaching + Community | 1:1 or group coaching with community as a supporting layer | Coaches/consultants wanting deeper client relationships |
| Digital Products + Community | Community supports/promotes your templates, guides, or tools | Digital product sellers building a customer community |
| Affiliate Opportunities | Community members access curated affiliate recommendations | Creators already active in affiliate marketing (see disclosure practices) |
| Premium Workshops | Paid live sessions/events offered to community members | Communities with an engaged base wanting deeper, timed learning |

*None of these models guarantee income. Each requires real effort, a genuine offer, and an audience that wants it.*

## Guidance for Introducing Monetization

- **Earn trust before you monetize.** A community that feels valuable for free earns the right to introduce a paid layer later — doing it too early can feel like a bait-and-switch.
- **Keep free value genuinely useful**, even if you introduce paid options. A community that becomes entirely a sales funnel tends to lose engagement.
- **Use the Promotional Posts category sparingly** (see Community Post Swipe File, Section 10) — a handful of well-placed posts outperform frequent pitching.
- **Test small before committing.** A single workshop or a small paid tier tells you more about demand than a fully built-out membership system.

**Note:** This planner does not promise or imply any specific income, sales, or growth outcome. Any figures you use in your own offer or marketing should reflect your own real, verifiable results — never estimates or guesses presented as facts.
