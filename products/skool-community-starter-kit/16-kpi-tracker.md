# COMMUNITY KPI TRACKER
### [TEMPLATE]

A simple metrics tracker to fill in manually each month. Pull numbers from your community platform's own member/analytics view.

---

## Metric Definitions

| Metric | What It Means | Why It Matters |
|---|---|---|
| Total Members | Everyone who has ever joined | Overall community size |
| New Members | Members who joined this period | Growth rate |
| Active Members | Members who posted, commented, or engaged this period | True engagement, not just headcount |
| Posts | Total posts made by anyone (you + members) | Content volume |
| Comments | Total comments across all posts | Depth of engagement |
| Engagement Rate | Active Members ÷ Total Members | What % of your community is actually participating |
| Challenge Participation | Members who joined/completed a challenge | Interest in structured activities |
| Member Retention | Members still active after 30/60/90 days | Whether people stick around |
| Conversion Rate | Members who took a desired action (e.g., upgraded to paid) ÷ Total Members | Monetization health, if applicable |
| Revenue | Total revenue generated through the community, if applicable | Only relevant for paid/monetized communities |

**Formula:**
```
Engagement Rate = Active Members ÷ Total Members
Member Retention (30-day) = Members still active at day 30 ÷ Members who joined 30 days ago
Conversion Rate = Converted Members ÷ Total Members
```

## How to Use This Tracker

1. **Fill it in monthly**, on the same date each time, right after your Community Audit (17).
2. **Focus on Engagement Rate over Total Members.** A smaller, highly engaged community is generally healthier than a large, quiet one.
3. **Watch trends, not single months.** One quiet month isn't a problem — a consistent downward trend across 3 months is worth investigating.
4. **Only track Revenue/Conversion Rate if monetization applies to you.** Leave blank otherwise — don't force metrics that don't fit your current model.
