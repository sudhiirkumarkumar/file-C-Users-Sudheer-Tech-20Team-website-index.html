# COMMUNITY STRUCTURE BLUEPRINT

A practical example of how a beginner can structure their community's category feed. Adapt the categories, emojis, and order to fit your niche.

---

## [EXAMPLE] Suggested Categories

| # | Category | What to Post Here |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | 👋 Start Here | Pinned welcome post, community guide, FAQ, "read this first" — the first thing any new member should see |
| 2 | 📢 Announcements | Updates from you: new content, schedule changes, community news. Members typically can't post here. |
| 3 | 💬 General Discussion | Open conversation — anything on-topic that doesn't fit another category |
| 4 | 🎯 Goals & Introductions | New member intros, personal goals, progress check-ins |
| 5 | 📚 Resources | Guides, templates, links, tools — anything reusable and reference-worthy |
| 6 | ❓ Questions & Support | Members ask for help; you and other members answer |
| 7 | 🏆 Wins & Results | Members share progress, milestones, and results — your best source of social proof |
| 8 | 🔥 Challenges | Active challenge threads (see 11-Challenge-Kit) |
| 9 | 🤝 Networking | Members connect with each other directly — collabs, intros, partnerships |
| 10 | 💡 Ideas & Feedback | Suggestions for the community, feature requests, feedback on your content |

---

## How to Decide What Goes Where

- **If it's from you and needs no reply** → Announcements
- **If it's a new member's first post** → Goals & Introductions
- **If someone needs help** → Questions & Support
- **If someone succeeded** → Wins & Results
- **If it's reusable later** → Resources
- **If you're not sure** → General Discussion (it's your catch-all)

Keeping this decision simple prevents both you and your members from over-thinking where to post — friction here is a common reason posting activity stays low.

## Setup Tips

- **Order categories by member journey**, not alphabetically — "Start Here" first, "Announcements" near the top, discussion-heavy categories in the middle.
- **Pin your most important post** in "Start Here" (your welcome/orientation post from 07-Onboarding-Swipe-File).
- **Review category usage monthly** (see 17-Community-Audit-Checklist) — merge or remove categories that stay empty after 60 days.
- **Fewer, well-used categories beat many empty ones.** Start smaller than you think you need; add categories as real demand shows up.
