# 14-DAY COMMUNITY ENGAGEMENT PLAN
## [TEMPLATE]

A complete day-by-day plan for your community's first two weeks — the window where engagement habits get set. Each day includes ready-to-use post copy. Replace brackets with your details.

---

### DAY 1 — INTRODUCTIONS
**Objective:** Get members posting immediately
**Post Idea:** Introduction prompt
**Post Copy:** "Welcome to [COMMUNITY NAME]! Let's kick things off — introduce yourself below: your name, where you're from, and what brought you here."
**Engagement Question:** "What's one thing you're hoping to get out of this community?"
**CTA:** "Reply below to say hi 👋"

### DAY 2 — COMMUNITY GOALS
**Objective:** Get members thinking about outcomes
**Post Idea:** Goal-setting prompt
**Post Copy:** "Day 2! Let's talk goals. What's one thing you want to accomplish in the next 30 days related to [TOPIC]?"
**Engagement Question:** "What's stood in your way of this goal so far?"
**CTA:** "Drop your goal in the comments"

### DAY 3 — QUICK WIN
**Objective:** Deliver immediate value
**Post Idea:** Share one fast, actionable tip
**Post Copy:** "Quick win for your [TOPIC] today: [ONE SPECIFIC, ACTIONABLE TIP]. Try it and report back!"
**Engagement Question:** "Did you already know this, or is it new to you?"
**CTA:** "Try it today and comment how it went"

### DAY 4 — POLL
**Objective:** Low-effort engagement, learn about your members
**Post Idea:** Simple poll
**Post Copy:** "Quick poll: when it comes to [TOPIC], what's your biggest challenge right now?"
**Engagement Question:** Poll options — "A) [Option] B) [Option] C) [Option] D) Something else (comment below)"
**CTA:** "Vote above 👆"

### DAY 5 — RESOURCE DROP
**Objective:** Build trust through value
**Post Idea:** Share a useful resource
**Post Copy:** "Sharing a resource that's helped me with [TOPIC]: [RESOURCE/LINK/TEMPLATE]. Take a look and let me know your thoughts."
**Engagement Question:** "What's a resource that's helped you with this?"
**CTA:** "Comment your favorite resource for [TOPIC]"

### DAY 6 — MEMBER QUESTION
**Objective:** Turn the spotlight on members
**Post Idea:** Ask the community a genuine question
**Post Copy:** "Curious to hear from you all: what's the #1 thing you'd want help with related to [TOPIC] this month?"
**Engagement Question:** (This IS the engagement question)
**CTA:** "Reply below — I read every answer"

### DAY 7 — WEEKLY CHECK-IN
**Objective:** Create a rhythm/ritual
**Post Idea:** End-of-week reflection prompt
**Post Copy:** "First week down! How's it going so far — any wins, any struggles? Let's celebrate and troubleshoot together."
**Engagement Question:** "What's one thing that went well this week?"
**CTA:** "Share your week in the comments"

### DAY 8 — BEHIND THE SCENES
**Objective:** Build personal connection
**Post Idea:** Share something personal/authentic about your process
**Post Copy:** "Behind the scenes: here's what my [TOPIC-related routine/workflow] actually looks like right now — [SHORT DETAIL]."
**Engagement Question:** "What does yours look like?"
**CTA:** "Share your version below"

### DAY 9 — MYTH VS. TRUTH
**Objective:** Educational engagement
**Post Idea:** Bust a common misconception
**Post Copy:** "Common myth about [TOPIC]: [MYTH]. Here's what's actually true: [TRUTH]."
**Engagement Question:** "Had you heard this myth before?"
**CTA:** "Comment yes/no + your experience"

### DAY 10 — MEMBER SPOTLIGHT
**Objective:** Recognition, social proof
**Post Idea:** Highlight a member's win or contribution
**Post Copy:** "Shoutout to [MEMBER NAME] for [SPECIFIC WIN/CONTRIBUTION] — love seeing this kind of progress!"
**Engagement Question:** "Anyone else made progress this week?"
**CTA:** "Drop your win below — big or small"

### DAY 11 — QUICK QUESTION
**Objective:** Easy, low-barrier engagement
**Post Idea:** A yes/no or either/or question
**Post Copy:** "Quick one: [OPTION A] or [OPTION B] when it comes to [TOPIC]?"
**Engagement Question:** (This IS the engagement question)
**CTA:** "Comment your pick"

### DAY 12 — HELPFUL FRAMEWORK
**Objective:** Deliver deeper value
**Post Idea:** Share a simple framework or process
**Post Copy:** "Here's a simple framework I use for [TOPIC]: [STEP 1] → [STEP 2] → [STEP 3]. Try applying it this week."
**Engagement Question:** "Where would you get stuck applying this?"
**CTA:** "Ask questions below if anything's unclear"

### DAY 13 — OPEN DISCUSSION
**Objective:** Let the community lead
**Post Idea:** Open-ended discussion starter
**Post Copy:** "Open floor: what's on your mind related to [TOPIC] this week? Ask, share, vent — this one's for you."
**Engagement Question:** (This IS the engagement question)
**CTA:** "Post your thoughts below"

### DAY 14 — TWO-WEEK CELEBRATION
**Objective:** Close the loop, set up ongoing rhythm
**Post Idea:** Recap + look ahead
**Post Copy:** "Two weeks in! Thank you for being part of the early days of [COMMUNITY NAME]. Here's what's coming next: [PREVIEW OF UPCOMING CONTENT/CHALLENGE]."
**Engagement Question:** "What's been your favorite part of the community so far?"
**CTA:** "Comment below — and invite a friend who'd benefit from joining"

---

## Usage Notes

- **Post at a consistent time each day** during this window — consistency matters more than the exact hour.
- **Reply to every comment** for these first 14 days, even a short one. This sets the norm that posting here gets a response.
- **After Day 14**, move into the 30-Day Content Calendar (09) and Community Post Swipe File (10) to keep the rhythm going.
