# COMMUNITY LAUNCH PLAN
## 7-Day Version
### [TEMPLATE]

A more spread-out alternative to the 48-Hour Quick Start (01-quick-start-guide.md) — one focused theme per day. Use this version if you'd rather build steadily instead of in two intense days.

---

### DAY 1 — POSITIONING
```
[ ] Complete the Community Idea Worksheet (03)
[ ] Write your Positioning Statement (choose from the 10 formulas)
[ ] Write your Community Description and short bio
[ ] Draft 2-3 landing page headline options
```

### DAY 2 — SETUP
```
[ ] Choose your community name and finalize branding (banner, logo, colors)
[ ] Set up your category structure (04-Community-Structure-Blueprint)
[ ] Set up your classroom's first 3 modules (05-Classroom-Planner)
[ ] Publish your Community Rules (06)
```

### DAY 3 — CONTENT PREPARATION
```
[ ] Draft your Welcome Post and "Start Here" post (07-Onboarding-Swipe-File)
[ ] Draft your first 7-10 posts using the Post Swipe File (10)
[ ] Choose and prepare your first challenge (11-Challenge-Kit)
[ ] Build out Days 1-7 of your engagement plan (08)
```

### DAY 4 — ONBOARDING
```
[ ] Finalize your onboarding sequence (07) — welcome message, first-day message, first-week check-in
[ ] Decide how you'll personally welcome each new member
[ ] Test your onboarding flow yourself, as if you were a new member
```

### DAY 5 — PRE-LAUNCH
```
[ ] Build your invitation list (personal network, email list, social followers)
[ ] Draft your launch announcement for email/social
[ ] Do a final walkthrough of the entire community as a new member would see it
[ ] Fix any gaps, confusing wording, or missing pieces
```

### DAY 6 — LAUNCH
```
[ ] Publish your Welcome Post and pin it
[ ] Send personal invitations — not just a mass announcement
[ ] Post your launch announcement on social/email
[ ] Personally welcome every member who joins today
```

### DAY 7 — ENGAGEMENT
```
[ ] Post Day 1 of your 14-Day Engagement Plan (08)
[ ] Reply to every comment and introduction
[ ] Announce your first challenge
[ ] Set a recurring calendar reminder for your monthly Community Audit (17)
```

---

## Choosing Between the 48-Hour and 7-Day Plans

| If you... | Use |
|---|---|
| Can block out two focused days | 48-Hour Quick Start (01) |
| Prefer to spread the work out and build steadily | 7-Day Launch Plan (this file) |
| Are launching alongside a full-time job or other commitments | 7-Day Launch Plan, extended over 1-2 weeks as needed |

Either path uses the exact same underlying templates — the only difference is pacing.
