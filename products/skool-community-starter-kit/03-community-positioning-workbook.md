# COMMUNITY POSITIONING WORKBOOK

Two parts: the **Community Idea Worksheet** (Section 1) defines what your community is. The **Positioning Kit** (Section 2) turns that definition into copy you can actually publish.

---

## PART 1: COMMUNITY IDEA WORKSHEET

Fill in each field in your own words. Don't overthink your first pass — you can refine this after your first 30 days of real member feedback.

## PART 2: COMMUNITY POSITIONING KIT

### Community Positioning Statement — 10 Formulas
### [TEMPLATE]

Fill in the brackets with your answers from Part 1.

1. "Join [COMMUNITY NAME] to help [TARGET AUDIENCE] achieve [DESIRED RESULT] without [MAJOR PAIN POINT]."
2. "[COMMUNITY NAME] is where [TARGET AUDIENCE] go to [DESIRED OUTCOME] — together, not alone."
3. "A community for [TARGET AUDIENCE] who want to [DESIRED RESULT], without [COMMON OBSTACLE]."
4. "[COMMUNITY NAME] helps [TARGET AUDIENCE] go from [CURRENT STATE] to [DESIRED STATE] in [TIMEFRAME/APPROACH]."
5. "For [TARGET AUDIENCE] who are tired of [PAIN POINT] — [COMMUNITY NAME] gives you [CORE OUTCOME]."
6. "[COMMUNITY NAME]: the place for [TARGET AUDIENCE] to [DESIRED ACTION] and [DESIRED RESULT]."
7. "We help [TARGET AUDIENCE] [DESIRED OUTCOME] through [METHOD — e.g., accountability, templates, coaching]."
8. "[COMMUNITY NAME] is a [FREE/PAID] community built for [TARGET AUDIENCE] who want [CORE OUTCOME]."
9. "Stop [PAIN POINT] alone. [COMMUNITY NAME] connects [TARGET AUDIENCE] working toward [DESIRED RESULT]."
10. "[COMMUNITY NAME] gives [TARGET AUDIENCE] the [RESOURCES/SUPPORT TYPE] to finally [DESIRED OUTCOME]."
