## CHALLENGE 1 — 5-Day Action Challenge

| Field | Details |
|---|---|
| Objective | Get members to take fast, visible action on one specific task related to [TOPIC] |
| Duration | 5 days |
| Rules | One action per day, posted in the 🔥 Challenges category. No action is too small to count. |
| Completion Criteria | Post on at least 4 of the 5 days |
| Recognition | Shoutout post naming everyone who completed 4+ days |

**Daily Themes & Post Templates:**
- **Day 1 — Commit:** "Post your goal for this challenge and why it matters to you."
- **Day 2 — First Step:** "What's the one action you took today toward [TOPIC]? Post it below."
- **Day 3 — Obstacle:** "What got in your way today, and how did you push through (or not)?"
- **Day 4 — Momentum:** "What's clicking for you so far in this challenge?"
- **Day 5 — Wrap-Up:** "Final day! Share your biggest takeaway from these 5 days."

**Member Instructions:** "Post your daily update in 🔥 Challenges using today's prompt. Miss a day? No problem — just pick back up the next day."

## CHALLENGE 2 — 7-Day Consistency Challenge

| Field | Details |
|---|---|
| Objective | Build a daily habit related to [TOPIC] |
| Duration | 7 days |
| Rules | Complete one small, repeatable action every day and check in |
| Completion Criteria | Check in on at least 5 of 7 days |
| Recognition | "Consistency Award" post for anyone who hit 6-7 days |

**Daily Activity:** Each day, members complete the same small repeatable action (e.g., "spend 15 minutes on [TASK]") and post a one-line check-in.

**Daily Post Template (reused each day):**
> "Day [X] check-in: Did [ACTION] today. [Optional: one sentence on how it went]."

**Member Instructions:** "Every day for 7 days, complete [SPECIFIC SMALL ACTION] and post a one-line check-in using the template above. This challenge is about consistency, not perfection."

## CHALLENGE 3 — 7-Day Skill Challenge

| Field | Details |
|---|---|
| Objective | Build one specific skill related to [TOPIC] through daily practice |
| Duration | 7 days |
| Rules | Each day focuses on a different component of the skill, building on the day before |
| Completion Criteria | Complete at least 5 of 7 daily exercises and post results |
| Recognition | Feature 2-3 standout submissions in a wrap-up post |

**Daily Themes:**
- **Day 1:** Learn the fundamentals — post what you learned
- **Day 2-3:** Practice the core technique — post your attempt
- **Day 4-5:** Apply it to a real example — post your result
- **Day 6:** Troubleshoot a common mistake — post what you'd fix
- **Day 7:** Show your final result — post a before/after or summary

**Daily Post Template:**
> "Day [X]: Here's what I worked on today — [SHORT DESCRIPTION]. Feedback welcome!"

**Member Instructions:** "Each day builds on the last, so try to keep up in order. Posting your work — even imperfect — is how you get useful feedback."

## CHALLENGE 4 — 14-Day Growth Challenge

| Field | Details |
|---|---|
| Objective | Make measurable progress on a specific goal related to [TOPIC] over two weeks |
| Duration | 14 days |
| Rules | Set a specific goal on Day 1, check in every 2-3 days, report results on Day 14 |
| Completion Criteria | Post the Day 1 goal, at least 3 check-ins, and the Day 14 results |
| Recognition | "Growth Story" spotlight post featuring top 2-3 results (with permission) |

**Structure:**
- **Day 1 — Set Your Goal:** Post a specific, measurable goal for the next 14 days
- **Days 3, 6, 9, 12 — Check-Ins:** Short progress update using the template below
- **Day 14 — Results:** Post what you achieved, what worked, and what you'd do differently

**Check-In Template:**
> "Day [X] check-in on my goal ([GOAL]): [Progress update in 1-2 sentences]."

**Member Instructions:** "Set one clear, specific goal on Day 1. Check in every few days — it doesn't need to be long, just honest. On Day 14, share your results, win or not."

## CHALLENGE 5 — 30-Day Accountability Challenge

| Field | Details |
|---|---|
| Objective | Sustained progress and accountability over a full month |
| Duration | 30 days |
| Rules | Members choose their own goal within [TOPIC], commit publicly on Day 1, and check in weekly |
| Completion Criteria | Weekly check-ins (at least 4) + a final Day 30 reflection post |
| Recognition | End-of-challenge "Accountability Champions" post + optional small prize/perk for top participants |

**Structure:**
- **Day 1 — Public Commitment:** Post your 30-day goal and why it matters
- **Weekly Check-Ins (Days 7, 14, 21, 28):** Progress update + one obstacle + one adjustment
- **Day 30 — Reflection:** What you achieved, what surprised you, what's next

**Weekly Check-In Template:**
> "Week [X] check-in on [GOAL]: Progress — [update]. Obstacle — [what got in the way]. Adjustment — [what I'm changing next week]."

**Member Instructions:** "This is a marathon, not a sprint. You don't need a perfect month — you need to show up, report honestly, and adjust as you go. Missing a week doesn't disqualify you; missing all of them does."

## Running Any Challenge — General Tips

- **Announce it at least 3-5 days before it starts** using the 30-Day Content Calendar or Post Swipe File.
- **Pin the challenge rules post** for the full duration.
- **Personally reply to check-ins**, especially in the first few days — participation drops fast if nobody responds.
- **Close every challenge with a recap post** naming participants and their outcomes — this is your best source of future testimonials and Wins & Results content (see Section 04 category structure).
