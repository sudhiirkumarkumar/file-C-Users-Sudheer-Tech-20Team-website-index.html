## What Is This?

This is a done-for-you community launch system — not a "how to use Skool" ebook. It contains the worksheets, checklists, templates, swipe files, and plans you need to go from "I want to start a community" to "my community is live, organized, and running."

Every template is designed to be copy-pasted, filled in, and used the same day you download it. This kit is platform-agnostic in its thinking (positioning, structure, content, engagement) while using Skool's common layout — categories, classroom, member feed — as the working example throughout.

---

## Who This Kit Is For

- Beginners launching their first online community
- Coaches, consultants, and course creators
- Digital product sellers and freelancers
- Educators, service providers, and small business owners
- Personal-brand creators who want a home base for their audience

No technical experience is assumed. If you can copy, paste, and fill in a blank, you can use every resource here.

## Recommended Implementation Order

1. **Start with 01-quick-start-guide.md** if you want to launch fast (48 hours), or **14-launch-plan.md** if you'd rather take a full 7 days.
2. **Do the Positioning Workbook (03) first**, even if you're eager to jump into setup — a clear niche and promise make every later template easier to fill in.
3. **Use the Setup Checklist (02) and Structure Blueprint (04)** together while building your actual Skool community.
4. **Set up your Classroom (05) and Rules (06)** before inviting anyone.
5. **Prepare your Onboarding (07) and first 14 days of content (08)** before you publicly launch.
6. **Layer in the 30-day calendar (09), Post Swipe File (10), and Challenge Kit (11)** as you move past week one.
7. **Revisit the Engagement Toolkit (12), Monetization Planner (13), Growth Planner (15), KPI Tracker (16), and Audit Checklist (17)** on an ongoing monthly rhythm.
8. **Keep 18-common-beginner-mistakes.md and 20-bonus-resources.md** as standing references.

## How to Customize the Templates

- Every template uses bracketed placeholders like `[Community Name]` or `[Topic]` — replace these with your real details.
- Nothing here is meant to be used word-for-word forever. Treat each template as a first draft you adjust to sound like you.
- The 30-day and 14-day calendars are examples of a healthy rhythm, not a rigid schedule — swap in your own topics as your community grows.

---

## Getting the Most Value From This Kit

- **Don't try to implement everything on day one.** Follow the recommended order above.
- **Revisit the worksheets as your community evolves** — your positioning and structure will sharpen after your first 30 days of real member feedback.
- **Treat the checklists as recurring, not one-time.** The Setup Checklist is used once; the Audit Checklist is used every month.
