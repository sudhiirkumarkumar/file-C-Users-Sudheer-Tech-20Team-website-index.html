# COMMUNITY RULES TEMPLATE
## [TEMPLATE]

A professional but friendly rules template, ready to copy into your "Start Here" post. Replace bracketed sections with your own details, and adjust the tone to match your community's personality.

---

> ## Welcome to [COMMUNITY NAME] — here's how we do things around here
>
> These rules exist to keep this a genuinely useful, respectful place for [TARGET AUDIENCE]. Please read before posting.
>
> **1. Respect**
> Treat every member the way you'd want to be treated. Disagreement is fine — disrespect, personal attacks, or harassment are not, and will result in removal.
>
> **2. No Spam**
> Don't post the same message repeatedly, drop unsolicited links, or flood the feed. One well-thought-out post beats five low-effort ones.
>
> **3. Self-Promotion**
> [Choose one and customize:]
> - "Self-promotion is welcome in the 🤝 Networking category only."
> - "Please ask before promoting your own offers — DM [HOST NAME] first."
> - "This community is a no-promotion zone — let's keep the focus on [TOPIC]."
>
> **4. Keep Content Relevant & Appropriate**
> Posts should relate to [COMMUNITY TOPIC]. Keep content appropriate for a professional, mixed audience — no offensive language, explicit content, or unrelated political/religious debate.
>
> **5. Ask Questions Freely**
> There's no such thing as a "dumb question" here. Use the ❓ Questions & Support category — you're expected to ask, and expected to help others when you can.
>
> **6. Give Constructive Feedback**
> When giving feedback on someone's work or idea, lead with what's working before what could improve. Be honest, not harsh.
>
> **7. Networking Is Encouraged — Respectfully**
> Connecting with other members is welcome. Don't cold-pitch people who haven't opted into a conversation.
>
> **8. Confidentiality**
> What's shared inside the community (personal situations, unfinished work, early ideas) stays inside the community unless the member says otherwise.
>
> **9. Moderation**
> [HOST NAME] and any listed moderators may remove posts/comments that break these rules, and may remove members after repeated or serious violations. Moderation decisions are final but can be discussed respectfully via DM.
>
> **10. Community Culture**
> Show up, contribute, and help build a place people are glad they joined. [OPTIONAL: add a specific cultural value — e.g., "We celebrate wins here, no matter how small."]
>
> Questions about these rules? Message [HOST NAME] directly.

---

## Customization Notes

- Keep the tone consistent with your positioning (from 03-Community-Positioning-Workbook) — a rules post that's stiffer or looser than the rest of your community feels off.
- Pin this post in your "👋 Start Here" category (see 04-Community-Structure-Blueprint).
- Reference these rules — don't re-litigate them — when moderating. "Per rule 3, let's keep self-promo in Networking" is faster and less personal than an open-ended correction.
- Revisit the rules after your first 60-90 days. Real community behavior will show you what's actually missing.
