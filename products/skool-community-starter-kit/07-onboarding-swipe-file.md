# WELCOME & ONBOARDING KIT
## [SWIPE FILE]

Ten ready-to-use templates covering a new member's first experience, from the moment they join through their first week. Fill in brackets and adjust tone to match your community.

---

### 1. Welcome Post (pinned in "Start Here")

> "Welcome to [COMMUNITY NAME]! 👋
>
> This community exists to help [TARGET AUDIENCE] [DESIRED OUTCOME].
>
> Here's how to get started:
> 1. Introduce yourself in 🎯 Goals & Introductions
> 2. Check out 📚 Resources for our core materials
> 3. Read our community guidelines below
> 4. Say hi in 💬 General Discussion — we're glad you're here!
>
> If you ever have a question, post in ❓ Questions & Support — someone (usually me!) will jump in fast."

---

### 2. New Member Introduction Post (prompt for members to answer)

> "New here? Introduce yourself! Tell us:
> 1. Your name and where you're from
> 2. What brought you to [COMMUNITY NAME]
> 3. One goal you're working toward right now
> 4. One fun fact about you
>
> Welcome — we're excited to have you!"

---

### 3. First-Day Message (DM to new members, if you send them personally)

> "Hey [Name], welcome to [COMMUNITY NAME]! Glad you're here. Quick tip to get the most out of it: start with the intro post in 🎯 Goals & Introductions — that's usually the fastest way to feel at home. Let me know if you have any questions!"

---

### 4. "Start Here" Post (pinned, orientation-focused)

> "New to [COMMUNITY NAME]? Start here.
>
> **What this community is:** [One-sentence description from your Positioning Workbook]
>
> **Where to go:**
> - 👋 Start Here — you're already here
> - 🎯 Goals & Introductions — introduce yourself
> - 📚 Resources — our core templates and guides
> - ❓ Questions & Support — ask anything
>
> **Your first action step:** [One specific, easy first action — e.g., 'Complete Module 1 in the classroom']"

---

### 5. Community Orientation Post

> "A quick tour of [COMMUNITY NAME] so you know where everything lives:
> - **Classroom:** [what's there]
> - **Community feed:** [what's there]
> - **Categories:** [1-2 line summary of your category structure]
>
> Bookmark this post — it's your map."

---

### 6. Member Goal Post (prompt)

> "Let's set some intentions. Reply below with:
> - One goal you want to achieve in the next 30 days
> - What's held you back from it so far
>
> We'll check back in on these in a few weeks!"

---

### 7. First Week Check-In (posted ~5-7 days after someone joins)

> "Hey [Name] — you joined about a week ago. How's it going so far? Anything you're stuck on, or anything I can point you toward?"

---

### 8. Personal Welcome Message (DM template)

> "Hi [Name], thanks for joining [COMMUNITY NAME]! I like to personally say hi to new members. What made you decide to join, and what are you hoping to get out of it? I'll point you toward the most relevant resources."

---

### 9. Onboarding Follow-Up (for members who haven't posted yet)

> "Hey [Name], noticed you haven't introduced yourself yet — no pressure, but the intro thread is a great way to meet people here. Would love to see you in there when you get a chance!"

---

### 10. Welcome-to-the-Classroom Post

> "If you haven't checked out the classroom yet, Module 1 ('Start Here') is the best place to begin — it takes about [X] minutes and sets you up for everything else."

---

## Onboarding Sequence Suggestion

| Timing | Action |
|---|---|
| Immediately on join | Welcome Post visible (pinned) + optional personal DM (#8) |
| Day 1 | First-Day Message (#3) if DMing personally |
| Day 1-2 | Member posts intro (#2 prompt) |
| Day 5-7 | First Week Check-In (#7) |
| Day 10-14 | Onboarding Follow-Up (#9) if they've gone quiet |
