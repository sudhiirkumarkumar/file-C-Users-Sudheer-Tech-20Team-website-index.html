## Growth Channels Overview

| Channel | What It Involves | Realistic For |
|---|---|---|
| Organic Promotion | Mentioning your community naturally in relevant places | Everyone |
| Social Media | Posting about your community and its value on your existing platforms | Anyone with an existing following, however small |
| Email | Inviting your existing email list/contacts | Anyone with an email list |
| Personal Network | Direct, personal invitations to people you know | Everyone — often the most effective early channel |
| Partnerships | Cross-promotion with complementary creators/communities | Once you have some initial traction to offer |
| Content Marketing | Creating content (posts, videos, articles) that leads back to your community | Creators already producing content |
| Lead Magnets | A free resource that requires joining the community to access | Communities with at least one polished resource ready |
| Referral Strategies | Encouraging current members to invite others | Once you have an engaged base worth referring people to |
| Member-Generated Content | Members sharing their own wins/experience publicly | Once you have real member results to showcase |

## 30-Day Growth Roadmap

### Week 1 — Foundation
```
[ ] Personally invite everyone in your existing network who fits your target audience
[ ] Announce the community across every social platform you're already active on
[ ] Email your existing list (if you have one) with a personal invitation
[ ] Update your bio/links across platforms to point to the community
```

### Week 2 — Content & Visibility
```
[ ] Create 2-3 pieces of content that reference your community (post, video, article)
[ ] Share 1-2 genuine wins/insights from inside the community publicly (with member permission)
[ ] Identify 3-5 potential partners or complementary creators for future cross-promotion
```

### Week 3 — Lead Magnet & Referrals
```
[ ] Create or polish one free resource that requires joining to access
[ ] Ask 3-5 of your most engaged members if they'd be willing to invite a friend/colleague
[ ] Reach out to 1-2 potential partners about cross-promotion
```

### Week 4 — Review & Double Down
```
[ ] Review your KPI Tracker (16) — which channel actually brought the most members?
[ ] Double down on whatever channel performed best
[ ] Publish a "member results" or "community highlights" post to use in future promotion
[ ] Plan your next 30 days based on what you learned
```

---

## Growth Principles for Beginners

- **Your personal network is usually your fastest first channel.** Don't skip direct, personal invitations in favor of only public posts.
- **One genuinely useful lead magnet outperforms scattered promotion.** Build one good free resource before spreading effort across many channels.
- **Referrals work best after you have real value to point to.** Wait until you have a few genuine member wins before leaning hard on referral asks.
- **Track where members actually come from** (ask on join, or note it yourself) so you know which channel to double down on.

**Note:** Growth results depend heavily on your existing audience, niche, content quality, and consistency. No specific member count or growth rate is promised or implied by this roadmap.
