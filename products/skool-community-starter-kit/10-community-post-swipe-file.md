# COMMUNITY POST SWIPE FILE
## [SWIPE FILE] — 50 Ready-to-Customize Templates

Fifty copy-paste post templates across five categories. Replace bracketed placeholders with your details. Use alongside the 30-Day Content Calendar (09) for variety.

---

## Engagement Posts (10)

1. "Quick check-in: how's your week going with [TOPIC]?"
2. "React with 🔥 if you're working on [GOAL] this week."
3. "Fill in the blank: 'The hardest part of [TOPIC] is ___.'"
4. "If you could master one skill related to [TOPIC] instantly, what would it be?"
5. "Tag someone in the comments who'd love this community."
6. "What's one small win you had today?"
7. "Rate your progress on [GOAL] this week, 1-10."
8. "What are you working on right now? Drop it below."
9. "Two truths and a lie about [TOPIC] — comment your guess."
10. "What's a tool or habit that's changed how you approach [TOPIC]?"

## Question Posts (10)

1. "What's the #1 question you have about [TOPIC] right now?"
2. "What's stopping you from [DESIRED ACTION]?"
3. "What would make this community more useful to you?"
4. "What's a mistake you made early on with [TOPIC] that taught you something?"
5. "What resource has helped you most with [TOPIC]?"
6. "If you're stuck right now, what specifically are you stuck on?"
7. "What's one thing you wish someone had told you when you started?"
8. "What's your current biggest priority related to [TOPIC]?"
9. "Have you tried [METHOD/APPROACH]? How did it go?"
10. "What topic should we cover next in the classroom?"

## Educational Posts (10)

1. "Here's a simple way to think about [CONCEPT]: [EXPLANATION]."
2. "The difference between [TERM A] and [TERM B], explained simply."
3. "3 things to know before you try [ACTION]."
4. "Here's the framework I use for [TOPIC]: [STEP 1] → [STEP 2] → [STEP 3]."
5. "A common misconception about [TOPIC] — and what's actually true."
6. "Here's what I'd do differently if I were starting [TOPIC] today."
7. "The one metric/signal that actually matters for [TOPIC]."
8. "A short breakdown of [CONCEPT] with a real example."
9. "Here's a checklist for [TASK] — save this for later."
10. "The most common question I get about [TOPIC], answered in full."

## Community-Building Posts (10)

1. "Shoutout to everyone who showed up and posted this week — this is what makes this community work."
2. "Member spotlight: [NAME] just [ACHIEVEMENT] — go show some love!"
3. "This community just hit [MILESTONE] — thank you for being part of it."
4. "What's something you're grateful for in your [TOPIC] journey right now?"
5. "Let's do a round of intros for anyone who joined this week — welcome!"
6. "What's a win, however small, that you haven't shared yet?"
7. "Tell us one thing about yourself that has nothing to do with [TOPIC]."
8. "Who's someone in this community that's helped you? Give them a shoutout."
9. "What brought you to [COMMUNITY NAME] originally — has it changed since?"
10. "Let's celebrate: drop one thing you're proud of from this month."

## Promotional / Conversion Posts (10)

*Use sparingly and transparently — these work best when they're a small fraction of your total posts.*

1. "Doors are open for [OFFER] — here's what's included: [DETAILS]. [LINK]"
2. "A few spots left for [COACHING/WORKSHOP] — comment 'interested' if you want details."
3. "Members have been asking about [OFFER] — here's the full breakdown: [DETAILS]."
4. "If [SPECIFIC PROBLEM] sounds familiar, [OFFER] might help. Here's how: [DETAILS]."
5. "New resource available: [RESOURCE NAME] — built based on what you've been asking for."
6. "Q&A time — drop your questions about [OFFER] below and I'll answer them here."
7. "Here's what's included if you upgrade to [PAID TIER]: [DETAILS]."
8. "Last call: [OFFER/DEADLINE] closes on [DATE]. Details here: [LINK]."
9. "A few members have had great results with [OFFER] — here's what changed for them: [BRIEF EXAMPLE]."
10. "Curious about [OFFER] but not sure if it's right for you? Comment below and I'll help you figure it out."

---

## Usage Guidance

- **Balance your categories.** A healthy feed leans on Engagement, Question, and Educational posts most heavily, with Community-Building woven throughout and Promotional posts used only occasionally.
- **Don't post promotional content back-to-back with other promotional content.** Space it out so the community doesn't start to feel like an ad feed.
- **Personalize every template before posting.** These are starting points, not finished copy — swap in your voice and real details.
