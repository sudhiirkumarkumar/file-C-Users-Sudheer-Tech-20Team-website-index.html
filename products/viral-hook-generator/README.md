# Viral Hook Generator
### Turn Any Topic Into Hundreds of Scroll-Stopping Hook Ideas

A premium, beginner-friendly spreadsheet system for Instagram, TikTok, and YouTube creators, coaches,
consultants, freelancers, social media managers, digital-product sellers, and small-business owners who
are tired of staring at a blank screen trying to write the perfect hook.

Works in both **Google Sheets** and **Microsoft Excel**.

---

## The Idea

Writing hooks from scratch feels hard because it's treated as a creative leap. This spreadsheet turns it
into a repeatable process instead:

**Choose a formula → Enter your topic → Add your angle → Generate variations → Test your favorites.**

It does not promise viral results, guaranteed views, or guaranteed followers — no tool can. What it
delivers is a fast, organized way to produce dozens of genuinely different hook options for any topic, so
there's always something worth testing.

---

## What's Inside (`Viral-Hook-Generator.xlsx`)

| # | Tab | What it does |
|---|-----|---------------|
| 1 | **START HERE** | Welcome page, 3-step quick start, workflow diagram, worked example, tips |
| 2 | **Hook Generator** | The main tool — fill in 10 yellow input cells, get up to 30 live hook variations |
| 3 | **Formula Library** | 120 hook formulas across 24 categories (Curiosity, Mistake, Contrarian, Story, etc.) |
| 4 | **Niche & Keyword Library** | 51 niches, each with example keywords — fully editable |
| 5 | **Pain Points** | 110 audience pain points, organized by category |
| 6 | **Desired Outcomes** | 110 desired outcomes, organized by category |
| 7 | **Content Angles** | 55 content angles with a one-line usage tip for each |
| 8 | **Tone Library** | 14 tones with a description and example phrase |
| 9 | **Platform Guide** | Hook style, length, and goal for 8 platforms |
| 10 | **Hook Examples** | 208 ready-to-use example hooks across 16 niches |
| 11 | **Testing Tracker** | Log real performance (views, likes, saves, etc.) — no invented "viral score" |
| 12 | **Power Words** | 100 hook words grouped into 8 categories (curiosity, urgency, emotion, etc.) |
| 13 | **CTA Library** | 50 natural calls-to-action grouped by goal |
| 14 | **30-Day Challenge** | A daily hook-writing and testing plan, one formula type per day |
| 15 | **Rewrite Tool** | Paste a weak hook, rewrite it 10 different ways using guided techniques |

The Hook Generator is formula-driven (`INDEX`/`MATCH`/`COUNTIF`/`SUBSTITUTE`/`IFERROR` — no volatile or
version-locked functions), so it recalculates live in both Excel and Google Sheets and stays fully
editable: customers can add their own niches, pain points, outcomes, angles, and formulas at any time.

## Product Positioning

**FROM:** "I don't know how to write a good hook."
**TO:** "I have dozens of hook ideas I can test."

No hook, formula, or spreadsheet can guarantee views, followers, watch time, or sales — this is stated
plainly on the START HERE tab and the Testing Tracker. The product is positioned as a speed and ideation
tool, not a performance guarantee.
