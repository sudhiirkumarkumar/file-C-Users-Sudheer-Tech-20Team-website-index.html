## 5. FAQ Section
*(add via an FAQ block, an accordion plugin, or plain text under the description)*

```
**Do I need any design or spreadsheet skills to use this?**
No. Every input is a simple dropdown or text box. If you can type into a spreadsheet cell,
you can use this.

**Will this work in Google Sheets, or only Excel?**
Both. The formulas used (INDEX, MATCH, COUNTIF, SUBSTITUTE, IFERROR) are supported natively
in Google Sheets and in Excel 2016 and newer.

**Does this guarantee my content will go viral?**
No — and we'd be lying if we said it did. No tool can guarantee views, followers, or sales.
This gives you a fast way to generate genuinely different hook ideas so you always have
something worth testing.

**Can I add my own niches, pain points, or formulas?**
Yes. Every library tab is fully editable. Add new rows any time and the generator will pick
them up automatically.

**How do I get the file after I buy?**
It's an instant digital download — you'll get a link right after checkout and in your
confirmation email. No physical product is shipped.

**What's the file format?**
A .xlsx spreadsheet, delivered inside a .zip along with a short getting-started guide.

**Is this a subscription?**
No. It's a one-time purchase — yours to keep and reuse forever.
