## PART 1: BEFORE YOU START (Setup)

### Step 1: Prepare Your Assets
You need these ready before opening Meta Ads Manager:

**Videos (upload to Meta):**
- Script A – "The Silent DM" (30s, vertical 9:16)
- Script B – "Before/After" (15s, vertical 9:16)
- Script C – "I timed myself" (20s, vertical 9:16)
- Script D – "Objection stack" (25s, text-only, vertical 9:16)

**Images (upload to Meta):**
- Hero listing image (`cover/product-hero-listing.jpg`)
- 3D cover image (`cover/product-cover-3d.jpg`)
- One 1:1 or 4:5 static image with price

**Copy:**
- Primary text (choose from 3 options in script kit)
- Headlines (5 options)
- Descriptions (3 options)
- URLs: your SuperProfile link + any UTM tags

**Important:** Make sure all videos are vertical (9:16), MP4 format, under 4GB each.

---

### Step 2: Get Your SuperProfile Link
1. Log into your SuperProfile dashboard
2. Go to your DM Script Pack product page
3. Copy the full URL: `https://superprofile.bio/[yourname]/product/[product-id]`
4. Test it on your phone to make sure it loads

**Optional: Add UTM tags (for tracking)**
Add this to your SuperProfile link:
```
?utm_source=meta&utm_campaign=dmpack&utm_medium=instagram
```

Full URL looks like:
```
https://superprofile.bio/[yourname]/product/[product-id]?utm_source=meta&utm_campaign=dmpack&utm_medium=instagram
```

---

### Step 3: Check Meta Pixel Setup
1. Go to **Meta Ads Manager** (ads.facebook.com)
2. Click **Settings** (bottom left) → **Business Settings**
3. Go to **Data Sources** → **Pixels**
4. If you have a pixel, note the Pixel ID
5. If you don't have one, create it (we'll use it for retargeting)

*Note: If SuperProfile doesn't allow pixel installation, just track by link clicks for now.*

## PART 2: CREATE YOUR FIRST CAMPAIGN (Cold Traffic)

### Step 4: Open Meta Ads Manager
1. Go to **ads.facebook.com**
2. Log in with your Facebook business account
3. Select your **Ad Account** (top left)
4. Click **Create** (top left button)

---

### Step 5: Create the Campaign
You're now in the Campaign Creation flow.

**Campaign setup:**

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| **Campaign Objective** | Conversions (or Traffic if pixel not installed) |
| **Campaign Name** | "DM Pack – Cold TOF" |
| **Campaign Budget** | ₹500–₹1,000 total (we'll spend ₹300–₹500/day) |
| **Conversion Event** | Purchase (if pixel installed) or Link Clicks |
| **Special Ad Categories** | None (unless you're selling in a restricted category) |

Click **Next** or **Continue**.

---

### Step 6: Set Up the Ad Set (Audience)
You're now in the "Ad Set" section.

**Basic Settings:**

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| **Ad Set Name** | "DM Pack – Cold – India 22-45" |
| **Campaign Budget or Daily Budget** | Choose "Daily Budget" → ₹300 per day |
| **Start Date** | Tomorrow |
| **End Date** | Leave blank (run until you pause it) |

**Audience (Targeting):**

1. Scroll to **Audience**
2. Click **Create New**
3. Fill in:

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| **Location** | India |

| **Languages** | Leave blank |

**Naming:** Call this "India 22-45 Small Biz" and save it.

4. Estimated Audience Size: should show **1M – 5M people**

## PART 4: ADD MORE ADS (To Scale Testing)

### Step 18: Duplicate This Ad Set
While your first ad is running, add 2 more video ads to the SAME ad set (to test which one wins).

1. Go back to **Ads Manager**
2. Click on your campaign
3. Click on your ad set
4. In the **Ads** tab, click **Create**
5. Choose **Existing Creative** (reuse a video from Meta library)
6. OR click **Upload Video** and add **Script B – Before/After**
7. Fill in headline, description, URL (same as before)
8. Change **Ad Name** to "DM Pack – Video B – Before After"
9. Click **Create Ad**
10. Repeat for **Script C** and **Script D**

Now you have 3–4 video ads testing in the same audience.

## PART 5: MONITOR & OPTIMIZE (Days 1-7)

### Step 19: Check Performance (Day 1-3)
1. Go to **Ads Manager** → Your Campaign
2. Look at the **Ad Set** tab
3. Check the metrics for each ad:

| Metric | Target |
|---|---|
| **CPM** (cost per 1,000 impressions) | Under ₹300 (lower is better) |
| **CPC** (cost per click) | Under ₹50 (lower is better) |
| **Impressions** | At least 1,000+ |
| **Link Clicks** | At least 50+ |
| **Conversions** (Purchase) | Aim for 1+ by day 3 |
| **ROAS** (Return on Ad Spend) | 3× or higher |

**Example:** If you spend ₹900 and get 3 sales at ₹300 each = ₹900 profit + ₹900 revenue = 2× ROAS (break-even).

---

### Step 20: Pause Losing Ads (Day 3-4)
If an ad has:
- CPM over ₹400
- CPC over ₹80
- 0 conversions after spending ₹1,000

**Pause it:** Click the ad → **Edit** → toggle **Status** to **Paused** → **Save**.

---

### Step 21: Scale the Winning Ad (Day 4-5)
If an ad has:
- CPM under ₹250
- At least 1+ conversions
- ROAS over 2×

**Increase budget:**
1. Go to the ad set
2. Click **Edit**
3. Change daily budget from ₹300 to ₹360 (20% increase)
4. Click **Save**

**Do this every 2 days** as long as ROAS stays above 2×.

## PART 6: ADD RETARGETING (Day 5+)

### Step 22: Create a Retargeting Campaign
After 3–4 days, start a second campaign to catch people who watched your ad but didn't buy.

1. Go to **Ads Manager** → **Create**
2. Campaign objective: **Conversions**
3. Campaign name: "DM Pack – Retargeting – Video Watchers"
4. Daily budget: ₹150

**Ad Set Setup:**

1. **Audience:** Click **Create New**
2. Choose **Custom Audience** → **Video Watchers**
3. Select your page/account
4. Choose "People who watched 50%+ of any video in the last 30 days"
5. Name it: "Video Watchers 50%+ (30d)"
6. Click **Audience Name:** Add another audience:
   - Add **Page Visitors** (last 7 days) if you installed pixel
   - Exclude: people who already bought (create a "Purchasers" audience from pixel data)

**Ad Creative:**
- Use the retargeting video from your script kit (15s urgency message)
- Same headline, description, URL

## PART 7: WEEKLY MONITORING

### Every Monday: Check Your Numbers
Create a simple tracking sheet:

| Week | Spend | Sales | Revenue | ROAS | Status |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | ₹2,100 | 4 | ₹1,200 | 0.57× | Too early, scaling |
| 2 | ₹3,500 | 15 | ₹4,500 | 1.28× | Close to break-even |
| 3 | ₹4,000 | 20 | ₹6,000 | 1.5× | Profitable, scaling |

**What to do:**
- ROAS < 1×? Pause the campaign, check your landing page, improve copy.
- ROAS 1–2×? Keep testing, scale slowly (20% budget increase every 2 days).
- ROAS > 2×? Scale aggressively (40% budget increase every day).
- ROAS > 3×? Double down, run 24/7 until it plateaus.

## PART 8: TROUBLESHOOTING

### Ads Not Approved?
**Check:** Ads Manager → Your Ad → **Status**

Common reasons:
- ✗ Link doesn't work — test your SuperProfile link on phone
- ✗ Image text covers > 20% — use fewer words on images
- ✗ Misleading claims — remove "guaranteed", "100% results"
- ✗ Copyright claim on music — use royalty-free audio only

**Fix:** Click ad → **Edit** → fix the issue → **Submit for Review** (takes 1–24 hours)

---

### Getting Clicks But No Sales?
1. Click your SuperProfile link on the ad (as a customer would)
2. Does it load? Does the price show?
3. Is the checkout button visible?
4. Try buying a test purchase (₹1, if possible)
5. If it breaks, contact SuperProfile support

---

### High Cost Per Click, No Sales?
1. Test different ad copies (A/B test by pausing 2 ads, keeping 1)
2. Test different hook videos
3. Lower your audience age to 25–35 (narrower = cheaper)
4. Switch to **Traffic** objective instead of **Conversions** (tests cheaper)

---

### ROAS Dropping Over Time?
This is normal (audience saturation). Fix it:
1. Expand audience (25–50 instead of 22–45)
2. Add new interests ("business growth", "passive income", "freelance")
3. Create a new video ad with a different hook
4. Lower daily budget, restart with fresh audience

## Questions During Campaign?

- **Why is CPM so high?** Your audience is small or saturated. Broaden interests.
- **Why no conversions?** Link may be broken, or price is too high. Test copy.
- **When should I stop?** When ROAS drops below 1× for 2 days straight.
- **Can I change the ad after launching?** Yes, but it resets learning (best to duplicate + create new).
- **How much should I spend?** Start with ₹300/day for 7 days (₹2,100 total), then scale if profitable.
