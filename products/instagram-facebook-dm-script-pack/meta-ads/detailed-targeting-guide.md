# Detailed Targeting Guide for Meta Ads
## Who to Target for DM Script Pack Sales

---

## OVERVIEW: Your Ideal Customer

**Primary profile:**
- Location: India
- Age: 22–45 (sweet spot: 25–35)
- Income: Middle to upper-middle class
- Job: Small business owner, freelancer, coach, reseller, service provider
- Pain point: Loses sales in DMs, doesn't know how to follow up, gets ghosted

**Where they hang out:** Instagram (DMs), Facebook groups, YouTube

## PART 6: HOW TO EXCLUDE AUDIENCES (Don't Waste Money)

### Who NOT to Target
Go to **Audience** → **Exclusions** and add:

| Exclude | Why |
|---|---|
| **People who already bought** | Don't re-sell to buyers (retargeting only) |
| **People under 18** | Too young to sell seriously |
| **Unemployed** | Lower purchasing power |
| **Users interested in MLM/network marketing** | May result in complaints (if you want to avoid) |

### How to exclude in Meta:
1. Go to **Detailed Targeting**
2. Scroll down to **Exclude People**
3. Add any interests/behaviors you want to EXCLUDE
4. These are marked with a red – icon

## PART 7: LOOKALIKE AUDIENCES (After 1-2 Weeks)

Once you have 50+ conversions (purchases), create Lookalike Audiences.

### How to Create Lookalike:

1. Go to **Meta Business Suite** → **Audiences**
2. Click **Create Audience** → **Lookalike Audience**
3. **Audience Source:** Choose your purchase audience (if you have pixel)
4. **Country:** India
5. **Audience Size:** 1% (most similar to buyers) — START HERE
6. Name it: "Lookalike 1% – Buyers"
7. **Duplicate at different sizes:**
   - 1% Lookalike (highest quality, smallest)
   - 2% Lookalike (good balance)
   - 5% Lookalike (more volume, less quality)

### How to Use Lookalike:
- Create new ad set targeting "Lookalike 1%"
- Budget: ₹500/day (test for 5 days)
- Compare ROAS with your original audience
- If better, scale it

## PART 8: CUSTOM AUDIENCES (For Retargeting)

After you have traffic, build Custom Audiences:

### Audience 1: Video Watchers (50%+)
**Who:** People who watched 50% or more of your video ads

**How to create:**
1. **Meta Business Suite** → **Audiences**
2. **Create Audience** → **Custom Audience**
3. **Data Source:** Meta pixel OR Instagram account
4. **Video Watchers:** Select 50%+ (last 30 days)
5. Name: "Video Watchers 50%+"
6. Use this audience to retarget with urgency copy

---

### Audience 2: Landing Page Visitors (No Purchase)
**Who:** People who clicked your ad, visited SuperProfile, but didn't buy

**How to create:**
1. (Requires Meta Pixel installed on SuperProfile)
2. **Data Source:** Meta Pixel
3. **View Content event:** (people who visited product page)
4. **Exclude:** Purchase event (remove buyers)
5. Name: "Viewed, Didn't Buy (7d)"
6. Retarget with "⏰ Last chance" copy

## PART 10: AUDIENCE TESTING STRATEGY (7-Day Plan)

### Week 1: Test 3 Audiences Side-by-Side

| Audience | Location | Age | Interests | Budget | Goal |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **A – Broad** | All India | 22–45 | 5 main interests | ₹300/day | Get volume fast |
| **B – Narrow** | Metros only | 25–35 | 5 main interests | ₹300/day | Lower cost, higher quality |
| **C – Stack** | All India | 25–40 | 8 combined interests | ₹300/day | Best balance |

**How to run:**
1. Create **ONE** ad set
2. Add **THREE** audiences (A, B, C) to the same ad set
3. Meta will optimize and show which wins
4. After 3 days, pause the worst performer
5. Keep spending on the 2 winners
