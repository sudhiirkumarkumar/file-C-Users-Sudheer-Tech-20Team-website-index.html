## 2. Campaign structure (Meta Ads Manager)

| Campaign | Objective | Audience | Daily budget | Goal |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| TOF – Cold | Sales (conversion event: Purchase) or Traffic if pixel not possible | Advantage+ audience, India, 22–45, interests: Instagram marketing, small business, online selling, Meesho, Shopify, digital marketing | ₹300–₹500 | Find the winning hook |
| TOF – Lookalike | Sales | 1% Lookalike of page engagers (90 days) + IG profile visitors | ₹300 | Scale winning creative |
| MOF – Retargeting | Sales | Video viewers 50%+, IG engagers 30 days, link clickers 14 days | ₹150–₹200 | Close the undecided |
| BOF – Cart abandon | Sales | SuperProfile page visitors 7 days (if pixel), minus purchasers | ₹100 | Urgency + proof |

**Setup notes**
1. Placements: Instagram Reels, Facebook Reels, Instagram Feed, Facebook Feed, Stories. Turn off Audience Network.
2. Format: 9:16 vertical video (15–30s) for Reels/Stories, 1:1 or 4:5 image/carousel for Feed.
3. Run 3 hooks × 2 formats = 6 ads in the cold ad set. Kill anything above 2× your target cost per purchase after 3 days. Scale the winner by 20% every 2 days.
4. If you cannot install a pixel on SuperProfile, optimize for Link Clicks for the first ₹2,000 of spend, then switch to Landing Page Views, and judge by sales in your SuperProfile dashboard.
5. Use the product hero image (`cover/product-hero-listing.jpg`) and 3D cover (`cover/product-cover-3d.jpg`) as the feed creatives.

## 10. Launch checklist

1. Install Meta Pixel on SuperProfile if the plan allows; otherwise add UTM tags to the link (`?utm_source=meta&utm_campaign=dmpack`) and track sales manually.
2. Upload 4 videos (Scripts A–D) + 1 carousel + 1 static image.
3. Cold ad set: 3 ads live, ₹300–₹500/day, 3 days untouched.
4. Day 4: pause ads with CPM above ₹400 and no purchases, duplicate the winner, raise budget 20%.
5. Day 5: switch on retargeting ad set with the retarget video and urgency copy.
6. Reply to every comment on the ad with a DM script from the pack — it is live proof the product works.
7. Target: cost per purchase under 40% of [PRICE] to be profitable after Meta and SuperProfile fees.
