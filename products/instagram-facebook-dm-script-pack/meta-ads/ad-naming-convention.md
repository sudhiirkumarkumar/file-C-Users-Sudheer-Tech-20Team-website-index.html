# Meta Ads Naming Convention Guide
## How to Name Your Ads for Easy Tracking & Organization

---

## WHY NAMING MATTERS

When you're running 10+ ads, you need to find the winner quickly. A good ad name tells you:
- ✅ What campaign it belongs to
- ✅ What creative type it uses (video, image, carousel)
- ✅ What hook or angle it's testing
- ✅ When it was created

Bad name: "Ad 1", "Video", "Campaign"
Good name: "DM Pack – Video A – Silent DM – 25-35"

---

## AD NAMING FORMULA

Use this structure (copy-paste and fill in):

```
[CAMPAIGN] – [FORMAT] – [HOOK] – [AUDIENCE]
```

### Formula Breakdown:

| Part | What Goes Here | Example |
|---|---|---|
| **[CAMPAIGN]** | Short campaign name | DM Pack, DM Pack Cold, DM Pack Retarget |
| **[FORMAT]** | Type of creative | Video A, Video B, Carousel, Image 1 |
| **[HOOK]** | The main message | Silent DM, Before/After, I Timed, Objection Stack |
| **[AUDIENCE]** | Target segment | 25-35, Metro, Broad, Resellers |

## QUICK REFERENCE: AD NAMING TEMPLATES

### Template 1 — Simplest (for beginners)
```
DM Pack – [Video/Image] – [Hook Name]
Example: DM Pack – Video A – Silent DM
```

### Template 2 — Medium Detail (recommended)
```
DM Pack – [Format] [Letter] – [Hook] – [Audience]
Example: DM Pack – Video A – Silent DM – 25-35
```

### Template 3 — Full Detail (for scaling)
```
[Campaign] – [Format] – [Hook] – [Audience] – [Date]
Example: DM Pack Cold – Video A – Silent DM – Metro 25-35 – Oct 7
```

### Template 4 — A/B Testing (testing variations)
```
DM Pack – Video A v1 – Silent DM – Original Copy
DM Pack – Video A v2 – Silent DM – Short Copy
DM Pack – Video A v3 – Silent DM – Urgency Copy
```

## HOW TO USE THESE NAMES IN ADS MANAGER

### Step 1: Create Your First Ad

In **Meta Ads Manager** → **Create Ad** section:

**Ad Name field:**
```
DM Pack – Video A – Silent DM – 25-35
```

### Step 2: View Your Ads

In **Ads Manager** → Click on your campaign:

You'll see a list like this:
```
DM Pack – Video A – Silent DM – 25-35          ✓ Running
DM Pack – Video B – Before After – 25-35       ✓ Running
DM Pack – Video C – I Timed – 25-35           ✓ Running
DM Pack – Carousel – Hero + Price – Metro     ✓ Running
```

### Step 3: Track Performance by Name

When comparing ads, you instantly see:
- Which **hook** wins (Silent DM? Before/After? I Timed?)
- Which **format** converts better (Video? Carousel? Image?)
- Which **audience** is cheaper (25-35? Metro? Broad?)

Example report:
```
DM Pack – Video A – Silent DM – 25-35    | CPM: ₹220 | CPC: ₹38 | ROAS: 2.1× ✅ WINNER
DM Pack – Video B – Before After – 25-35 | CPM: ₹280 | CPC: ₹45 | ROAS: 1.5× 
DM Pack – Video C – I Timed – 25-35      | CPM: ₹320 | CPC: ₹52 | ROAS: 0.9× ❌ PAUSE
```

## COMMON MISTAKES & HOW TO AVOID

### ❌ Bad Names
```
Ad 1, Ad 2, Ad 3
Campaign 1, Campaign 2
Video, Image, Carousel
Test, Test 2, Test 3
DM Pack Ad
New Ad
```

**Problem:** You can't tell which ad performs best or what you're testing.

### ✅ Good Names
```
DM Pack – Video A – Silent DM – 25-35
DM Pack – Video B – Before After – 25-35
DM Pack – Carousel – Hero + Price – Metro
DM Pack Retarget – Video – Urgency – Video Watchers
```

**Advantage:** You instantly see what's running and why.

---

## FINAL CHECKLIST

Before launching, check your ad names:

- [ ] Campaign name is clear (e.g., "DM Pack", "DM Pack Retarget")
- [ ] Format is obvious (Video A, Carousel, Image 1)
- [ ] Hook/angle is named (Silent DM, Before/After, etc.)
- [ ] Audience is specified (25-35, Metro, Broad, Coaches)
- [ ] Name is under 255 characters (Meta limit)
- [ ] No typos or numbers that don't mean anything
- [ ] All similar ads follow the same naming pattern
- [ ] You can search/find ads by name later

## AFTER LAUNCH: How to Find Winners by Name

In **Ads Manager**, click on your campaign and look at the table:

1. **Lowest CPM** → likely winning creative
2. **Most conversions** → best audience
3. **Highest ROAS** → scale this

Example:
```
Ad Name                                    CPM    Conversions  ROAS
─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
DM Pack – Video A – Silent DM – 25-35     ₹210      8         2.3× ✅ SCALE
DM Pack – Video B – Before After – 25-35  ₹280      5         1.8× Keep
DM Pack – Video C – I Timed – 25-35       ₹340      2         0.9× ❌ PAUSE
DM Pack – Carousel – Hero + Price – Metro  ₹320      3         1.2× Keep
```

**Action:** Scale Video A, keep B, pause C.
