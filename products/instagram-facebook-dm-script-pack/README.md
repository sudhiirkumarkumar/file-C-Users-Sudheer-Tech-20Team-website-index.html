# Instagram & Facebook DM Script Pack
## Ready-to-Use Messages for Leads, Customers & Sales

200 done-for-you DM scripts covering the full customer journey — from first contact to closing, support, reviews, and referrals — across 20 sections and 4 batches.

## Target Audience

This pack is built for anyone who sells or supports customers through Instagram and Facebook DMs, including:

- **Social sellers & Instagram/Facebook shop owners** who need consistent, high-converting messages for leads and customers without writing from scratch every time.
- **Coaches, consultants & service providers** who rely on DMs to qualify leads, handle objections, and close sales.
- **Small business owners & solopreneurs** managing their own social channels who want a professional voice without hiring a copywriter.
- **Social media managers & virtual assistants** who handle DMs for clients and need a repeatable script library to stay on-brand and responsive.
- **Customer support & community teams** looking for tested templates for onboarding, complaints, reviews, and retention conversations.
- **E-commerce & dropshipping sellers** who need scripts for post-purchase follow-up, upsells, and referral requests.

Anyone who wants faster, more consistent DM conversations — without sounding automated — will get value from this pack.

## What's Inside

| Batch | Sections | Focus |
|---|---|---|
| Batch 1 | 1–5 | Conversation starters, engagement, product inquiries, lead qualification, follow-ups |
| Batch 2 | 6–10 | Objection handling, closing & sales, post-purchase service, upsells, referrals & retention |
| Batch 3 | 11–15 | Price & trust objections, difficult customers, complaints, boundaries |
| Batch 4 | 16–20 | Customer support, reviews, referral programs, community & loyalty |

Each script includes the situation it's for, the ready-to-send DM, personalization placeholders, and a tip for using it effectively.
