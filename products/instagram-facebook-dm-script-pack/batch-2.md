## Section 6: Objection Handling DMs

### Script 51 — "It's Too Expensive"
**Situation:**
A lead says your price is too high and you need to respond without being defensive or dropping the price impulsively.
**DM:**
Totally understand, [Name] — budget matters. Can I ask what you were expecting to invest? That way I can see if there's an option that fits better, like [Alternative Option].
**Personalize:**
[Name], [Alternative Option]
**Tip:**
Ask about their expectation before offering a discount — sometimes the issue is value clarity, not price itself.

### Script 52 — "I Need to Think About It"
**Situation:**
A lead is hesitant and wants time before deciding.
**DM:**
Of course, [Name], no rush at all. While you're thinking it over, is there anything specific that's holding you back? Happy to clear it up.
**Personalize:**
[Name]
**Tip:**
This question often surfaces the real objection hiding behind "I need to think about it."

### Script 53 — "I'll Come Back Later"
**Situation:**
A lead postpones the decision without giving a clear reason.
**DM:**
No problem, [Name] — whenever works for you. If it helps, I can keep the details handy so it's easy to pick back up when you're ready.
**Personalize:**
[Name]
**Tip:**
Offering to "keep it handy" removes friction for when they do return.

### Script 54 — "I'm Not Sure It'll Work For Me"
**Situation:**
A lead doubts whether your product or service fits their specific situation.
**DM:**
That's a fair concern, [Name]. Can you tell me a bit more about your situation with [Topic]? That'll help me be honest about whether it's a good fit.
**Personalize:**
[Name], [Topic]
**Tip:**
Being willing to say "it might not be a fit" builds more trust than pushing past the doubt.

### Script 55 — "I Don't Have Time Right Now"
**Situation:**
A lead is interested but feels they don't have bandwidth currently.
**DM:**
Totally get it, [Name] — things get busy. [Product/Service] is actually built to take [Time Commitment], so it might fit better than you'd think. Want me to explain how it works?
**Personalize:**
[Name], [Product/Service], [Time Commitment]
**Tip:**
Address the time concern with a real, specific number — vague reassurance won't overcome this objection.

### Script 56 — "I Need to Ask Someone Else First"
**Situation:**
A lead needs approval or input from a partner, boss, or spouse before deciding.
**DM:**

**Personalize:**
[Name]
**Tip:**
Offering a shareable summary makes it easier for them to advocate for you internally.

### Script 57 — "I've Tried Something Similar Before"
**Situation:**
A lead has had a past experience with a similar product or service that didn't work out.
**DM:**

**Personalize:**
[Name], [Product/Service]

Listen first — don't assume your offer solves their past frustration until you know what it was.

### Script 58 — "I'm Not Ready Yet"
**Situation:**
A lead is interested but doesn't feel it's the right time to commit.
**DM:**

**Personalize:**

**Tip:**

### Script 59 — Comparing to a Competitor

**Situation:**

## Section 7: Closing & Sales DMs

**DM:**

**Personalize:**
[Name], [Product/Service]
**Tip:**
Only use this after genuine qualification — a premature close can feel pushy.

**DM:**

[Name], [Link]

### Script 65 — Bundle/Package Offer

**Tip:**
Only suggest a bundle if it genuinely matches their needs, not just because it's a bigger sale.

[Name], [Custom Recommendation]

[Name], [Product/Service], [Instructions]

[Name], [Product/Service]

**Personalize:**
[Name], [Product], [Complementary Product], [Benefit]
**Tip:**
Frame it around a specific benefit, not just "you might also like this."

**Personalize:**
[Name], [Current Plan], [Upgraded Plan], [Extra Benefit]

Reference their actual usage or results before suggesting an upgrade — it should feel earned, not generic.
