# Instagram & Facebook DM Script Pack
## Ready-to-Use Messages for Leads, Customers & Sales
### Batch 1 — 50 Scripts

---

## Section 1: Conversation Starters

### Script 01 — New Follower Welcome
**Situation:**
Someone new follows your account and you want to open a natural conversation without sounding automated.
**DM:**
Hey [Name], thanks for the follow! I always like to say hi to new faces around here — what brought you to [Business Name]?
**Personalize:**
[Name], [Business Name]
**Tip:**
Send this within 24-48 hours of the follow so it still feels timely and relevant.

### Script 02 — New Connection
**Situation:**
You've just connected with someone through a mutual contact, group, or event and want to start building the relationship.
**DM:**
Hi [Name], [Mutual Contact] mentioned we should connect — I run [Business Name] and always enjoy meeting people in [Industry/Niche]. How's things going on your end?
**Personalize:**
[Name], [Mutual Contact], [Business Name], [Industry/Niche]
**Tip:**
Naming the mutual connection first builds instant trust and context.

### Script 03 — Warm Introduction
**Situation:**
A client, friend, or partner has introduced you to a potential lead and you're reaching out for the first time.
**DM:**
Hey [Name], [Referrer Name] said we should chat — they mentioned you're looking into [Topic/Need]. I'd love to hear more about what you're working on.
**Personalize:**
[Name], [Referrer Name], [Topic/Need]
**Tip:**
Reference the referrer's exact words if possible — it shows you were actually briefed, not just cold messaging.

### Script 04 — Content-Based Conversation
**Situation:**
Someone left a thoughtful comment on your post and you want to continue the conversation in DMs.
**DM:**
Hey [Name], loved your comment on my [post about X] — curious, is that something you're dealing with right now too?
**Personalize:**
[Name], [post about X]
**Tip:**
Ask a genuine follow-up question rather than pitching anything right away.

### Script 05 — Networking Outreach
**Situation:**
You want to connect with someone in your industry for potential collaboration, not a sale.
**DM:**
Hi [Name], I've been following your work at [Business Name/Account] for a while — really like your approach to [Specific Thing]. Would love to connect and swap notes sometime.
**Personalize:**
[Name], [Business Name/Account], [Specific Thing]
**Tip:**
Keep the tone peer-to-peer, not pitchy — this is about building a relationship first.

### Script 06 — Potential Customer Opener
**Situation:**
You've noticed someone engaging with your niche or products but haven't interacted with you directly yet.
**DM:**
Hi [Name], noticed you're into [Topic/Interest] — I actually help people with exactly that through [Product/Service]. Mind if I share a bit more?
**Personalize:**
[Name], [Topic/Interest], [Product/Service]
**Tip:**
Ask permission before pitching — it respects their time and gets a better response rate.

### Script 07 — Potential Business Client
**Situation:**
Reaching out to a business account or decision-maker who could become a B2B client.
**DM:**
Hi [Name], I work with businesses like [Business Name] on [Service Area] and thought there might be a good fit here. Would you be open to a quick chat about what you're currently doing for [Area]?
**Personalize:**
[Name], [Business Name], [Service Area], [Area]
**Tip:**
Lead with relevance to their business, not your offer — it reads as research, not a template.

### Script 08 — Friendly Opener
**Situation:**
A low-pressure, casual first message to someone in your community with no immediate sales intent.
**DM:**
Hey [Name]! Just wanted to say hi — I've enjoyed seeing your posts about [Topic]. Hope things are going well with [Business Name/Project]!
**Personalize:**
[Name], [Topic], [Business Name/Project]
**Tip:**
Don't attach any ask to this one — let it stand as a genuine, no-strings message.

### Script 09 — Re-Engagement Opener
**Situation:**
Reconnecting with someone you spoke with a while ago but the conversation went quiet.
**DM:**
Hey [Name], it's been a bit! Just checking in — how did things end up going with [Previous Topic/Project]?
**Personalize:**
[Name], [Previous Topic/Project]
**Tip:**
Reference something specific from your last chat so it doesn't feel like a cold restart.

### Script 10 — General Inquiry Opener
**Situation:**
Someone messages your page with a vague greeting like "Hi" or "Hello" and you need to guide the conversation.
**DM:**
Hi [Name], thanks for reaching out! Happy to help — are you looking for info on [Product], [Service], or something else I can point you to?
**Personalize:**
[Name], [Product], [Service]
**Tip:**
Give 2-3 concrete options so the person doesn't have to guess what to ask about.

## Section 2: Instagram Story & Engagement DMs

### Script 11 — Story Reaction Reply
**Situation:**
Someone reacts to your Instagram Story with an emoji and you want to turn it into a conversation.
**DM:**
Hey [Name], thanks for the reaction! Was that story about [Topic] something you're currently thinking about too?
**Personalize:**
[Name], [Topic]
**Tip:**
Keep it light — a reaction is a small signal, so don't over-pitch this early.

### Script 12 — Story Poll Response
**Situation:**
Someone votes on a poll sticker in your story and you want to follow up on their choice.
**DM:**
Hi [Name], saw you picked "[Poll Option]" on my poll — good choice! Want me to send over more info on that?
**Personalize:**
[Name], [Poll Option]
**Tip:**
This works best when your poll directly relates to a product or service you offer.

### Script 13 — Question Sticker Follow-Up
**Situation:**
Someone submitted a question through your Story's question sticker and you're following up with a fuller answer.
**DM:**

**Personalize:**
[Name], [Topic], [Short Answer]
**Tip:**
Answer in the DM even if you also feature the question publicly — it makes the person feel personally heard.

### Script 14 — Reel Engagement Follow-Up
**Situation:**
Someone comments on or shares your Reel, showing strong interest in the topic.
**DM:**
Hey [Name], thanks for sharing/commenting on that Reel about [Topic]! Was there a part of it that stood out to you the most?
**Personalize:**
[Name], [Topic]
**Tip:**
Asking what stood out gives you insight into what they actually care about before you pitch anything.

### Script 15 — Product Story Response
**Situation:**
Someone reacts to a story where you featured a specific product.
**DM:**
Hi [Name], glad you liked the story on [Product]! It's designed for [Use Case] — want me to send the details or pricing?
**Personalize:**
[Name], [Product], [Use Case]
**Tip:**
Offer a direct next step (details or pricing) instead of leaving the conversation open-ended.

### Script 16 — Educational Story Response
**Situation:**
Someone engages with an educational or tip-based story you posted.
**DM:**
Hey [Name], glad that tip on [Topic] was useful! If you want, I can send a couple more resources on [Related Topic].
**Personalize:**
[Name], [Topic], [Related Topic]
**Tip:**
Offering more value first builds trust before any product conversation starts.

### Script 17 — Customer Story Response
**Situation:**
Someone reacts to a customer testimonial or success story you shared.
**DM:**
Hi [Name], thanks for checking out that story! [Customer Name]'s results came from [Product/Service] — happy to explain how it could work for your situation too.
**Personalize:**
[Name], [Customer Name], [Product/Service]
**Tip:**
Use a real, specific result rather than a vague claim to keep it credible.

### Script 18 — Behind-the-Scenes Story Response
**Situation:**
Someone engages with a behind-the-scenes look at your business or process.
**DM:**
Hey [Name], thanks for the love on that behind-the-scenes look! That's actually how we put together [Product/Service] — curious what part interested you most?
**Personalize:**
[Name], [Product/Service]
**Tip:**
Behind-the-scenes content builds trust — use the reply to deepen that, not to sell immediately.

### Script 19 — Interest-Based Response
**Situation:**
Someone consistently engages with a specific type of content (e.g., always reacts to pricing or product posts).
**DM:**
Hi [Name], noticed you've checked out a few of my posts on [Topic] — are you exploring [Product/Service] for yourself right now?
**Personalize:**
[Name], [Topic], [Product/Service]
**Tip:**
This works well after 3+ engagements on similar content — it shows genuine, not random, interest.

### Script 20 — Moving Engagement to Conversation
**Situation:**
You want to shift someone from passive engagement (likes, views) into an active DM conversation.
**DM:**
Hey [Name], I've noticed you around my page lately — always nice to see familiar faces! Is there anything specific about [Topic/Product] I can help you with?
**Personalize:**
[Name], [Topic/Product]
**Tip:**
Frame it as noticing them, not tracking them — keep the tone warm and casual.

## Section 3: Product & Service Inquiry DMs

### Script 21 — "How Much?" Response
**Situation:**
A lead asks for pricing directly, often as their first message.
**DM:**
Hi [Name], happy to share! [Product/Service] starts at [Price]. To make sure I give you the right option, can I ask what you're mainly looking to achieve?
**Personalize:**
[Name], [Product/Service], [Price]
**Tip:**
Give the price first, then ask a qualifying question — withholding pricing often kills trust.

### Script 22 — "What Do You Offer?" Response
**Situation:**
Someone wants a general overview of what your business provides.
**DM:**
Hi [Name], thanks for asking! I offer [Product/Service 1] and [Product/Service 2], both aimed at helping [Target Outcome]. Is there one that sounds more relevant to you?
**Personalize:**
[Name], [Product/Service 1], [Product/Service 2], [Target Outcome]
**Tip:**
Keep the overview to 2-3 offers max so the person isn't overwhelmed.

### Script 23 — "Send Me Details" Response
**Situation:**
A lead asks for more information without specifying what exactly they want to know.
**DM:**
Sure thing, [Name]! Here's a quick rundown of [Product/Service]: [Short Details]. Want me to send the full link too?
**Personalize:**
[Name], [Product/Service], [Short Details]
**Tip:**
Summarize in 2-3 lines before sending a link — most people skim, they don't click blind.

### Script 24 — "What Is Included?" Response
**Situation:**
Someone wants to know exactly what they get with a purchase.
**DM:**
Hi [Name], great question! [Product/Service] includes [Item 1], [Item 2], and [Item 3]. Let me know if you'd like me to break any part down further.
**Personalize:**
[Name], [Product/Service], [Item 1], [Item 2], [Item 3]
**Tip:**
List items clearly with line breaks or bullets — it reads faster than one long sentence.

### Script 25 — "How Does It Work?" Response
**Situation:**
A lead wants to understand the process or mechanics of your product or service.
**DM:**
Hi [Name], happy to explain! Basically, [Step 1], then [Step 2], and finally [Step 3]. It's pretty straightforward — any part you'd like me to expand on?
**Personalize:**
[Name], [Step 1], [Step 2], [Step 3]
**Tip:**
Break the process into 3 simple steps max — too much detail upfront can overwhelm a new lead.

### Script 26 — "Is This For Beginners?" Response
**Situation:**
Someone is unsure if your product or service matches their experience level.
**DM:**
Hi [Name], yes, [Product/Service] is designed to work for beginners too — it starts with [Beginner-Friendly Feature]. What's your current experience with [Topic]?
**Personalize:**
[Name], [Product/Service], [Beginner-Friendly Feature], [Topic]
**Tip:**
Asking about their experience helps you tailor the rest of the conversation accurately.

### Script 27 — "Can I See a Sample?" Response
**Situation:**
A lead wants proof or a preview before committing to buy.
**DM:**
Of course, [Name]! Here's a sample of [Product/Service]: [Link/Attachment]. Let me know what you think once you've had a look.
**Personalize:**
[Name], [Product/Service], [Link/Attachment]
**Tip:**
Always follow up after sending a sample — silence after a sample often just means they forgot to reply.

### Script 28 — "Where Can I Buy?" Response
**Situation:**
Someone is ready to purchase and just needs the link or process.
**DM:**
Great news, [Name]! You can grab [Product/Service] right here: [Link]. Let me know if you hit any issues checking out.
**Personalize:**
[Name], [Product/Service], [Link]
**Tip:**
Offer to help with checkout issues — it removes friction and shows you're available.

### Script 29 — "Do You Provide Support?" Response
**Situation:**
A lead wants reassurance about after-purchase help before buying.
**DM:**
Yes, [Name], you'll have [Support Type — e.g., email/chat support] after purchase, so you're never left figuring things out alone. Does that answer your question?
**Personalize:**
[Name], [Support Type]
**Tip:**
Be specific about the type and availability of support — vague reassurance doesn't build confidence.

### Script 30 — General Product Inquiry
**Situation:**
A lead asks a broad or unclear question about your product without specifics.
**DM:**
Hi [Name], happy to help! Just so I point you in the right direction — are you asking about pricing, what's included, or how [Product/Service] actually works?
**Personalize:**
[Name], [Product/Service]
**Tip:**
Use this to narrow a vague question into something you can answer precisely.

## Section 4: Lead Qualification DMs

### Script 31 — Understanding Customer Needs
**Situation:**
You want to learn more about what the lead actually needs before recommending anything.
**DM:**
Hi [Name], before I suggest anything, I'd love to understand your situation a bit better — what are you currently trying to achieve with [Topic/Area]?
**Personalize:**
[Name], [Topic/Area]
**Tip:**
Ask this before pitching — recommendations land better when they're clearly tailored.

### Script 32 — Identifying the Customer's Problem
**Situation:**
You want to pinpoint the specific challenge the lead is facing.
**DM:**
Hi [Name], can I ask — what's the biggest challenge you're running into with [Topic] right now?
**Personalize:**
[Name], [Topic]
**Tip:**
Let them answer in their own words — you'll often get language you can reuse later in your pitch.

### Script 33 — Asking Budget-Related Questions Naturally
**Situation:**
You need to understand budget range without sounding blunt or transactional.
**DM:**
Hi [Name], to make sure I recommend the right fit, do you have a rough budget range in mind for [Product/Service], or would you like me to share a few options at different price points?
**Personalize:**
[Name], [Product/Service]
**Tip:**
Offering "a few price points" as an alternative makes the question feel optional, not pushy.

### Script 34 — Understanding Requirements
**Situation:**
You need specific details about what the customer needs before quoting or recommending.
**DM:**
Hi [Name], quick question so I can point you to the right option — are you looking for [Option A] or more like [Option B]?
**Personalize:**
[Name], [Option A], [Option B]
**Tip:**
Giving two clear choices is easier to answer than an open-ended "what do you need?"

### Script 35 — Service Qualification
**Situation:**
You want to confirm the lead is a good fit for your service before moving forward.
**DM:**
Hi [Name], to see if [Service] is the right fit, can I ask what stage you're at with [Area] right now — just starting out, or already have something in place?
**Personalize:**
[Name], [Service], [Area]
**Tip:**
This helps you avoid pitching the wrong tier of service to someone at the wrong stage.

### Script 36 — Product Suitability
**Situation:**
You want to confirm the product actually solves the lead's specific need.
**DM:**
Hi [Name], just to make sure [Product] is the right match — is your main goal more about [Outcome A] or [Outcome B]?
**Personalize:**
[Name], [Product], [Outcome A], [Outcome B]
**Tip:**
If neither outcome fits, be upfront — it builds trust even if it means no sale today.

### Script 37 — Asking About Goals
**Situation:**
You want to understand what success looks like for the lead.
**DM:**
Hi [Name], curious — what would success look like for you with [Topic/Product] a few months from now?
**Personalize:**
[Name], [Topic/Product]
**Tip:**
This question often reveals the real motivation behind their interest, not just the surface request.

### Script 38 — Identifying Urgency
**Situation:**
You want to know how soon the lead is looking to act, without pressuring them.
**DM:**
Hi [Name], is this something you're hoping to sort out soon, or more just exploring options for now?
**Personalize:**
[Name]
**Tip:**
Their answer tells you whether to follow up quickly or give them more space.

### Script 39 — Determining Decision Stage
**Situation:**
You want to know if the lead is comparing options, ready to buy, or just researching.
**DM:**
Hi [Name], are you currently comparing a few options, or already leaning toward moving forward with something like [Product/Service]?
**Personalize:**
[Name], [Product/Service]
**Tip:**
Knowing their decision stage tells you whether to educate more or move straight to an offer.

### Script 40 — Moving Qualified Leads Toward an Offer
**Situation:**
Based on their answers, the lead is a good fit and ready for a direct next step.
**DM:**
Hi [Name], based on what you've shared, I think [Product/Service] would be a great fit for [Their Goal]. Want me to send over the details to get started?
**Personalize:**
[Name], [Product/Service], [Their Goal]
**Tip:**
Only use this once you've confirmed fit through prior questions — it should feel like a natural next step, not a pitch out of nowhere.

## Section 5: Follow-Up DMs

### Script 41 — Follow-Up After Sending Details
**Situation:**
You sent product or service details and haven't heard back yet.
**DM:**
Hi [Name], just checking in — did you get a chance to look over the details on [Product/Service]? Happy to answer any questions.
**Personalize:**
[Name], [Product/Service]
**Tip:**
Send this 1-2 days after sharing details, not immediately, to give them time to review.

### Script 42 — Follow-Up After Sending Price
**Situation:**
You shared pricing and want to check in without sounding pushy.
**DM:**
Hi [Name], following up on the pricing for [Product/Service] — happy to break it down further or answer anything that's unclear.
**Personalize:**
[Name], [Product/Service]
**Tip:**
Offer to clarify rather than asking "did you decide?" — it keeps the tone helpful, not pressuring.

### Script 43 — Follow-Up After Sending a Link
**Situation:**
You sent a purchase or booking link and want to confirm they received it okay.
**DM:**
Hi [Name], just making sure the link for [Product/Service] came through okay — let me know if you run into any trouble accessing it.
**Personalize:**
[Name], [Product/Service]
**Tip:**
Framing it as a technical check-in feels less like a sales push and more like genuine support.

### Script 44 — 24-Hour Follow-Up
**Situation:**
A day has passed since your last message with no response.
**DM:**
Hi [Name], just floating this back up in case it got buried — still happy to help if you have any questions about [Topic/Product].
**Personalize:**
[Name], [Topic/Product]
**Tip:**
"Floating this back up" is a light, non-intrusive way to re-surface the conversation.

### Script 45 — 3-Day Follow-Up
**Situation:**
Several days have passed with no reply and you want to check in once more.
**DM:**
Hi [Name], hope things have been busy on your end! Wanted to check if you still have questions about [Product/Service] or if now isn't the right time.
**Personalize:**
[Name], [Product/Service]
**Tip:**
Giving them an easy out ("if now isn't the right time") often prompts an honest response either way.

### Script 46 — No-Response Follow-Up
**Situation:**
Multiple follow-ups have gone unanswered and you want one more low-pressure attempt.
**DM:**
Hi [Name], no worries if the timing isn't right — just let me know whenever you're ready to pick this back up, I'm here.
**Personalize:**
[Name]
**Tip:**
This message removes pressure entirely, which sometimes is exactly what prompts a reply.

### Script 47 — Gentle Reminder
**Situation:**
You want to remind the lead about a deadline, appointment, or open offer without pressure.
**DM:**
Hi [Name], just a gentle reminder about [Offer/Appointment] — let me know if you need anything from me before then.
**Personalize:**
[Name], [Offer/Appointment]
**Tip:**
Keep reminders factual and calm — avoid pairing them with pressure language.

### Script 48 — Re-Engagement
**Situation:**
The conversation went cold weeks or months ago and you want to reopen it naturally.
**DM:**
Hi [Name], it's been a little while! Wanted to check in and see how things are going with [Topic/Area] — still relevant for you these days?
**Personalize:**
[Name], [Topic/Area]
**Tip:**
Treat this as a fresh conversation, not a continuation of an old pitch.

### Script 49 — Final Polite Follow-Up
**Situation:**
You want to send a last, respectful message before stepping back from the conversation.
**DM:**
Hi [Name], I'll leave this here for now so I'm not filling your inbox! If [Product/Service] becomes relevant down the line, I'm just a message away.
**Personalize:**
[Name], [Product/Service]
**Tip:**
Ending on a warm, no-pressure note keeps the door open for future contact.

### Script 50 — Follow-Up After Customer Shows Interest
**Situation:**
The lead has responded positively and you want to move the conversation forward.
**DM:**
Hi [Name], glad to hear you're interested! The next step is [Next Step — e.g., sending the link, booking a call]. Want me to go ahead and set that up?
**Personalize:**
[Name], [Next Step]
**Tip:**
Give one clear next action — momentum is easiest to keep when the next step is obvious.
