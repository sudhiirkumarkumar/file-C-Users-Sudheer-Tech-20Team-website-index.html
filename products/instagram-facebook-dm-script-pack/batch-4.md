## Section 16: Customer Support — General & Onboarding Support

### Script 151 — Welcome Support Introduction
**Situation:**
A new customer needs to know how and where to reach you for support going forward.
**DM:**
Hi [Name], welcome again! If you ever need help with [Product/Service], just message me here — I usually reply within [Response Time].
**Personalize:**
[Name], [Product/Service], [Response Time]
**Tip:**
Setting a clear response-time expectation upfront prevents frustration later if replies aren't instant.

### Script 152 — FAQ Quick-Answer Template
**Situation:**
A customer asks a question that comes up often and you want a fast, clear answer.
**DM:**
Hi [Name], great question! [Short Answer]. Let me know if that covers it or if you'd like more detail.
**Personalize:**
[Name], [Short Answer]
**Tip:**
Keep a running list of your most common questions so you can answer quickly and consistently.

### Script 153 — Password/Login Issue
**Situation:**
A customer can't access their account or is having login trouble.
**DM:**
Hi [Name], sorry about that! Try [Reset Step — e.g., resetting your password via this link]. Let me know if you're still locked out after that.
**Personalize:**
[Name], [Reset Step]
**Tip:**
Give one clear action first rather than a full list of troubleshooting steps — it's faster to follow.

### Script 154 — Order Status Inquiry
**Situation:**
A customer wants to know the current status of their order.
**DM:**
Hi [Name], let me check that for you — your order is currently [Status]. Expected to [Next Milestone] by [Date].
**Personalize:**
[Name], [Status], [Next Milestone], [Date]
**Tip:**
Always give a specific date or timeframe rather than "soon" — it reduces repeat check-ins.

### Script 155 — Shipping/Tracking Inquiry
**Situation:**
A customer wants tracking details for a physical product order.
**DM:**
Hi [Name], here's your tracking info: [Tracking Link/Number]. It looks like it's currently [Status] and should arrive by [Date].
**Personalize:**
[Name], [Tracking Link/Number], [Status], [Date]
**Tip:**
Link directly to the tracking page when possible — it saves the customer an extra step.

### Script 156 — Cancellation Request Handling
**Situation:**
A customer wants to cancel an order, subscription, or booking.
**DM:**
Hi [Name], I can take care of that for you. Just to confirm — you'd like to cancel [Item/Service], correct? I'll process it right after you confirm.
**Personalize:**
[Name], [Item/Service]
**Tip:**
Always confirm the exact item before cancelling — it prevents accidental cancellations of the wrong thing.

### Script 157 — Modifying an Order or Appointment
**Situation:**
A customer wants to change details of an existing order or booking.
**DM:**
Hi [Name], happy to update that! You'd like to change it to [New Detail], is that right? I'll get it adjusted once you confirm.
**Personalize:**
[Name], [New Detail]
**Tip:**
Restate the change back to them before making it — this avoids miscommunication on updates.

### Script 158 — Explaining a Feature or How-To
**Situation:**
A customer needs guidance on how to use a specific feature of your product or service.
**DM:**
Hi [Name], good question! Here's how [Feature] works: [Short Explanation]. Let me know if you'd like a walkthrough of anything else.
**Personalize:**
[Name], [Feature], [Short Explanation]
**Tip:**
Offer a follow-up walkthrough proactively — some customers won't ask even if they're still confused.

### Script 159 — Multi-Issue Support Triage
**Situation:**
A customer raises several issues at once and you need to organize the response.
**DM:**
Hi [Name], thanks for laying all that out! Let's tackle them one at a time — starting with [Issue 1]: [Answer]. I'll follow up on the rest right after.
**Personalize:**
[Name], [Issue 1], [Answer]
**Tip:**
Addressing issues one at a time keeps the conversation organized and avoids answers getting lost.

### Script 160 — Support Wrap-Up/Closing Confirmation
**Situation:**
An issue has been resolved and you want to formally close out the support conversation.
**DM:**
Hi [Name], glad we got that sorted! Just checking — is everything working the way it should now, or is there anything else I can help with?
**Personalize:**
[Name]
**Tip:**
Always end support conversations with an open question — it catches any lingering issues before they escalate.

### Script 170 — Confirming a Resolved Issue Long-Term
**Situation:**

## Section 18: Review Requests

### Script 171 — First-Time Review Request After Purchase
**Situation:**
A customer has just had a positive first experience and you want to ask for a review.
**DM:**
Hi [Name], so glad [Product/Service] has been working out! If you have a minute, a quick review would really help others find us too.
**Personalize:**
[Name], [Product/Service]
**Tip:**
Only send this after confirming satisfaction — timing the ask right matters more than the wording.

### Script 172 — Review Request With Direct Link/Instructions
**Situation:**
You want to make leaving a review as frictionless as possible.
**DM:**
Hi [Name], here's the direct link if you'd like to leave a review: [Link]. Takes less than a minute — really appreciate it either way!
**Personalize:**
[Name], [Link]
**Tip:**
Always include the direct link — extra steps to find where to review significantly lower response rates.

### Script 173 — Video Testimonial Request
**Situation:**
You want to ask a satisfied customer for a short video testimonial instead of a text review.
**DM:**

**Personalize:**
[Name], [Product/Service]
**Tip:**
Mention the time commitment upfront — "30 seconds" feels far more manageable than an open-ended ask.

### Script 174 — Review Request After Milestone Usage
**Situation:**
A customer has used your product or service long enough to have a well-formed opinion.
**DM:**
Hi [Name], you've been using [Product/Service] for [Time Period] now — I'd love to hear how it's been if you're open to sharing a review.
**Personalize:**
[Name], [Product/Service], [Time Period]
**Tip:**
Asking after real usage time produces more detailed, credible reviews than asking right after purchase.

### Script 175 — Gentle Review Reminder
**Situation:**
A customer agreed to leave a review but hasn't gotten around to it yet.
**DM:**
Hi [Name], no pressure at all — just floating this back up in case it got buried. Here's the link again if you still want to leave that review: [Link].
**Personalize:**
[Name], [Link]
**Tip:**
One gentle reminder is fine — avoid repeated follow-ups, which can feel like nagging.

### Script 176 — Requesting a Review on a Specific Platform
**Situation:**
You want the review to be posted on a particular platform, like Google or Facebook.
**DM:**

**Personalize:**
[Name], [Link]

### Script 177 — Responding to a Positive Review Received

**Personalize:**

### Script 179 — Asking Permission to Feature a Testimonial

**Personalize:**

### Script 180 — Thank-You After Review Is Posted
**Situation:**
A customer's review has gone live and you want to formally thank them.
**DM:**

**Personalize:**
[Name], [Business Name]

## Section 19: Referral Requests & Referral Programs

### Script 181 — Introducing a Referral Program
**Situation:**
You want to let a customer know about a referral program for the first time.
**DM:**
Hi [Name], quick heads-up — I actually have a referral program where you get [Reward] for every person you send my way who becomes a customer. Want the details?
**Personalize:**
[Name], [Reward]
**Tip:**
Lead with the specific reward — vague "referral perks" language gets less engagement than a concrete number.

### Script 182 — Explaining Referral Rewards/Incentive
**Situation:**
A customer is interested in the referral program and wants to know exactly how it works.
**DM:**
Hi [Name], here's how it works: you share [Referral Link/Code], and once someone you refer [Action — e.g., makes a purchase], you both get [Reward]. Simple as that!
**Personalize:**
[Name], [Referral Link/Code], [Action], [Reward]
**Tip:**
Keep the mechanics to two or three steps max — complicated referral processes discourage participation.

### Script 183 — Asking a Happy Customer for a Referral
**Situation:**
A customer has expressed satisfaction and you want to ask if they know others who might benefit.
**DM:**
Hi [Name], really glad to hear [Product/Service] has worked out so well! Do you know anyone else who might benefit from something similar?
**Personalize:**
[Name], [Product/Service]
**Tip:**
Ask this in the moment right after they express satisfaction — it's the most natural time to bring it up.

### Script 184 — Reminding About the Referral Program
**Situation:**
A customer knows about your referral program but hasn't used it, and you want to remind them casually.
**DM:**
Hi [Name], just a friendly reminder — you're still eligible for [Reward] anytime you refer someone to [Business Name]. No rush, just wanted it on your radar.
**Personalize:**
[Name], [Reward], [Business Name]
**Tip:**
Frame it as a reminder, not a push — it keeps the tone helpful rather than salesy.

### Script 185 — Thanking for a Successful Referral Conversion
**Situation:**
Someone a customer referred has become a paying customer, and you want to close the loop.
**DM:**
Hi [Name], great news — [Referred Name] just became a customer! Your [Reward] is on its way. Thanks again for thinking of us.
**Personalize:**
[Name], [Referred Name], [Reward]
**Tip:**
Confirming the reward is "on its way" reassures the customer the program is genuinely honored, not just promised.

### Script 186 — Group/Team Referral Request (B2B)
**Situation:**
You're asking a business contact if their team or company might benefit from your services.
**DM:**
Hi [Name], since [Service] has worked well for your team, do you think it could be useful for any other teams or departments at [Company]? Happy to put together something tailored if so.
**Personalize:**
[Name], [Service], [Company]
**Tip:**
Offering something "tailored" signals you're not just trying to duplicate the same pitch elsewhere.

### Script 187 — Referral Request Tied to a Specific Result
**Situation:**
A customer has achieved a clear, specific result and you want to connect that to a referral ask.
**DM:**
Hi [Name], that's such a great result with [Specific Outcome]! If you know anyone chasing something similar, I'd love an introduction.
**Personalize:**
[Name], [Specific Outcome]
**Tip:**
Tying the ask to a concrete result makes the referral feel earned rather than randomly timed.

### Script 188 — Asking for a Social Share or Tag
**Situation:**
You want a customer to share or tag your business on social media rather than a formal referral.
**DM:**
Hi [Name], if you ever post about [Product/Service], I'd love it if you tagged [Business Name] — always fun to see it in action!
**Personalize:**
[Name], [Product/Service], [Business Name]
**Tip:**
Keep this ask casual — it should feel like an invitation, not an obligation.

### Script 189 — Partner/Collaboration Referral Request
**Situation:**
You're reaching out to another business owner about cross-referring customers to each other.
**DM:**
Hi [Name], I think our audiences might overlap well — would you be open to referring each other's businesses when it makes sense? Happy to talk through how that could work.
**Personalize:**
[Name]
**Tip:**
Frame this as mutual value from the start — a one-sided referral ask to another business rarely lands well.

### Script 190 — Referral Leaderboard/Incentive Update
**Situation:**
You're updating an engaged referrer on their progress within an ongoing referral program.
**DM:**
Hi [Name], quick update — you're currently at [Number] successful referrals, just [Number] away from [Next Reward Tier]. Thought you'd want to know!
**Personalize:**
[Name], [Number], [Next Reward Tier]
**Tip:**
Showing exact progress toward the next tier motivates action far more than a generic "keep referring" message.
