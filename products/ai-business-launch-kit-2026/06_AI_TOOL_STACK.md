# AI TOOL STACK
## AI Business Launch Kit 2026 — File 06

A practical directory of AI and no-code tools by category. This kit is intentionally tool-agnostic — the prompts, worksheets, and systems in Files 02-05 work regardless of which specific tools you choose, and will keep working even after individual tools change.

**Important:** AI tools, their pricing, and their feature sets change frequently. Every "Free/Paid Status" note below is a general description of how the tool is typically positioned (e.g., "has a free tier") — **verify current pricing and features directly on the tool's website before signing up or budgeting for it.** Nothing here should be treated as a locked-in price.

This is not a paid endorsement list. Use whichever tool in each category fits your budget and comfort level — most categories have a free or low-cost starting option.

## How To Choose Your Starting Stack

You do not need every tool in this list. For your first 30 days, this is realistically enough:

```
□ One AI assistant (Claude or ChatGPT)
□ One design tool (Canva)
□ One landing page tool (Carrd, or your website builder)
□ One email tool (ConvertKit or Mailchimp)
□ One scheduling tool (Buffer or Later) — optional if posting manually
□ One spreadsheet (Google Sheets, or the File 09 dashboard)
```

Add automation, video, and audio tools only once your manual process is proven and repetitive enough to be worth automating.
