# AI BUSINESS WORKBOOK
## AI Business Launch Kit 2026 — File 04

15 printable worksheets covering your entire business, from vision to 30-day action plan. Print one section at a time, or fill in digitally. Each worksheet is designed to be completed in 10-20 minutes.

Pair these with the AI Prompt Vault (File 03) — most worksheets note which prompt category can help you fill in tougher fields.
