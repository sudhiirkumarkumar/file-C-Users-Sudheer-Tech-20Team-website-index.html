# 30-DAY LAUNCH PLANNER
## AI Business Launch Kit 2026 — File 05

This file has two systems:
1. **The 7-Day Launch Challenge** — an intensive sprint if you want to move fast.
2. **The 30-Day Business Roadmap** — a steadier, fuller build-out if you'd rather pace yourself.

Pick one. Don't try to run both at once. If you finish the 7-day sprint and want to keep building, the 30-day roadmap's Week 3-4 content works well as your natural next step.

---

# PART A — 7-DAY AI BUSINESS LAUNCH CHALLENGE

## DAY 1 — Choose The Business Idea

**Goal:** Land on one business idea worth testing this week.

**Tasks:**
- Review the 50 ideas in File 02, Module 1
- Shortlist 3 ideas that use a skill/interest you have
- Run the Idea Comparison Prompt on your shortlist
- Score your top idea on the AI Opportunity Scorecard

**AI Prompts to use:** P001 (Idea Brainstorm From Skills), P003 (Idea Comparison Prompt), P015 (First-Week Feasibility Prompt)

**Deliverables:**
- One chosen business idea
- Completed Opportunity Scorecard
- Completed Worksheet 2 (Business Idea)

**Checklist:**
```
□ Reviewed the 50-idea list
□ Shortlisted 3 ideas
□ Ran comparison prompt
□ Scored top idea
□ Filled in Worksheet 2
```

**Estimated time:** 1.5–2 hours

---

## DAY 2 — Define Customer + Problem

**Goal:** Know exactly who you're serving and what problem you're solving.

**Tasks:**
- Draft an Ideal Customer Profile
- Find 10 real pain-point quotes from forums, reviews, or conversations
- Write the problem in the customer's own words
- Complete the Problem Validation Checklist

**AI Prompts to use:** P026 (Deep ICP Builder), P034 (Interview Synthesis Prompt), Module 2 prompts in File 02

**Deliverables:**
- Completed Ideal Customer Profile
- Completed Worksheets 3 and 4 (Customer, Problem)
- 10 recorded pain-point quotes

**Checklist:**
```
□ ICP drafted
□ 10 pain-point quotes collected
□ Problem Validation Checklist — 4+ boxes checked
□ Worksheets 3 & 4 completed
```

**Estimated time:** 2 hours

---

## DAY 3 — Build The Offer

**Goal:** Have one clear, sellable offer with pricing tiers.

**Tasks:**
- Run the Full Offer Draft Prompt (P046)
- Build your Starter / Core / Premium tiers
- Draft your FAQ using P053
- Complete Worksheets 7 and 8 (Offer, Pricing)

**AI Prompts to use:** P046, P047 (Feature-to-Benefit Translator), P053 (Objection-to-FAQ), P055 (Package Tier Naming)

**Deliverables:**
- Complete offer draft with 3 tiers
- FAQ section
- Completed Worksheets 7 and 8

**Checklist:**
```
□ Offer draft generated
□ 3 pricing tiers defined
□ FAQ drafted
□ Worksheets 7 & 8 completed
```

**Estimated time:** 2 hours

---

## DAY 4 — Create Brand + Assets

**Goal:** Have a name, voice, and the minimum visual identity to look legitimate.

**Tasks:**
- Run the Business Name Generator and pick a name
- Draft positioning, tagline, and brand voice
- Write your About page and social bios
- Complete Worksheet 9 (Brand)

**AI Prompts to use:** P036–P045 (all of Module 4's Branding section)

**Deliverables:**
- Confirmed business name
- Brand voice + positioning statement
- About page draft
- Instagram/LinkedIn bio drafts

**Checklist:**
```
□ Name chosen
□ Positioning + voice drafted
□ About page written
□ Social bios written
□ Worksheet 9 completed
```

**Estimated time:** 2–2.5 hours

---

## DAY 5 — Create Marketing System

**Goal:** Have your first content, lead magnet, and outreach ready to go.

**Tasks:**
- Choose your lead magnet concept and draft its content (P101, P102)
- Write 3-5 pieces of launch-week content (P083, P100)
- Draft your outreach first message + follow-ups (File 07)
- Complete Worksheets 10 and 11 (Content, Lead Generation)

**AI Prompts to use:** P083, P100, P101, P102, P104, P105

**Deliverables:**
- Lead magnet content
- 3-5 content pieces ready to post
- Outreach message templates

**Checklist:**
```
□ Lead magnet drafted
□ 3-5 content pieces ready
□ Outreach templates drafted
□ Worksheets 10 & 11 completed
```

**Estimated time:** 2.5–3 hours

---

## DAY 6 — Prepare Launch

**Goal:** Everything is staged and ready — nothing left to build on launch day.

**Tasks:**
- Set up your payment/booking method
- Publish your lead magnet opt-in page
- Schedule or queue your launch-day content
- Run through the full Launch Checklist (File 08)
- Complete Worksheet 13 (Launch)

**AI Prompts to use:** P103 (Landing Page Copy), P127 (Launch Announcement Email)

**Deliverables:**
- Live payment/booking setup
- Live opt-in page
- Scheduled launch content
- Completed Launch Checklist

**Checklist:**
```
□ Payment/booking method live
□ Opt-in page live
□ Launch content scheduled
□ Launch Checklist reviewed
□ Worksheet 13 completed
```

**Estimated time:** 2–3 hours

---

## DAY 7 — Launch

**Goal:** Go live and tell real people.

**Tasks:**
- Publish your launch announcement (content + email, if you have a list)
- Send your first 5-10 outreach messages
- Post your launch content across your chosen channel(s)
- Personally tell 5 people in your network

**AI Prompts to use:** P127 (Launch Announcement Email), P104–P107 (Outreach)

**Deliverables:**
- Live, public offer
- First outreach batch sent
- Launch content published

**Checklist:**
```
□ Launch announcement published
□ 5-10 outreach messages sent
□ Launch content posted
□ 5 people in your network personally told
□ First entries logged in your dashboard (File 09)
```

**Estimated time:** 2 hours, plus ongoing response time

**After Day 7:** Move into the KPI tracking habit (Worksheet 14, weekly) and start the 30-Day Roadmap below from Week 3 onward — you've already covered Weeks 1-2 in this sprint.

## After Day 30

You now have a live, launched, AI-assisted business with a defined offer, brand, content system, and lead pipeline. From here:
- Keep running Worksheet 14 (KPI Tracking) weekly
- Use File 06 (AI Tool Stack) to add efficiency as you scale
- Revisit File 03 (Prompt Vault) any time you hit a new task type
- Use the Monthly Business Retrospective prompt (P149) at the end of every month going forward

Progress compounds. The goal of these 30 days was never to have a finished business — it was to have a *real, tested, launched* one you can keep improving.
