# LAUNCH CHECKLIST
## AI Business Launch Kit 2026 — File 08

One master checklist covering the entire journey from idea to launched business. Use this as your final review before Day 7 (7-Day Launch) or Day 26 (30-Day Roadmap), and again anytime you want a quick gut-check on where you stand.

---

## Master Launch Checklist

```
IDEA & VALIDATION
□ Idea selected
□ Idea scored on the Opportunity Scorecard
□ Problem Validation Checklist — 4+ boxes checked
□ At least 3 competitors/alternatives researched

CUSTOMER
□ Customer identified (specific, not "everyone")
□ Ideal Customer Profile completed
□ Problem validated with real evidence (quotes, conversations, research)

OFFER
□ Offer created (name, positioning, value proposition)
□ Starter / Core / Premium tiers defined
□ Pricing decided (with reasoning, not guesswork)
□ FAQ drafted
□ Objection responses prepared

BRAND
□ Business name chosen
□ Positioning statement written
□ Brand voice defined
□ About page written
□ Social bios written (Instagram, LinkedIn, or relevant platforms)

PRODUCT / SERVICE
□ Product/service created or delivery process defined
□ Scope clearly bounded (what's included / excluded)
□ Onboarding process defined for new customers

WEBSITE
□ Website or landing page ready (even a single page is enough to start)
□ Payment or booking method connected
□ Contact method clearly available

LEAD GENERATION
□ Lead magnet created
□ Lead magnet opt-in page live
□ Outreach templates drafted (first message + follow-ups)

CONTENT
□ Content pillars defined
□ First content batch ready (minimum 3-5 pieces)
□ Posting channel(s) chosen

SALES SYSTEM
□ Sales conversation framework reviewed
□ Discovery call script ready (if applicable)
□ Proposal template ready (if applicable)
□ Follow-up sequence planned

LAUNCH CAMPAIGN
□ Launch date set
□ Launch announcement content drafted
□ Launch email drafted (if you have a list)
□ People in your network identified to personally notify

LAUNCH COMPLETED
□ Announcement published
□ First outreach batch sent
□ Personal network notified
□ First leads/inquiries logged in your dashboard (File 09)

RESULTS REVIEWED
□ Week 1 KPIs logged (Worksheet 14)
□ What worked / what didn't reviewed
□ Next 30 days planned
```

---

## Quick Pre-Launch Gut Check

If you can honestly check these five, you're ready to launch — everything else can improve after you're live:

```
□ I can explain my offer in one sentence
□ I know exactly who I'm selling to
□ Someone can actually pay me or book me right now
□ I have at least one way for a new person to find me
□ I have a next step ready for the first person who says "yes"
```

Perfection is not the bar. A real, working, honest offer that a real customer can say yes to today — that's the bar.
