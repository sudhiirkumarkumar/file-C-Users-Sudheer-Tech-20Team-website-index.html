## How To Use This Kit

1. **Don't read everything first.** Start with the 60-Minute Quick Start below, then move to the 7-Day Launch System in file 05.
2. **Work in order the first time.** Idea → Validate → Build → Brand → Market → Launch → Improve. Each step feeds the next.
3. **Use the prompts as written, then customize.** Every prompt in the Prompt Vault has a "variables to customize" section — replace the bracketed placeholders with your own details before pasting into your AI assistant of choice (Claude, ChatGPT, or similar).
4. **Print the workbook.** The worksheets in file 04 are designed to be printed or filled in digitally, one per business decision.
5. **Treat the dashboard as a living document.** Update file 09 weekly once you launch — it's your business at a glance.
6. **Verify tool details before you rely on them.** AI tools, pricing, and features change quickly. Anywhere this kit references a specific tool, treat the details as a starting point and confirm current pricing/features before use.

## Your First 60 Minutes

You don't need a business plan to start. You need one idea, one customer, one problem, one offer, one channel, and one next action. Set a timer and work through this now.

**00–10 min — Pick a direction**
Open file 02, Module 1 (AI Business Idea Finder). Skim the 50 idea list. Circle 3 that use a skill or interest you already have. Don't overthink — you're shortlisting, not deciding.

**10–20 min — Choose one idea**
Run the "Idea Comparison Prompt" (Prompt Vault, Business Ideas category) on your 3 shortlisted ideas. Pick the one with the highest combined score on problem clarity + your ability to deliver it.

**20–30 min — Name your customer**
Open the Ideal Customer Profile worksheet (file 04, Worksheet 3). Fill in just the top third: who they are, what they do, where they spend time online. Don't fill in every field — just enough to picture one real person.

**30–40 min — Name the problem**
Open the Customer Pain Point Map (file 02, Module 2). Write down the one problem your customer would pay to have solved, in their own words, not yours.

**40–50 min — Sketch one offer**
Use the AI Offer Creator prompt (Prompt Vault, Offer Creation category). Paste in your idea, customer, and problem. Let the AI draft a Starter Offer. Don't polish it — just get a first draft.

**50–60 min — Pick one channel and one action**
Choose the single marketing channel where your customer already spends time (file 06 can help you decide). Write down the one action you'll take in the next 24 hours — one outreach message, one post, one conversation.

**By the end of this hour you will have:**
- One business idea
- One target customer
- One problem
- One offer concept
- One marketing channel
- One next action

That's the whole point. Everything else in this kit exists to help you go deeper on each of those six things.
