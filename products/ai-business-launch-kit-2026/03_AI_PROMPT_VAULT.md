# AI PROMPT VAULT
## AI Business Launch Kit 2026 — File 03

150 ready-to-use AI prompts organized by business task. Every prompt is written to work with Claude, ChatGPT, or any comparable AI assistant.

**How to use this file:**
1. Find the category matching your current task.
2. Copy the prompt exactly as written.
3. Replace every `[VARIABLE]` with your own details.
4. Paste into your AI assistant and review the output — always fact-check, personalize, and add your own judgment before publishing or sending anything.

No prompt in this vault will fabricate statistics, guarantee results, or replace your own decision-making. Treat every output as a strong first draft, not a final answer.

## SECTION 1 — BUSINESS IDEAS (15 prompts)

#### P001 — Idea Brainstorm From Skills
**What it does:** Generates business idea options based on your existing skills.
**When to use it:** At the very start, when you don't yet know what to build.
**Prompt:**
```
Act as a business idea consultant. I have these skills/experience:
[LIST SKILLS]. Suggest 10 AI-assisted business ideas that use these
skills, across a mix of services, digital products, and consulting.
For each, briefly note the target customer and how AI would speed up
delivery. Avoid generic ideas that don't clearly use my listed skills.
```
**Variables to customize:** `[LIST SKILLS]`
**Expected output:** A list of 10 tailored idea concepts with target customer and AI-leverage notes.

#### P002 — Idea Brainstorm From Problem
**What it does:** Reverses the process — starts from a problem you've noticed and generates business ideas around it.
**When to use it:** When you've spotted a real problem but don't have an idea yet.
**Prompt:**
```
Act as a business strategist. I've noticed this problem: [PROBLEM
DESCRIPTION] affecting [WHO IT AFFECTS]. Suggest 5 different
business models that could solve this problem, ranging from a simple
service to a scalable digital product. For each, note the delivery
model and rough startup complexity.
```
**Variables to customize:** `[PROBLEM DESCRIPTION]`, `[WHO IT AFFECTS]`
**Expected output:** 5 business model options addressing the same problem.

#### P003 — Idea Comparison Prompt
**What it does:** Compares your shortlisted ideas side by side.
**When to use it:** After you've narrowed down to 2-4 ideas and need to pick one.
**Prompt:**
```
Act as an objective business advisor. Compare these business ideas:
[IDEA 1], [IDEA 2], [IDEA 3]. Evaluate each on: problem clarity,
likely demand, ease of delivery for a beginner, and differentiation
potential. Recommend which to pursue first and explain why, while
noting this is a starting recommendation, not a guarantee.
```
**Variables to customize:** `[IDEA 1]`, `[IDEA 2]`, `[IDEA 3]`
**Expected output:** A structured comparison and one recommended idea with reasoning.

#### P004 — Idea Simplification Prompt
**What it does:** Strips an overly complex idea down to a testable version.
**When to use it:** When your idea feels too big or vague to start.
**Prompt:**
```
Act as a lean-startup advisor. Here's my business idea: [IDEA
DESCRIPTION]. Help me simplify it into the smallest testable version
I could launch within 7 days, cutting anything not essential to
proving the core value proposition.
```
**Variables to customize:** `[IDEA DESCRIPTION]`
**Expected output:** A simplified, minimum-viable version of the idea.

#### P005 — Idea-to-Niche Prompt
**What it does:** Helps narrow a broad idea into a specific, ownable niche.
**When to use it:** When your idea targets "everyone" and needs focus.
**Prompt:**
```

Suggest 5 specific niche angles I could focus on first (e.g., a
specific industry, customer size, or use case), and explain why
starting narrow could help me get traction faster.
```
**Variables to customize:** `[BROAD IDEA]`
**Expected output:** 5 niche-down options with reasoning.

#### P006 — AI-Leverage Check Prompt
**What it does:** Tests whether AI meaningfully improves your idea, or if it's just a buzzword add-on.
**When to use it:** Before committing — to make sure "AI" isn't just a label.
**Prompt:**
```

DESCRIPTION]. Honestly assess where AI genuinely speeds up or improves
delivery, versus where it's just a marketing label. Suggest how to
strengthen the real AI leverage in this business.
```
**Variables to customize:** `[IDEA DESCRIPTION]`
**Expected output:** An honest breakdown of real vs. superficial AI usage, plus improvement suggestions.

#### P007 — Delivery Model Prompt
**What it does:** Suggests possible ways to deliver your idea (async, live, subscription, one-time).
**When to use it:** When you know the idea but not how to structure delivery.
**Prompt:**
```

DESCRIPTION], suggest 4 possible delivery models (e.g., one-time

```
**Variables to customize:** `[IDEA DESCRIPTION]`
**Expected output:** 4 delivery model options with tradeoffs.

```

**What it does:** Sanity-checks whether an idea can realistically be tested within a week.

**Variables to customize:** `[IDEA DESCRIPTION]`

## SECTION 2 — MARKET RESEARCH (10 prompts)

#### P016 — Market Landscape Overview
**What it does:** Gives a general lay of the land for your market.
**When to use it:** Early research, before deep competitor analysis.
**Prompt:**
```
Act as a market research analyst. Give me an overview of the market
for [PRODUCT/SERVICE CATEGORY] serving [TARGET CUSTOMER]. Cover: types
of existing solutions, how mature this market seems, and what trends
you're aware of. Clearly mark anything you're inferring rather than
citing as confirmed fact, and note that I should verify with current
sources.
```
**Variables to customize:** `[PRODUCT/SERVICE CATEGORY]`, `[TARGET CUSTOMER]`
**Expected output:** A general market landscape summary with confidence flags.

#### P017 — Competitor Deep-Dive
**What it does:** Analyzes a specific competitor in depth.
**When to use it:** After identifying key competitors.
**Prompt:**
```
Act as a competitive analyst. Analyze this competitor: [COMPETITOR
NAME/DESCRIPTION]. Cover: their offer, target customer, apparent
positioning, and one gap I could exploit. Note that pricing/features
should be verified directly since they may have changed.
```
**Variables to customize:** `[COMPETITOR NAME/DESCRIPTION]`
**Expected output:** A structured competitor profile with a positioning gap.

#### P018 — Market Gap Finder
**What it does:** Identifies underserved angles in a market.
**When to use it:** When looking for a differentiation angle.
**Prompt:**
```
Act as a market strategist. Based on these competitors/alternatives:
[LIST COMPETITORS], identify 3 possible gaps or underserved segments I
could target with [MY OFFER TYPE].
```
**Variables to customize:** `[LIST COMPETITORS]`, `[MY OFFER TYPE]`
**Expected output:** 3 potential market gaps.

#### P019 — Pricing Landscape Prompt
**What it does:** Helps understand how similar offers are typically priced.
**When to use it:** Before setting your own prices.
**Prompt:**
```
Act as a pricing analyst. For offers similar to [MY OFFER] in the
[NICHE] space, describe common pricing structures and models used
(not specific numbers, since I'll verify current pricing myself).
Suggest what factors would justify pricing at the higher vs. lower end.
```
**Variables to customize:** `[MY OFFER]`, `[NICHE]`
**Expected output:** Pricing structure patterns and value-based reasoning.

#### P020 — Trend Awareness Prompt
**What it does:** Surfaces trends relevant to your market, with appropriate caveats.
**When to use it:** When shaping your positioning around current trends.
**Prompt:**
```
Act as a trend analyst. What trends are you aware of (as of your
training data) relevant to [MARKET/NICHE]? For each, note your
confidence level and flag that I should verify against current 2026
sources before using it in marketing claims.
```
**Variables to customize:** `[MARKET/NICHE]`
**Expected output:** A trend list with confidence and verification flags.

#### P021 — Market Size Reasoning Prompt
**What it does:** Helps reason through market size without fabricating numbers.
**When to use it:** When you want a rough sense of opportunity size.
**Prompt:**
```
Act as a market analyst. Help me reason through the rough opportunity
size for [OFFER] targeting [TARGET CUSTOMER] by thinking through: how
many potential customers likely exist, how often they'd need this, and
what would make the addressable market larger or smaller. Do not
output a specific market-size number as fact — walk through the
reasoning instead.
```
**Variables to customize:** `[OFFER]`, `[TARGET CUSTOMER]`
**Expected output:** A reasoning walkthrough, not a fabricated statistic.

#### P022 — Industry Language Prompt
**What it does:** Captures the specific vocabulary your market uses.
**When to use it:** Before writing marketing copy, so it sounds native to the industry.
**Prompt:**
```
Act as an industry insider for [NICHE]. List the specific terms,
phrases, and jargon this audience commonly uses to describe [PROBLEM
AREA], so I can use authentic language in my marketing instead of
generic phrasing.
```
**Variables to customize:** `[NICHE]`, `[PROBLEM AREA]`
**Expected output:** A vocabulary list specific to the niche.

#### P023 — Regulatory/Constraint Check Prompt
**What it does:** Flags any obvious constraints or considerations for the niche.
**When to use it:** For niches with compliance-sensitive customers (health, finance, legal-adjacent).
**Prompt:**
```
Act as a business advisor. For a business offering [OFFER] to
[TARGET CUSTOMER] in the [INDUSTRY] space, what general considerations
or constraints should I be aware of (e.g., claims I shouldn't make,
data sensitivity)? Note this is general awareness only, not legal
advice, and I should consult a professional for specifics.
```
**Variables to customize:** `[OFFER]`, `[TARGET CUSTOMER]`, `[INDUSTRY]`
**Expected output:** General awareness flags with a clear non-legal-advice disclaimer.

#### P024 — Alternative Solutions Mapping Prompt
**What it does:** Maps out every way customers currently solve the problem, including non-obvious ones.
**When to use it:** Broader competitive context, including DIY/manual alternatives.
**Prompt:**
```
Act as a market researcher. For customers dealing with [PROBLEM], map
out every way they might currently be solving it — including doing it
manually, using a different category of tool, hiring someone
informally, or simply not solving it. This should include non-obvious
alternatives, not just direct competitors.
```
**Variables to customize:** `[PROBLEM]`
**Expected output:** A broad map of direct and indirect alternatives.

#### P025 — Market Entry Angle Prompt
**What it does:** Suggests a specific angle to enter a crowded market.
**When to use it:** When your market has many existing players.
**Prompt:**
```
Act as a go-to-market strategist. The market for [CATEGORY] already
has established players like [EXAMPLES]. Suggest 3 specific entry
angles a small new solo business could use to find early traction

```
**Variables to customize:** `[CATEGORY]`, `[EXAMPLES]`
**Expected output:** 3 focused entry angle strategies.

**Prompt:**
```
