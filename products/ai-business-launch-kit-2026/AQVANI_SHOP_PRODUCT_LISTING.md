## FULL PRODUCT DESCRIPTION

### Start Here

You have a business idea, or you're circling a few. What you don't have is a clear, non-technical process for turning it into something real — and a pile of scattered AI tips isn't a process.

The AI Business Launch Kit 2026 is a complete, practical toolkit: prompts, worksheets, checklists, and planners that walk you from idea to launched business, one step at a time.

### The Problem

Most people trying to start a business today face the same wall: too much generic advice, not enough usable structure. "Use AI to grow your business" isn't a plan — it's a suggestion. Beginners are left guessing which prompts to use, how to structure an offer, what to post, and where to even begin.

### The Solution

This Kit replaces guesswork with a system. Every module — from idea validation to offer building to your first week of launch content — comes with the exact worksheets and AI prompts to complete it. You don't need to be technical. You need to be willing to fill in a worksheet and follow a process that's already been built for you.

### What's Inside

- **AI Business Launch Guide** — idea generation (50 ideas), validation framework, customer research, offer building, branding, and automation basics
- **AI Prompt Vault** — 150+ copy-paste AI prompts across 15 business categories
- **Business Workbook** — 15 printable worksheets covering your entire business
- **30-Day Launch Planner** — a 7-day launch sprint plus a full 30-day day-by-day roadmap
- **AI Tool Stack** — a practical, category-by-category AI tool directory
- **Marketing Swipe File** — 30-day content calendar, 50 attention-grabbing hook templates, lead magnet system, ethical outreach scripts, and a full sales kit
- **Launch Checklist** — one master checklist from idea to launch
- **AI Business Dashboard** — a working spreadsheet with a one-page business snapshot and an auto-calculating KPI tracker
- **100 Content Ideas** — organized across 10 content styles for ongoing marketing

### Who It's For

- Aspiring entrepreneurs who don't know where to start
- Freelancers and solopreneurs ready to productize their skills
- Creators and coaches building their first offer
- Consultants who want a repeatable system
- Side-hustle beginners with limited time
- Non-technical professionals who want to use AI without needing to "learn AI" first

By working through this Kit, you'll walk away with: a validated business idea, a defined ideal customer, a complete offer with pricing tiers, core brand assets (name, voice, About page, bios), a lead magnet, a first batch of content, an outreach system, and a real, launched business with a working dashboard to track it.

1. Open the Start Here file and complete your first 60 minutes

### FAQ

**Do I need technical or AI experience?**
No. Every prompt is copy-paste ready, and every worksheet tells you exactly what to fill in.

**Which AI tool do I need?**
Any capable AI assistant works — Claude, ChatGPT, or a similar tool. The Kit is not tied to one specific tool.

**How long does this take?**
The 7-Day Launch Challenge is built for one intensive week. The 30-Day Roadmap spreads the same work across a full month at a steadier pace.

**Will this guarantee I make money?**
No digital product can guarantee that, and we won't pretend otherwise. This Kit gives you a complete, practical process and the exact tools to execute it — your results depend on your market, offer, and effort.

**What format is this in?**
A set of PDF guides/workbooks and one working spreadsheet (.xlsx), delivered as an instant digital download.

**Can I use this for client work too?**
Yes — the frameworks and prompts can help you build offers and systems for your own clients, within the terms of the included license.
