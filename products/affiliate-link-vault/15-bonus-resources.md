## BONUS 5 — Affiliate Program Research Checklist

Use this **before** applying to a new affiliate program, to make an informed decision rather than joining blind.

```
[ ] Read the program's official terms and conditions in full
[ ] Confirm the commission structure (flat, percentage, recurring, tiered)
[ ] Confirm the cookie duration
[ ] Confirm the payout threshold and payment schedule
[ ] Confirm accepted payment methods (and whether they work for you)
[ ] Check for any restricted promotional methods (e.g., no paid ads, no coupon sites)
[ ] Check for content/brand guideline requirements
[ ] Look up independent reviews of the product/service itself
[ ] Look up independent reviews or discussion of the affiliate program specifically
[ ] Confirm there's a way to contact an affiliate manager or support
[ ] Check whether the program requires exclusivity (can't promote competitors)
[ ] Assess whether the product genuinely fits your audience
```

## BONUS 6 — Affiliate Marketing Quick Reference Cheat Sheet

A one-page glossary and formula sheet for fast lookup.

**Key Terms**
- **Affiliate Link** — Your unique tracking URL for a product/program
- **Deep Link** — A link to a specific page, not just the homepage
- **Cookie Duration** — How long a click is tracked before it expires
- **Commission Type** — One-time, Recurring, Tiered, or Flat Fee
- **Payout Threshold** — Minimum earned balance before you get paid
- **Affiliate Network** — A third-party platform hosting multiple programs
- **EPC** — Earnings per click, a performance metric some networks report

**Status Definitions (Quick View)**
- ACTIVE — Verified and working
- CHECK REQUIRED — Not verified in 30+ days
- BROKEN — Confirmed not working
- EXPIRED — Program/promo ended
- PENDING — Application awaiting approval
- REJECTED — Application declined
- REPLACED — Swapped for a new link

**Core Formulas**
```
Conversion Rate = Sales ÷ Clicks
Net Commission = Commission Earned − Refunds
```

**Monthly Rhythm**
```
Week 1: Publish planned content (Content Planner)
Week 2–3: Continue publishing, log performance weekly
Week 4: Run the Link Audit Checklist + update Dashboard
```
