# 12 — AFFILIATE PRODUCT EVALUATION SCORECARD

Before you commit time to promoting a new affiliate product, run it through this scorecard. It's a structured way to think through fit — **it does not calculate a "best" product for you.** You review the criteria, score honestly, and use your own judgment to decide.

---

## Scoring System

Score each criterion from **1 (poor) to 5 (excellent)** based on your own research and experience with the product.

| Criteria | What to Consider | Score (1–5) |
|---|---|---|
| Product Quality | Is it genuinely well-built, reliable, and does what it claims? | |
| Audience Relevance | Would your specific audience actually want or need this? | |
| Commission | Is the commission rate/structure reasonable for the effort involved? | |
| Cookie Duration | Is the tracking window long enough to credit realistic buying timelines? | |
| Brand Reputation | Does the company have a solid track record and reviews? | |
| Conversion Potential | Based on the offer and landing page, would you expect this to convert reasonably well? | |
| Recurring Commission | Does it pay once, or continue paying for renewals/subscriptions? | |
| Customer Support | Will your audience be taken care of if something goes wrong post-purchase? | |
| Refund Risk | How likely are refunds/chargebacks based on the product type and price point? | |
| Content Potential | Can you realistically create multiple pieces of content around this product? | |

**Total Score: ___ / 50**

---

## How to Read Your Score

This scorecard is a **decision-support tool, not a verdict.** A high total score suggests a product is generally worth further consideration; a low score suggests caution. But context matters — a product scoring lower on Commission might still be worth promoting if it scores very high on Audience Relevance and Content Potential, and vice versa.

**Use the score to ask better questions, not to make the decision automatically.** Always combine the scorecard with your own research into the program's actual terms (Program Directory, Section 03) before committing.

## Evaluation Tips

- **Score before you apply**, if you can access enough information — landing pages, existing reviews, program terms pages often provide enough detail.
- **Re-score after 60–90 days of promoting it.** Real performance data (from your Performance Tracker, Section 05) may shift your original estimate.
- **Don't skip low scores.** A product that scores poorly on Refund Risk or Customer Support can create real problems for your audience even if the commission looks attractive.
