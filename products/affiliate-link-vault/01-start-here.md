# 01 — START HERE
## Start Here: 30-Minute Affiliate Link Vault Setup

You don't need to build a perfect system on day one. This guide gets your vault functional in about 30 minutes, using whatever programs and links you already have. You can refine it every month using the Audit Checklist (Section 09).

Set a timer if it helps. Each step below is designed to take 2–5 minutes.

---

### Step 1 — List All Current Affiliate Programs
Before touching any templates, write a plain list of every affiliate program you're currently part of — even ones you haven't used in months. Check your email inbox for "affiliate," "partner," or "commission" to jog your memory. Don't organize yet, just capture.

### Step 2 — Enter Affiliate Links
Open the **Affiliate Link Master Vault** (Section 02) and add one row per link for each program from Step 1. Paste your actual affiliate URL into the **Affiliate URL** field. If you use deep links for specific products, add those too.

### Step 3 — Categorize Products
Assign each link a category using the **Link Organizer** categories (Section 04) — Software, AI Tools, Hosting, Courses, etc. This is what makes the vault searchable later instead of just being a long list.

### Step 4 — Add Commission Information
Go back to each row and fill in **Commission Rate**, **Commission Type** (flat vs. percentage vs. recurring), **Cookie Duration**, and **Payout Threshold**. Pull this from your affiliate dashboard or your program's terms page — never guess.

### Step 5 — Add Promotional Channels
For each link, note where you actually use it: blog, YouTube, Instagram, TikTok, email list, etc. This tells you later which channels are carrying your affiliate income and which ones aren't.

### Step 6 — Add Existing Content
Open the **Content Planner** (Section 06) and log any content you've already published that contains affiliate links — old blog posts, videos, pinned social posts. This becomes your baseline for the Audit Checklist.

### Step 7 — Set Up Performance Tracking
Open the **Performance Tracker** (Section 05). Enter the current month and, if available, pull last month's clicks/sales/commission numbers from each affiliate dashboard so you have a starting baseline.

### Step 8 — Create Your First Affiliate Content Plan
Pick one product from your vault and plan one piece of content using the **Content Planner** template. Use the **CTA Swipe File** (Section 07) to choose a call-to-action instead of writing one from scratch.

### Step 9 — Add Affiliate Disclosures
Open **Disclosure Templates** (Section 08) and choose the version that matches the platform you're publishing on. Customize the bracketed placeholders and add it to your live content if it isn't there already.

### Step 10 — Schedule Your Monthly Audit
Put a recurring reminder on your calendar — same day each month — to run through the **Link Audit Checklist** (Section 09). This is the single habit that keeps a vault useful instead of turning into another abandoned spreadsheet.

---

**That's it.** Your vault is live. From here, add new programs as you join them, and let the monthly audit keep everything current.
