# AFFILIATE LINK VAULT
## Your All-in-One System to Organize, Track & Manage Affiliate Links

**A premium digital template system for affiliate marketers, bloggers, creators, freelancers, coaches, and small business owners.**

---

## What This System Does

If your affiliate links currently live scattered across browser bookmarks, random spreadsheets, sticky notes, old emails, and half-remembered dashboards — this system replaces all of that with one organized workflow.

Affiliate Link Vault gives you a single home to:

- Store and organize every affiliate link you use
- Track affiliate programs, commissions, and login details
- Monitor which links are active, broken, or need review
- Log clicks, sales, and commissions by hand each month
- Plan affiliate content in advance
- Keep your disclosures consistent and compliant with your own research
- Evaluate new affiliate offers before you promote them

This is a **template and workflow system**, not affiliate software. You fill it in with your own real programs, links, and numbers.

## How to Use This System

1. **Read Section 01 (Start Here)** first — it walks you through a 30-minute setup.
2. **Convert each section** into the format that works for you: Google Sheets/Excel tabs, a Notion database, or a printed workbook. Table structures are provided so this conversion is fast.
3. **Replace every "Demo Example" row** with your own real affiliate programs and links.
4. **Revisit the Dashboard and Audit Checklist monthly** to keep your vault accurate.

This system is designed to be **lived in**, not read once and forgotten. Block 30 minutes today for setup, then 15–20 minutes once a month for maintenance.
