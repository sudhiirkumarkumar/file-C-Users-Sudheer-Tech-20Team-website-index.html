# 14 — FAQ

General education below. It is not legal, tax, or financial advice, and affiliate program terms vary — always confirm specifics with the individual program or network.

---

**1. What is an affiliate link?**
An affiliate link is a unique tracking URL given to you by a company or affiliate network. When someone clicks it and completes a qualifying action (usually a purchase), the tracking system credits you and you may earn a commission, as defined by that program's terms.

**2. What is an affiliate program?**
An affiliate program is a formal arrangement offered by a company that allows approved partners (affiliates) to promote its products or services in exchange for a commission on resulting sales or leads.

**3. What is an affiliate network?**
An affiliate network is a third-party platform that connects multiple companies' affiliate programs with affiliates in one place, handling tracking, reporting, and payouts. Some companies instead run their affiliate program directly ("in-house"), without a network.

**4. How should I organize affiliate links?**
Centralize them in one system (like this Vault) rather than spreading them across bookmarks, notes, and old emails. Categorize by product type, tag each with a status, and record commission/cookie details alongside each link so you're not hunting for information later.

**5. What is a deep link?**
A deep link is an affiliate link that points to a specific page — like a particular product or category — rather than just the homepage. Deep links generally perform better because they send the visitor directly to what you're recommending.

**6. What is cookie duration?**
Cookie duration is the length of time a tracking cookie stays active after someone clicks your affiliate link. If they complete a qualifying purchase within that window, you may still be credited even if they don't buy immediately. Durations vary widely by program — always confirm the current terms.

**7. What is recurring commission?**
Recurring commission means you continue earning a commission for as long as the referred customer stays subscribed or keeps paying (common with subscription/SaaS products), rather than receiving a single one-time payout.

**8. How do I track affiliate clicks?**
Most affiliate networks and in-house programs provide a dashboard showing clicks, leads, and sales tied to your account. This Vault doesn't track clicks automatically — you log the numbers manually into the Performance Tracker (Section 05) from those dashboards.

**9. How often should I audit affiliate links?**
A full audit monthly is a reasonable baseline for most creators, with higher-traffic content checked more frequently. See the recommended schedule in the Link Organizer (Section 04) and the checklist in Section 09.

**10. What happens when an affiliate link breaks?**
Mark it BROKEN immediately in your Master Vault, remove or update it in any live content, and check whether the program has issued a new link format. If the program has ended, mark the status EXPIRED and remove the reference from published content.

**11. How should affiliate disclosures be handled?**
Disclose clearly and before the affiliate link appears, using language your audience can easily understand. Requirements differ by country, platform, and individual program — use the templates in Section 08 as a starting point and confirm current requirements for your specific situation.

**12. Can beginners use this vault?**
Yes. The system is built to start simple — even with just one or two affiliate programs — and grow as you add more. The Quick Start Guide (Section 01) walks through a 30-minute setup regardless of experience level.

**13. Can I use it for multiple websites?**
Yes. Add a field or tab noting which website each link belongs to, or duplicate the Master Vault per site if you manage several separate projects.

**14. Can I use it for social media?**
Yes. The Promotional Channel field in the Master Vault and the Content Planner (Section 06) both include social platforms, and the CTA Swipe File (Section 07) includes social-specific templates.

**15. Can I use it for YouTube?**
Yes. YouTube-specific CTAs, disclosure templates, and content types are included throughout the system.

**16. Can I customize the categories?**
Yes. The Link Organizer's default categories (Section 04) are a starting point — add, remove, or rename categories to match your specific niche.

**17. Can I add unlimited affiliate programs?**
Yes. There's no built-in limit — the Master Vault and Program Directory are designed to scale as you add more rows, whether that's 5 programs or 500.

**18. How do I track commissions?**
Log commission earned and any refunds each month in the Performance Tracker (Section 05), which calculates Net Commission automatically using the provided formula. Pull the source figures from each program's own dashboard.

**19. How do I identify my best-performing products?**
Use the "Top Performing Program/Product/Channel" fields on the Affiliate Dashboard (Section 10), which are based on Net Commission data from your Performance Tracker. Review this monthly to see what's actually working, not just what you assume is working.

**20. How should I keep affiliate information updated?**
Run the monthly Link Audit Checklist (Section 09), which specifically includes re-checking commission rates, cookie durations, and program terms — since these details change over time without much notice from the program.

**21. Do I need technical or coding skills to use this system?**
No. Everything is built as tables and text templates that work in spreadsheets, Notion, or a printed workbook — no coding, plugins, or technical setup required.

**22. What if an affiliate program changes its terms?**
Update the relevant fields (Commission Rate, Cookie Duration, Payout Threshold, etc.) in your Master Vault and Program Directory as soon as you notice the change, and note the date of the change in your Notes field for your own records.
