# 04 — LINK ORGANIZER
## Categories & Status System

This section defines the two tagging systems used throughout the vault: **Product Category** (what kind of product it is) and **Status** (what condition the link is in). Applying both consistently is what makes your Master Vault filterable and searchable instead of just a long flat list.

---

## Part A: Default Product Categories

Use these as your **Product Category** values in the Master Vault (Section 02). Assign one primary category per link.

| Category | Typical Examples |
|---|---|
| Software | General business or productivity software |
| AI Tools | AI writing, image, video, or automation tools |
| Hosting | Web hosting, VPS, managed hosting |
| Domains | Domain registration and DNS services |
| Marketing | General marketing platforms and tools |
| SEO | SEO tools, keyword research, rank tracking |
| Email Marketing | Email service providers, automation platforms |
| Courses | Online courses, cohort programs |
| Productivity | Task management, note-taking, planning tools |
| Finance | Budgeting, invoicing, accounting tools |
| Business Tools | CRMs, legal templates, business formation services |
| Design Tools | Graphic design, video editing, stock assets |
| E-commerce | Store platforms, dropshipping tools, print-on-demand |
| Creator Tools | Podcasting, editing, link-in-bio, community platforms |
| Other | Anything that doesn't fit the above |

### Adding Custom Categories
You are not limited to this list. If your niche needs a category not shown here (e.g., "Fitness Equipment," "Pet Products," "Travel Gear"), add it directly to your Product Category list. Keep the total number of categories manageable — 10–20 is usually the sweet spot before a list becomes hard to scan.

---

## Part B: How to Organize by Category

1. **Tag every link** in the Master Vault with one Product Category.
2. **Group your Content Planner** (Section 06) by category to see which product types you cover most — and which are underused.
3. **Use category filters** when planning new content: "What haven't I covered in SEO tools lately?"
4. **Review category balance quarterly.** If 80% of your links sit in one category, consider whether that reflects your actual audience or just old habit.

## Part C: Affiliate Link Status Tracker

Every link also carries a **Status** value. Affiliate links break, expire, and change more often than most people expect: programs shut down, URLs get restructured, tracking parameters expire, or a merchant simply ends their affiliate program. Use this system to keep a clear, honest record of each link's condition.

### Status Definitions

| Status | Meaning | When to Use It |
|---|---|---|
| **ACTIVE** | Link works and tracking is confirmed | Verified within the last audit cycle |
| **CHECK REQUIRED** | Link hasn't been verified recently | Default status for anything not checked in 30+ days |
| **BROKEN** | Link no longer loads or redirects incorrectly | Confirmed dead link or wrong destination |
| **EXPIRED** | Program or promotion has ended | Affiliate program closed or promo period over |
| **PENDING** | Application submitted, not yet approved | Waiting on program approval |
| **REJECTED** | Application was declined | Program did not accept your application |
| **REPLACED** | Old link swapped for a new one | You've updated content with a new link/program |

### How to Verify a Link (Manual Check)

For each link marked **CHECK REQUIRED**, walk through this quick process:

1. **Click the live link** from the actual published content (not just from your vault) to confirm it loads correctly.
2. **Confirm it lands on the correct product page**, not a generic homepage or an outdated page.
3. **Check that your affiliate ID or tracking parameter is present** in the URL after any redirects.
4. **Log in to the affiliate dashboard** and confirm the program still shows your account as active.
5. **Update the Status and "Last Checked" date** in the Master Vault.

### Recommended Verification Schedule

| Link Type | How Often to Check |
|---|---|
| High-traffic content (top 10% of your posts/videos) | Monthly |
| Regularly published content (recent 90 days) | Every 60 days |
| Older evergreen content | Quarterly |
| Seasonal or promotional links | Before and after the promotion window |

### When a Link Comes Back Broken

1. Mark it **BROKEN** immediately — don't leave it live in published content.
2. Check whether the program has a new affiliate link format (many do after site redesigns).
3. If a new link is available, update your content and mark the old row **REPLACED**.
4. If the program has closed entirely, mark it **EXPIRED**, remove it from live content, and consider a relevant replacement offer.

Consistent link verification protects both your income and your audience's trust — a broken or mistracked link means lost commissions and a poor experience for the person clicking it. This status system is enforced monthly through the **Link Audit Checklist** (Section 09).

**Rule of thumb:** Category tells you *what* a link is. Status tells you *whether you can still trust it*. Keep both up to date and your vault stays reliable.
