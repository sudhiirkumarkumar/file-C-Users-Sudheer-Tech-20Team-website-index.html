## 1. How the 90-Day Roadmap Works

Your strategy (W-STR-22) says *where* you're going. This roadmap says *what you'll do each week* to get there.

```
            BUILD                       LAUNCH                      IMPROVE
     Weeks 1 ─ 4                 Weeks 5 ─ 9                 Weeks 10 ─ 13
  Prepare the offer,          Put it in front of          Learn from results,
  materials and systems       customers; sell; deliver    fix, document, plan next
         │                           │                            │
    REVIEW 1 (wk 4)             REVIEW 2 (wk 9)              REVIEW 3 (wk 13)
```

**Three principles:**

1. **One milestone per week.** A milestone is a finished thing ("Care Plan one-pager sent to 40 past customers"), not an activity ("work on marketing").
2. **Plan 60–70% of strategic hours.** Daily operations and surprises will claim the rest.
3. **Review on schedule, not when you remember.** Reviews at weeks 4, 9 and 13 are where plans are corrected.

**What you need before you start:**
☐ W-STR-17 SMART goals (max 3) ☐ W-STR-18 roadmap draft (or use P-STR-030) ☐ W-STR-19 top risks ☐ W-STR-20 current priorities

## 2. Capacity Calculator

Most 90-day plans fail because they assume time that doesn't exist. Calculate honestly.

| Line | Hours per week |
|---|---|
| A. Total hours you work in a typical week | |
| B. Hours on delivery (serving customers, making products) | |
| C. Hours on admin and operations (messages, bookings, accounts, purchasing) | |
| D. Hours lost to interruptions and problems (estimate honestly) | |
| **E. Strategic hours available = A − B − C − D** | |
| **F. Plannable strategic hours = E × 0.7** | |

**Check:** A = 50, B = 22, C = 14, D = 4 → E = 10 → F = 10 × 0.7 = **7 hours/week**.

**If F is under 4 hours:** reduce your 90-day ambition to one goal, or free time first — the Priority Matrix (W-STR-20) and System 06 workflows are designed for this.

## 8. Roadmap Reset — When You Fall Behind

Falling behind is normal: illness, a big order, staff changes, a festival rush. The mistake is pretending the original plan still works. A reset takes 30 minutes and keeps the 90 days useful.

**Reset when:** you've missed two milestones in a row, or you're more than two weeks behind, or a major change has happened.

**The three reset moves:**

| Move | What it means |
|---|---|
| **Cut** | Remove milestones that are no longer essential to the 90-day outcome |
| **Shrink** | Make remaining milestones smaller (fewer customers contacted, simpler version) |
| **Shift** | Move the 90-day outcome date or reduce the target — honestly, in writing |

### AI prompt

#### P-STR-040 · Roadmap Reset

| | |
|---|---|
| **Purpose** | Rebuild a realistic plan for the remaining weeks after falling behind. |
| **When to use** | After missing two milestones in a row, being two or more weeks behind, or a major change. |
| **Required input** | Original roadmap, what's done, what's missed and why, remaining weeks, current capacity. |

```
ROLE
You are a calm, practical planning advisor. You help owners recover plans without guilt or unrealistic catch-up schedules.

CONTEXT
Business: [BUSINESS NAME], [BUSINESS TYPE].
90-day outcomes: [OUTCOMES]
Original roadmap: [PASTE ROADMAP OVERVIEW]
Completed milestones: [LIST]
Missed milestones and reasons: [LIST]
Current week: [WEEK NUMBER] of 13
Plannable strategic hours per week from now: [HOURS]
What changed: [NOTES]

OBJECTIVE
Create a realistic reset plan for the remaining weeks.

CONSTRAINTS
- Do not schedule catch-up weeks above my plannable hours.
- Use three moves: Cut (remove non-essential milestones), Shrink (make milestones smaller), Shift (adjust the outcome or date).
- Protect the milestone most important to my top goal.
- Be honest if the original outcome is no longer realistic; propose a revised outcome.

PROCESS
1. Identify which remaining milestones are essential to the 90-day outcomes.
2. Apply Cut, Shrink and Shift.
3. Rebuild the remaining weeks.

OUTPUT FORMAT
1. Diagnosis: why the plan slipped (max 60 words, based on my notes)
2. Table: Milestone | Cut / Shrink / Shift / Keep | New version | Week
3. Revised 90-day outcomes (if needed)
4. One change to prevent the same slip again

QUALITY CHECK
Total hours in every remaining week must be at or below my plannable hours.
```

**Expected output:** a realistic plan for the remaining weeks, a revised outcome if needed, and one prevention step.
**Optional customization:** add "My busiest period starts in week [N]; plan around it."

## 9. Filled Example — BrightNest Cleaning Co. (fictional)

**Capacity:** A = 50 · B = 22 · C = 14 · D = 4 → E = 10 → **F = 7 hours/week**.
