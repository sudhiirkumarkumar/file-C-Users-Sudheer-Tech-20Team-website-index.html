## How to Use These Templates

These are the blank, printable versions of every worksheet in the **Business Strategy Workbook**. The workbook explains each tool and shows a worked example; these pages are where you do the work.

1. **Work in order.** Each worksheet uses answers from the ones before it.
2. **Fill in what you know first.** Write "unknown" where true — never guess facts.
3. **Then use the prompt.** Each page names the matching AI prompt (P-STR-###) from the workbook. Paste your completed worksheet into it.
4. **Record the final decision here.** The worksheet — not the AI chat — is your record.
5. **Date every page.** Strategy changes; dates show you which version is current.

**Digital use:** fill this PDF on screen, or copy the tables into a document or spreadsheet. Worksheets W-STR-04, 05 and 06 also exist in `Customer-Persona-Builder.xlsx` with automatic scoring.
