<h2>Your business deserves a strategy — not another stack of random AI prompts.</h2>

<p>Most small businesses don't struggle because the owner isn't working hard. They struggle because the owner is working hard without a clear plan. They sell to "everyone", compete on price, describe the business differently every time and plan one week at a time.</p>

<p><strong>The AI Business Strategy System</strong> fixes that with a structured, step-by-step process. You don't write a 40-page business plan. You make a short series of clear decisions. Each one is recorded on a worksheet, and an AI prompt does the heavy drafting for you.</p>

<p>When you finish, you'll have:</p>
<ul>
<li>✔ <strong>A clearly defined customer</strong>, based on real evidence instead of guesswork</li>
<li>✔ <strong>An offer you can explain in 30 seconds</strong>, with clear deliverables and terms</li>
<li>✔ <strong>A reason customers should choose you</strong>: a USP you can actually prove</li>
<li>✔ <strong>A price you can defend</strong>, calculated from your real costs instead of copied from competitors</li>
<li>✔ <strong>A realistic 90-day plan</strong> with one clear priority every week</li>
<li>✔ <strong>Your entire strategy on one page</strong>, ready to share with your team or your AI assistant</li>
</ul>

<h2>How it works: the 4-step loop</h2>
<p>Every tool in the system follows the same simple loop:</p>
<ol>
<li><strong>INPUT</strong>: fill in a short worksheet with what you actually know.</li>
<li><strong>AI PROCESS</strong>: paste it into the matching ready-made prompt.</li>
<li><strong>OUTPUT</strong>: review the draft against a "Done when" checklist.</li>
<li><strong>ACTION</strong>: record your final decision and move to the next tool.</li>
</ol>
<p>Because the AI works from <em>your</em> real information, you get specific answers built around your business instead of generic AI text.</p>
