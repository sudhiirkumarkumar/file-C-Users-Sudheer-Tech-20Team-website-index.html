### BONUS 3 — Product Offer Formula

**Problem + Solution + Outcome + Convenience**

Fill in your own product's offer using this formula:

- **Problem:** ______________________________________
- **Solution (your product):** ______________________________________
- **Outcome (the result they get):** ______________________________________
- **Convenience (why it's easy/fast with your product):** ______________________________________

**Example:**
"Beginners struggle to know what steps to take to launch a digital product *(Problem)*. This step-by-step action checklist *(Solution)* walks them from idea to first sale *(Outcome)*, using ready-made checklists and templates so they never have to guess what's next *(Convenience)*."
