## SECTION 8 — PRICING

**Goal:** Set a price that reflects real value, not guesswork.

### How to think about pricing
- **Customer value:** What result or time-savings does this deliver? A product that saves 10 hours of work is worth more than the hours it took you to make it.
- **Product depth:** More templates, more worksheets, more bonuses can justify a higher price — but only if they're genuinely useful, not padding.
- **Time saved:** Price partly reflects how much frustration or research the customer skips by buying this.
- **Problem solved:** A painful, urgent problem supports a higher price than a "nice to have."
- **Competitor pricing:** Use your Section 3 research table as your price range guide — don't price wildly outside it without a reason.
- **Product positioning:** A "quick win" checklist sits lower ($7–$19). A complete system or toolkit sits higher ($19–$49+).
