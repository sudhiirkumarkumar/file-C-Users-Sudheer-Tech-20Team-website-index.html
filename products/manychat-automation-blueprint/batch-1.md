# SECTION 1 — QUICK START

## What Is ManyChat Automation, In Plain English?

ManyChat is a tool that lets your Instagram or Facebook account **auto-reply** to comments, DMs, and messages — instantly, 24/7, without you typing anything yourself.

Think of it as a receptionist who never sleeps. Someone comments "GUIDE" on your post, and within seconds they get a DM with your free guide, a friendly welcome, and a link to learn more — all without you lifting a finger.

## Why Businesses Use Comment-to-DM Automation

- It turns public comments into private conversations (where sales actually happen).
- It captures leads the moment someone shows interest — not hours later when you finally check your phone.
- It answers repetitive questions ("How much?", "How do I buy?") automatically, freeing you up for real conversations.
- It works while you sleep, post content, or serve existing customers.

## The Building Blocks (In Simple Language)

| Term | What It Actually Means |
|---|---|
| **Flow** | A pre-planned conversation your bot has with someone — a sequence of messages and buttons. |
| **Trigger** | The action that starts a flow. Example: someone comments a keyword, or sends your page a DM. |
| **Action** | What the bot does in response — send a message, add a tag, wait, ask a question. |
| **Condition** | A simple "if this, then that" rule. Example: if they click "Yes," show offer A; if "No," show offer B. |
| **Keyword** | A specific word or phrase (like "GUIDE" or "PRICE") that triggers a flow when someone types or comments it. |
| **User Journey** | The full path a person takes through your automation, from their first comment to becoming a customer. |
| **Lead Capture** | The step where you collect a name, email, or answer from the subscriber so you can follow up later. |

> **Beginner Mistake:** Trying to build one giant flow that handles everything. Start with one goal, one trigger, and one simple path. You can always connect more flows later.

## "START HERE" — THE 10-MINUTE SETUP

Follow this sequence for your very first automation. Don't skip steps — each one sets up the next.

**Step 1 — Choose ONE goal.**
Pick a single outcome for this automation: deliver a freebie, capture an email, or answer a common question. One goal only.

**Step 2 — Choose your trigger.**
Decide what starts the flow: a comment keyword on a specific post, any DM containing a keyword, or a button tap on your welcome message.
*(Check the current ManyChat interface/options under Automation → Instagram/Facebook → Comments or Keywords to set this up.)*

**Step 3 — Create the opening message.**
Write a short, warm welcome that confirms what they'll get. Keep it under 3 sentences.

**Step 4 — Add the CTA.**
Add one clear button so the person knows exactly what to do next (e.g., "Send Me the Guide").

**Step 5 — Add the lead capture step.**
Ask for an email or a quick qualifying question — only if you need it for this goal.

**Step 6 — Add the follow-up.**
Schedule one follow-up message for 24 hours later, in case they didn't act on the first message.

**Step 7 — Test the automation.**
Use ManyChat's built-in preview/test mode, or comment on your own post from a second account, to run through the entire flow yourself.

**Step 8 — Publish.**
Turn the automation live. Check the current ManyChat interface/options for the exact "Publish" or "Turn On" toggle, as this varies by automation type.

> **Pro Tip:** Save this 10-minute sequence as your personal checklist. Every new automation you ever build in ManyChat follows these same 8 steps — only the messages change.

# SECTION 2 — AUTOMATION BLUEPRINT MAP

Every automation in this toolkit — no matter how advanced — follows the same basic shape. Once you understand this map, every flow in this blueprint will make instant sense.

```
TRIGGER
   ↓
WELCOME MESSAGE
   ↓
VALUE / ANSWER
   ↓
CTA
   ↓
LEAD CAPTURE
   ↓
FOLLOW-UP
   ↓
CONVERSION
```

### What Each Step Means

**TRIGGER**
The event that starts everything. A comment keyword, a DM keyword, a button click, or an ad click. Nothing happens until this fires.

**WELCOME MESSAGE**
The first thing the person sees in DM. It should confirm they're in the right place and set a friendly tone. This is your first impression — keep it human.

**VALUE / ANSWER**
Deliver what you promised, or answer what they asked. This is the freebie, the price, the FAQ answer, or the product info.

**CTA (Call-to-Action)**
A single clear button or question that moves the conversation forward. Never leave a message without a next step.

**LEAD CAPTURE**
Where you collect information — usually an email, sometimes a name, sometimes a qualifying answer — so you can follow up even if they go quiet.

**FOLLOW-UP**
An automatic message sent later (hours or days) if the person doesn't respond or doesn't take action. This alone can double your conversion rate.

**CONVERSION**
The final outcome you wanted: a sale, a booking, a signed-up lead, or a resolved question.

> **Quick Tip:** Before building anything in ManyChat, sketch your flow using this 7-step map on paper first. It takes two minutes and prevents most beginner mistakes.
