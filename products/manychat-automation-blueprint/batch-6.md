# SECTION 20 — QUICK REFERENCE CHEAT SHEET

### Best Trigger Ideas
- Comment keyword on a specific post
- Any DM containing a keyword
- Button tap on a welcome message
- Story reply or reaction
- First-time message to your page

### Best CTA Ideas
- "Send Me the Guide"
- "View Product"
- "Book a Call"
- "I'm Interested"
- "Ask a Question"

### Lead Capture Ideas
- Ask for email only (fastest, simplest)
- Ask for name + email (better personalization)
- Ask a qualifying question before capturing contact info (best for services)

### Follow-Up Timing Ideas
- 1 hour: check message was received
- 24 hours: first real follow-up
- 2-3 days: second follow-up with new angle
- 7 days: final no-pressure message

### FAQ Ideas
- Price and what's included
- How to purchase
- How the product/service is delivered
- Support availability
- Beginner-friendliness

### Conversion Ideas
- Give price directly instead of hiding it
- Always end a message with one clear next step
- Use urgency (expiry dates) only when genuinely true
- Follow up at least twice before giving up on a lead

- [ ] Trigger fires correctly
- [ ] Every button works
- [ ] Every link opens correctly
- [ ] Lead info is captured and saved
- [ ] Follow-up timing is correct
