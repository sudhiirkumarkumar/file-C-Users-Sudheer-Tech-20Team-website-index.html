# SECTION 3 — COMMENT-TO-DM MASTER FUNNEL

This is the core funnel every flow in this blueprint is built from. Learn this one well — the 10 flows in Section 4 are all variations of it.

```
USER COMMENTS "GUIDE"
        ↓
AUTOMATION TRIGGER
        ↓
PUBLIC COMMENT REPLY
        ↓
PRIVATE DM OPENS
        ↓
WELCOME MESSAGE
        ↓
DELIVER RESOURCE
        ↓
ASK PERMISSION / NEXT STEP
        ↓
COLLECT LEAD
        ↓
FOLLOW-UP
        ↓
CTA
```

### Step-by-Step Breakdown

**1. Trigger**
- **What starts it:** Someone comments the keyword `GUIDE` on a specific Instagram post or Facebook post.
- **Automation action:** Set up a "Comment Growth Tool" / "Comments Automation" on that specific post, with `GUIDE` as the keyword.
- **Where to build it:** Check the current ManyChat interface/options — usually under Automation → Instagram (or Facebook) → New Automation → Comment Growth Tool.
- **Purpose:** Only people genuinely interested in this exact offer enter the flow.

**2. Public Comment Reply**
- **Automation action:** ManyChat auto-replies publicly under their comment (optional but recommended — it shows social proof to other viewers).
- **Exact message:**

> **Copy & Paste:**
> Just sent it to your DMs, [NAME]! Check your inbox 📩

- **Button text:** None needed at this step.
- **Recommended delay:** Instant.
- **Next step:** DM opens automatically.
- **Purpose:** Confirms publicly that the automation worked, encouraging others to comment too.

**3. Private DM — Welcome Message**
- **Automation action:** Send first DM message.
- **Exact message:**

> **Copy & Paste:**
> Hey [NAME]! 👋 Thanks for commenting "GUIDE" — I've got exactly what you're looking for right here.

- **Button text:** "Send It To Me"
- **Recommended delay:** Instant (0 seconds).
- **Next step:** Deliver the resource.
- **Purpose:** Confirms the person is in the right place and builds a warm, human tone before delivering value.

**4. Deliver Resource**
- **Automation action:** Send the resource link or file.
- **Exact message:**

> **Copy & Paste:**
> Here's your [FREE RESOURCE] 🎉
> [PRODUCT LINK]
>
> Take a look and let me know what you think!

- **Button text:** "Got It, Thanks!"
- **Recommended delay:** Instant after button tap.
- **Next step:** Ask permission for next step.
- **Purpose:** Delivers on the promise immediately — this builds trust and keeps momentum.

**5. Ask Permission / Next Step**
- **Automation action:** Ask a soft, low-pressure question to continue the conversation.
- **Exact message:**

> **Copy & Paste:**
> Quick question, [NAME] — would it be okay if I sent over a couple of extra tips that pair well with this? Totally optional!

- **Button text:** "Yes, Send Them" / "No Thanks"
- **Recommended delay:** 5–10 minutes (gives them time to open the resource first).
- **Next step:** If "Yes" → Collect Lead. If "No" → move to a lighter follow-up sequence.
- **Purpose:** Respects their time while opening the door to deeper engagement.

**6. Collect Lead**
- **Automation action:** Ask for their email (or another qualifying detail).
- **Exact message:**

> **Copy & Paste:**
> Awesome! What's the best email to send those extra tips to?

- **Button text:** None — this is a text-input question field.
- **Recommended delay:** Instant.
- **Next step:** Confirmation + Follow-up.
- **Purpose:** Captures a lead you can reach even outside of Instagram/Facebook.

**7. Follow-Up**
- **Automation action:** Send an automated check-in 24–48 hours later.
- **Exact message:**

> **Copy & Paste:**
> Hey [NAME], just checking in — did the [FREE RESOURCE] help with what you were looking for? Happy to answer any questions!

- **Button text:** "I Have a Question" / "All Good, Thanks!"
- **Recommended delay:** 24 hours after resource delivery.
- **Next step:** Route to FAQ flow or CTA.
- **Purpose:** Re-engages silent leads and often surfaces buying questions.

**8. Final CTA**
- **Automation action:** Present your paid offer or next logical step.
- **Exact message:**

> **Copy & Paste:**
> By the way — if you want to go deeper, I put together [PRODUCT NAME] which builds on everything in the [FREE RESOURCE]. Want me to send the details?

- **Button text:** "Yes, Show Me" / "Maybe Later"
- **Recommended delay:** 2–4 days after the freebie delivery.
- **Next step:** Send product info flow (Section 7) or close conversation warmly.
- **Purpose:** Converts an engaged free-resource lead into a paying customer without feeling like a hard sell.

> **Pro Tip:** Never combine steps 6 and 7 (lead capture and follow-up) into the same message. Asking for an email and then instantly pitching feels transactional — space it out.

# SECTION 4 — 10 READY-TO-USE COMMENT-TO-DM FLOWS

Each flow below follows the Master Funnel structure. Pick the one matching your goal, copy the messages, and swap in your placeholders.

---

## FLOW 1 — FREE GUIDE

**Goal:** Deliver a free guide and capture an email.
**Trigger Keyword:** `GUIDE`

**Public Comment Response:**
> Sending it now, [NAME]! Check your DMs 📩

**DM Message 1:**
> Hey [NAME]! Thanks for asking about my [FREE RESOURCE] guide — here it is 👇

**Button:** "Send Me the Guide"

**DM Message 2:**
> Here you go: [PRODUCT LINK]
> Let me know if you have any questions after reading through it!

**Lead Capture:**
> Want me to send you a couple of bonus tips too? Just drop your email below.

**Follow-Up (24 hrs later):**
> Hey [NAME], did you get a chance to check out the guide? Happy to answer anything that came up.

**Final CTA:**
> If you'd like personalized help applying this, I offer [SERVICE] — want the details?

**Simple Flow Map:**
```
COMMENT "GUIDE" → DM → GUIDE LINK → EMAIL → FOLLOW-UP → SERVICE OFFER
```
