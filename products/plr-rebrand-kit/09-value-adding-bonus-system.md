# Section 9: Value-Adding Bonus System

Adding even one well-made bonus can meaningfully increase how valuable your product feels — and how it compares to other sellers offering the same underlying PLR. Below are 30 bonus ideas with an explanation of what each one adds.

---

## 30 Bonus Ideas

| # | Bonus | What It Adds |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Checklist | Turns advice into a fast, scannable action list the customer can follow without re-reading the full guide |
| 2 | Planner | Gives the customer a structured place to apply the content over time (weekly/monthly) |
| 3 | Tracker | Lets the customer measure progress, which increases perceived results and follow-through |
| 4 | Workbook | Converts passive reading into active practice through guided exercises |
| 5 | Cheat Sheet | Provides a one-page quick reference for customers who don't want to re-read the whole product |
| 6 | Prompt Pack | Saves the customer time by giving them ready-to-use prompts (writing, journaling, AI, etc.) |
| 7 | Swipe File | Gives ready-to-adapt examples (scripts, templates, captions) the customer can use immediately |
| 8 | Resource List | Curates helpful tools, links, or further reading so the customer doesn't have to search themselves |
| 9 | Templates | Removes the "blank page" problem by giving a ready structure to fill in |
| 10 | Scripts | Gives exact wording for conversations, outreach, or presentations the customer would otherwise have to write from scratch |

---

## How to Choose Bonuses

Don't add all 30 — that dilutes quality and slows down your launch. Instead:

1. Pick **1–3 bonuses** that most directly support the transformation your product promises (from Section 2, Part C).
2. Favor bonuses that are **fast for you to create** but **high in perceived value** for the customer (checklists, trackers, and templates are usually the best ratio for beginners).
3. Make sure every bonus matches your visual style direction (Section 7) so it feels like part of one cohesive product, not an afterthought.

---

## Bonus Naming Tip

Name each bonus specifically instead of generically:

- Generic: "Bonus Checklist"
- Specific: "The 10-Minute Daily Focus Checklist"

A specific bonus name communicates value at a glance and makes your product listing more compelling.
