## 10. FAQ

**Does this kit include PLR content I can sell?**
No. This kit teaches the *process* of rebranding PLR you already own (or plan to purchase separately). It does not include any specific PLR product.

**Do I need design software?**
The Canva Design Checklist is written for Canva specifically, but the branding principles apply to any design tool you're comfortable with.

**Will this guarantee my product sells?**
No. This kit gives you a practical, repeatable system for creating a professional, well-positioned product — the results depend on the quality of your underlying PLR, the effort you put into customization, and the market you sell into.

**Do I need to know how to use AI tools?**
No prior experience is required. The Prompt Vault gives you ready-to-copy prompts you can paste directly into any AI writing tool.

**What if my PLR license doesn't allow everything in this kit?**
Always follow your specific PLR license. Section 11 walks you through exactly what to check before you sell anything, and the transformation levels in Section 4 let you adjust your approach accordingly.

**What format is the kit delivered in?**
Instant digital download — PDF guides, worksheets, and checklists.
