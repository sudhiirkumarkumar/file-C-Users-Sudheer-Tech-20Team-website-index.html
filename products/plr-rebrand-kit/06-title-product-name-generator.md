# Section 6: PLR Title & Product Name Generator

A strong name does two jobs at once: it tells the customer exactly what they'll get, and it makes your product feel distinct from every other seller using the same PLR base. Use the formulas below to generate options, then test them against the checklist at the end.

---

## Naming Formulas

| Formula | Example |
|---|---|
| [Number] + [Result] + [Audience] | "7 Focus Habits for Busy Freelancers" |
| The [Outcome] Blueprint | "The Consistent Income Blueprint" |
| The [Niche] Starter Kit | "The Etsy Seller Starter Kit" |
| [Result] in [Time] | "A Clearer Budget in 14 Days" |
| The Ultimate [Topic] Toolkit | "The Ultimate Client Onboarding Toolkit" |
| The [Audience] Guide to [Outcome] | "The Coach's Guide to Booked-Out Calendars" |
| [Number]-Day [Outcome] Plan | "21-Day Habit Reset Plan" |
| The [Adjective] [Topic] System | "The Simple Content Batching System" |
| [Outcome] Without [Common Obstacle] | "Consistent Posting Without the Burnout" |
| The [Topic] Playbook | "The Freelance Pricing Playbook" |

**How to use these:** pick one formula, plug in your Section 2 worksheet answers (new niche angle, new promise, new target customer), and generate 5–10 variations before choosing.

---

## Title Checklist

Before finalizing, check your title against these questions:

- [ ] Does it name a specific outcome, not just a topic?
- [ ] Would my target customer recognize themselves in this title?
- [ ] Is it free of exaggerated claims ("guaranteed," "instant," "overnight")?
- [ ] Does it avoid sounding identical to the original PLR title or other common rebrands of it?
- [ ] Is it short enough to read at a glance on a product thumbnail?
- [ ] Does the subtitle add a detail the title doesn't (time frame, method, or audience)?

## Subtitle Formula

Pair your title with a subtitle that adds one missing detail — usually the audience, the method, or the time frame:

```
[Title]: [Method or Time Frame] for [Specific Audience]
```

Example: **The Content Batching Playbook: A One-Afternoon System for Busy Solopreneurs**
