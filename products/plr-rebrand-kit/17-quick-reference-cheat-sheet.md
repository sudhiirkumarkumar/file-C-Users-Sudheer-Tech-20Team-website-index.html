# Section 17: Quick Reference Cheat Sheet

## PLR Rebranding Cheat Sheet — One Page

**Workflow:** PLR → Rebrand → Customize → Improve → Package → Market → Sell

**Formula:** R-E-B-R-A-N-D — Research, Evaluate, Build, Rewrite, Add, Name, Distribute

---

### DO

- ✅ Customize the content, not just the cover
- ✅ Improve on the original with your own insight
- ✅ Add value (a bonus, checklist, worksheet, or example)
- ✅ Reposition for a specific audience
- ✅ Add original examples and personal notes
- ✅ Check the specific license before you sell anything

### DON'T

- ❌ Blindly resell PLR content unedited
- ❌ Ignore your license's actual terms
- ❌ Make unsupported income or results claims
- ❌ Leave obvious PLR branding or watermarks in place
- ❌ Copy competitors' rebrands of the same PLR
- ❌ Assume every PLR license grants identical rights

---

### Before You Publish, Ask:

1. Have I changed more than the cover?
2. Does this speak to a specific audience?
3. Have I added something the original didn't have?
4. Have I confirmed my license allows this use?
5. Would I be comfortable putting my name on this?

If any answer is "no," go back to the relevant section of this kit before you publish.
