# Section 12: Product Quality Control Checklist

A 50-point checklist to run through before you publish. Work through each category in order — content and licensing issues are far more costly to fix after launch than before.

---

## Content (10 points)

- [ ] 1. Introduction is fully rewritten in your own voice
- [ ] 2. Conclusion is fully rewritten in your own voice
- [ ] 3. At least one example per section is original or personalized
- [ ] 4. No leftover references to the original PLR provider, niche, or unrelated topics
- [ ] 5. Content matches the audience defined in your Section 2 worksheet
- [ ] 6. No repetitive or filler sentences remain
- [ ] 7. All AI-generated text (if used) has been human-edited, not published raw
- [ ] 8. Tone is consistent from the first page to the last
- [ ] 9. Every claim made is something the content can actually support
- [ ] 10. Spelling and grammar have been checked

## Design (10 points)

- [ ] 11. Cover follows the hierarchy rules in Section 7 (title > subtitle > visual > brand)
- [ ] 12. Colors are limited to your chosen 3–4 brand colors
- [ ] 13. Fonts are limited to one heading font and one body font
- [ ] 14. Margins and spacing are consistent across every page
- [ ] 15. Page numbers appear on every page except the cover
- [ ] 16. Headers/footers are consistent throughout
- [ ] 17. Checklist and worksheet pages have enough blank space to be usable
- [ ] 18. Icons (if used) are all the same visual style
- [ ] 19. Images are consistent in tone and quality
- [ ] 20. Final PDF has been previewed at 100% zoom for layout errors

## Branding (5 points)

- [ ] 21. Product name matches what's used throughout every file
- [ ] 22. Brand name / author name is consistent across the cover, interior, and marketing assets
- [ ] 23. Visual style direction (Section 7) is applied consistently across all files, not just the cover
- [ ] 24. No trace of the original PLR provider's branding, logo, or watermark remains
- [ ] 25. About-the-author page reflects your actual brand identity

## Usability (10 points)

- [ ] 26. Table of contents matches actual page numbers
- [ ] 27. All worksheets have clear instructions
- [ ] 28. All checklists use consistent, scannable formatting
- [ ] 29. Any links included are tested and working
- [ ] 30. File opens correctly on both desktop and mobile PDF viewers
- [ ] 31. Font sizes are legible on a phone screen, not just a large monitor
- [ ] 32. Bonus files are clearly labeled and easy to find
- [ ] 33. Read Me file accurately describes what's included
- [ ] 34. Folder structure matches Section 10's recommended layout
- [ ] 35. File names are clean and descriptive, with no leftover draft/version clutter

## Licensing (5 points)

- [ ] 36. You have read the specific PLR license for this content
- [ ] 37. You have confirmed resale and modification rights apply to your intended use
- [ ] 38. Any required attribution or branding removal has been completed
- [ ] 39. Proof of purchase / license terms are saved for your records
- [ ] 40. Your own customer-facing license/terms document is included and accurate

## Customer Experience (5 points)

- [ ] 41. Delivery format is clear (instant download, email delivery, etc.)
- [ ] 42. Customer knows exactly what they purchased before buying (accurate listing description)
- [ ] 43. Support contact information is included somewhere in the files
- [ ] 44. Thank-you page or message is included
- [ ] 45. The product delivers on the specific promise made in your marketing copy

## Marketing Readiness (5 points)

- [ ] 46. Product description is written and proofread (Section 13)
- [ ] 47. Short description for listing platforms is ready (Section 13)
- [ ] 48. Preview/mockup images are prepared and match the final design
- [ ] 49. At least one launch post or email is drafted (Section 13)
- [ ] 50. Pricing has been decided and matches the value/level of transformation delivered (Section 4)

---

## How to Use This Checklist

Don't treat this as a one-time formality — run it **after** your first full draft, fix everything you find, then run it again before publishing. Products that pass all 50 points feel noticeably more polished and trustworthy to customers than those that skip straight from writing to publishing.
