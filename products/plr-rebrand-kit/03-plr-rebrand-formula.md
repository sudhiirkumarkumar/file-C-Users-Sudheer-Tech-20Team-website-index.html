# Section 3: The REBRAND Formula

A simple, memorable framework to run every PLR project through — from the moment you pick a file to the moment it's listed for sale.

```
R — Research your audience
E — Evaluate the original PLR
B — Build your unique angle
R — Rewrite and personalize
A — Add value
N — Name and package the product
D — Distribute and market
```

---

## R — Research Your Audience

Before you touch the content, get specific about who you're selling to.

- Where does this audience already spend time (Etsy searches, Facebook groups, Pinterest boards, subreddits)?
- What language do they use to describe their problem? (Use their words, not industry jargon.)
- What have they already tried that didn't work?
- What price range is normal in this niche?

**Action step:** Write a 2–3 sentence description of your ideal customer before moving to the next letter.

---

## E — Evaluate the Original PLR

Read the entire PLR product before deciding what to do with it.

- Is the content accurate and still relevant?
- Is it well-organized, or does it need restructuring?
- Is the writing quality strong enough to build on, or does it need heavy rewriting?
- Does it match the audience you identified in step R, or does it need repositioning?
- What's missing that your audience would expect?

**Action step:** Make a short list of what's usable as-is, what needs rewriting, and what needs to be added.

---

## B — Build Your Unique Angle

This is where a generic PLR file becomes *your* product.

- Pick one clear angle: a specific outcome, a specific audience, or a specific method
- Avoid trying to serve everyone — a narrower angle is easier to market and to fulfill
- Decide what your product will be known for (e.g., "the beginner-friendly version," "the done-in-15-minutes version," "the version with real templates")

**Action step:** Write your angle as a single sentence: "Unlike the generic version, mine is for ___ and helps them ___."

---

## R — Rewrite and Personalize

Use the 3-Level Transformation System (Section 4) and the Prompt Vault (Section 5) to:

- Rewrite the introduction and conclusion completely in your own words
- Replace generic examples with ones relevant to your audience
- Adjust the tone to match your brand voice
- Add your own opinions, tips, and short personal notes where appropriate

**Action step:** Rewrite at minimum the introduction, one full section, and the conclusion by hand (not just AI-generated) so the voice feels consistent throughout.

---

## A — Add Value

Original content plus something extra is what separates a rebrand from a re-skin.

- Add a checklist, worksheet, or quick-reference summary
- Add a bonus using the ideas in Section 9
- Add an FAQ addressing objections specific to your audience
- Add a short case study or example scenario

**Action step:** Choose at least one addition from Section 9's Bonus System and build it before packaging.

---

## N — Name and Package the Product

- Finalize your product name using Section 6
- Design the cover and interior using Sections 7 and 8
- Organize your files using the folder structure in Section 10

**Action step:** Confirm your final file names, folder structure, and cover are complete and consistent with your brand.

---

## D — Distribute and Market

- Write your product description and short description (Section 13)
- Build your sales page (Section 14)
- Prepare your launch social posts, emails, and hooks (Section 13)
- Publish on your chosen platform (Etsy, Gumroad, your own site, etc.)

**Action step:** Schedule your launch content using the ready-to-customize assets in Section 13.

---

## REBRAND at a Glance

| Letter | Focus | Output |
|--------|-------|--------|
| R | Research | Audience description |
| E | Evaluate | Content usability notes |
| B | Build | One-sentence unique angle |
| R | Rewrite | Personalized draft |
| A | Add | At least one bonus |
| N | Name | Final title + packaged files |
| D | Distribute | Marketing assets + launch |

Keep this page open as a running checklist for every PLR product you transform.
